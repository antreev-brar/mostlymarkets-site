What to Expect From Apple's (AAPL) Q4 Earnings

Apple reports after the close on Monday, 2 November. Analysts expect revenue of about $113 billion, up 10%, at the top of Apple's own forecast, with the stock at a record.

Marcus Lindqvist·Big tech and earnings·29 Oct 2026, 4:56 p.m. ET·2 min read
AAPLApple
▲ 1.48% $348.91 at the close, 29 Oct

Apple (AAPL) reports results for its fiscal fourth quarter, the three months to 26 September, after the market closes on Monday, 2 November. The release is due at 4:30 p.m. ET, with the call at 5 p.m. ET. It will be the first results call for John Ternus, who succeeded Tim Cook as chief executive on 1 September.

Last quarter was Apple’s best June quarter ever. Revenue rose 16% to $109.4 billion and EPS rose 29% to $2.02, above the $1.88 analysts expected, according to Zacks data cited by the AP. But Apple forecast growth of 9% to 11% for the September quarter, short of the roughly 12% Wall Street wanted, Reuters reported, and Services grew more slowly than expected. The stock sank 7.4% on 31 July, its worst day since April 2025, by our count.

What analysts expect

Analysts expect revenue of $112.95 billion, up 10.2% from $102.47 billion a year ago, and EPS of $1.98, up from $1.85, according to Zacks via Yahoo Finance. The average on Yahoo Finance is a little higher on sales, at $113.32 billion, and also $1.98 a share.

That sits at the top of Apple’s own range. Its 9% to 11% forecast works out to $111.7 billion to $113.7 billion, MacRumors calculated. Apple also guided to a gross margin of 47% to 48%, including about 1 percentage point from tariff refunds.

What to watch

  • The iPhone. Apple expects iPhone revenue to grow by a mid-teens percentage. Only the first weeks of iPhone 18 Pro sales, from 18 September, fall in this quarter. The foldable iPhone Duo, which went on sale on 23 October from $1,999, will show up in the forecast for the holiday quarter instead.
  • Memory costs. Cook called the memory-chip shortage “very significant” in July, according to Reuters. “We estimate the memory cost would rise further,” Jefferies analyst Edison Lee wrote, and he sees iPhone gross margin possibly falling to 34.5% from 38%. Watch how much of the margin comes from one-time tariff refunds.
  • Services. Apple’s App Store, iCloud and other services brought in $30.7 billion last quarter, short of the $31.3 billion analysts expected, Yahoo Finance reported. Apple expects growth similar to the June quarter’s.

How the stock has done

Apple rose 1.5% on Thursday to $348.91, a record close by our count, above the $348.22 of 22 October. The stock is up 28.3% this year and 4.8% over the past month, and it has gained 12.9% since its close on 31 July, the day after its last report. It has moved 5% or more on only 3 of the last 252 sessions.

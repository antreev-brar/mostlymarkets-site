 SAXONBURG, Pa., November 4, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Coherent Corp. (NYSE: COHR)
(“Coherent,” “We,” or the “Company”), a global leader in photonics, announced financial results today for its first quarter of fiscal year 2027 ended September 30, 2026. 

 Revenue for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 was $2.37 billion, with GAAP gross margin of 39.1% and GAAP net income of $1.90 per diluted share. On a non-GAAP basis, gross margin was 40.6% with net income per diluted share of $2.09. 
 “Q1 marked a strong start to
fiscal 2027, with pro forma revenue up 56% year over year and non-GAAP EPS up 80%,” said Jim Anderson, CEO. “Our established businesses delivered strong growth, while new growth areas are beginning
to ramp. As AI datacenters transition from copper to optical connectivity, Coherent’s broad photonics platform and manufacturing scale position us to help customers meet rising needs for bandwidth and energy efficiency. We expect fiscal 2027
pro forma revenue growth to exceed 60%.” 
 Sherri Luther, CFO, said, “Our Q1 results delivered substantial operating leverage and durable gross
margin expansion, driven by pricing optimization and cost improvements. This margin strength gives us the confidence to raise our longer-term target. We expect to achieve our first quarter with non-GAAP gross
margin above 45% before the end of calendar 2028.”

 Everpure Announces Third Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Results 
 Total revenue growth of 51% year-over-year 
 Increased FY27 revenue and operating income guidance 

 SANTA CLARA, Calif. — November 18, 2026 — Everpure (NYSE: P), the company revolutionizing storage and data management, today announced financial results for its third quarter fiscal year 2027 ended November 1, 2026. 

 “The strength of our Q3 results reinforces Everpure’s role as the trusted partner customers rely on for their most critical data infrastructure,” said Charles Giancarlo, Chairman and CEO of Everpure. “As organizations work to break down data silos and modernize their data estates, our growth across both core and new, high-growth markets shows we are uniquely equipped to solve their most complex data challenges at scale.” 

 Third Quarter Financial Highlights 

 • Revenue $1.5 billion, up 51% year-over-year 
 • Product revenue $948 million, up 77% year-over-year 
 • Subscription services revenue $512 million, up 19% year-over-year 
 • Subscription annual recurring revenue (ARR) $2.2 billion, up 20% year-over-year 
 • Remaining performance obligations (RPO) $4.2 billion, up 44% year-over-year 
 • GAAP gross margin 68.0%; non-GAAP gross margin 69.2% 
 • GAAP operating income $150 million; non-GAAP operating income $345 million 
 • GAAP operating margin 10.3%; non-GAAP operating margin 23.6% 
 • Operating cash flow $443 million; free cash flow $265 million 
 • Total cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities $1.1 billion 
 • Returned approximately $67 million to stockholders through share repurchases of 0.5 million shares. 

 “Q3 marked another outstanding quarter of execution for Everpure, where we delivered record revenue and operating income, exceeding the high end of our guidance,” said Everpure CFO Tarek Robbiati. “Our results reflect the strength of our diversified portfolio, with our core and core AI offerings complemented by increasing contributions from our new businesses. We are raising full-year guidance for the third consecutive quarter and reiterating our strong preliminary FY28 outlook, underscoring confidence in our durable business model and broad-based momentum.” 

 Third Quarter Company Highlights 

 Advancing Data Primacy 
 • Expanded Everpure Data Intelligence with native Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration and privacy-first file intelligence, giving AI agents secure, governed access to live enterprise data context. 
 • Released the Global Data Sovereignty Report 2026 , finding that while 90% of surveyed enterprises recognize data sovereignty as a business concern, 64% lack a formal data sovereignty strategy. 

 Driving Hyperscaler Adoption and Industry-Leading AI Performance 
 • Secured a design win with a second top-five hyperscaler , validating Everpure DirectFlash® technology in the world’s largest cloud environments. 
 • FlashBlade//EXA ranked first on every checkpointing and KV-cache metric submitted in the MLPerf Storage v3.0 closed division. 
 • Introduced PureKVA on FlashBlade , delivering faster time to first token for large language model inference. 

 Industry and Market Recognition 
 • Joined the S&P 500 index in September 2026. 
 • Named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Enterprise Storage Platforms , positioned highest in execution and furthest in vision for the second consecutive year. 
 • Named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Infrastructure Platform Consumption Services , positioned highest in execution and furthest in vision for the second consecutive year.

 Key Performance Metrics 
 Subscription ARR is a key business metric that refers to the annualized recurring contract value of all active, non-cancelable customer subscription agreements with subscription terms of any length at the end of the quarter, plus on-demand billings for the quarter multiplied by four. 

 Total Contract Value (TCV) Sales, or bookings, of Everpure's Evergreen//One and similar consumption- and subscription-based offerings is an operating metric, representing the value of orders received during the period. 

 Non-GAAP Financial Measures 
 To supplement our unaudited condensed consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP, Everpure uses the following non-GAAP financial measures: non-GAAP gross profit, non-GAAP gross margin, non-GAAP operating income, non-GAAP operating margin, non-GAAP net income, non-GAAP net income per share, and free cash flow. 

 We use these non-GAAP financial measures for financial and operational decision-making and as a means to evaluate period-to-period comparisons. Our management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures provide meaningful supplemental information regarding our performance and liquidity by excluding certain expenses such as stock-based compensation expense, payroll tax expense related to stock-based activities, amortization of debt issuance costs related to debt, amortization of acquired intangible assets and gains and losses from mark-to-market adjustments on strategic investments that may not be indicative of our ongoing core business operating results. Everpure believes that both management and investors benefit from referring to these non-GAAP financial measures in assessing our performance and when analyzing historical performance and liquidity and planning, forecasting, and analyzing future periods. The presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures is not meant to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for our financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP, and our non-GAAP measures may be different from non-GAAP measures used by other companies. 

 For a reconciliation of these non-GAAP financial measures to GAAP measures, please see the tables captioned "Reconciliations of non-GAAP results of operations to the nearest comparable GAAP measures" and "Reconciliation from net cash provided by operating activities to free cash flow," included at the end of this release.

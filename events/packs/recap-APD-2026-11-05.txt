 Air Products Reports Fiscal 2026 Full-Year and Fourth Quarter Results; Provides Fiscal 2027 Guidance 
 Delivered double-digit adjusted EPS growth in fiscal 2026 | Improved core business performance
Reduced capital spend relative to prior year | Expanded backlog, including over $2.0 billion of new electronics wins 

 Fiscal 2026 Full-Year Summary of Results ( comparison versus prior year) 
 • GAAP earnings per share ("EPS") # of $2.39 and operating income of $23 million (compared to loss per share of $1.74 and operating loss of $877 million in the prior year); both periods reflect significant charges related to project exits 
 • Adjusted earnings per share * of $13.45 increased 12 percent and exceeded guidance midpoint; adjusted operating income * of $3.2 billion increased 11 percent 
 • Cash used for investing activities of $4.1 billion, down $3.0 billion; capital expenditures * of $3.3 billion, down $1.8 billion 
 Q4 FY26 Summary of Results ( comparison versus prior year) 
 • GAAP EPS # of $2.62 and operating income of $633 million reflect the impact of project exits, primarily related to two China coal gasification plants 
 • Adjusted EPS * of $3.61 exceeded guidance midpoint; adjusted operating income * of $856 million 
 Guidance 
 • Fiscal 2027 full-year adjusted EPS guidance * of $14.15 to $14.50; fiscal 2027 first quarter adjusted EPS guidance * of $3.35 to $3.45 
 • Expect fiscal year 2027 capital expenditures * in the range of $3.0 billion to $3.5 billion 

 Fiscal 2026 Full-Year Consolidated Results ( comparison versus prior year) 
 LEHIGH VALLEY, Pa. (November 5, 2026) - Air Products (NYSE:APD) today reported full-year fiscal 2026 GAAP results, including operating income of $23 million, operating margin of 0.2 percent, and EPS # of $2.39. This compares with an operating loss of $877 million, operating margin of negative 7.3 percent, and loss per share # of $1.74 in the prior year. Both periods reflect significant charges related to business and asset actions, primarily associated with previously announced project exit decisions. The non-GAAP financial measures discussed below exclude these charges, as well as other items, as described in the "Reconciliations of Non-GAAP Financial Measures" section of this release. 
 Fiscal 2026 full-year sales of $13.0 billion increased eight percent, or $954 million, on four percent higher volumes, two percent higher energy cost pass-through, and two percent favorable currency. 
 On a non-GAAP basis, fiscal 2026 full-year adjusted operating income* of $3.2 billion increased 11 percent over the prior year, primarily due to higher on-site volumes, favorable currency, and higher pricing. These benefits were partially offset by higher costs, driven by fixed-cost inflation and higher incentive compensation, partially offset by productivity improvements and lower depreciation. Adjusted operating margin * of 24.4 percent improved 70 basis points. Adjusted EPS * of $13.45 increased 12 percent over the prior year and also benefited from higher equity affiliates' income. 

 # Per share amounts are calculated and presented on a diluted basis from continuing operations attributable to Air Products. 
 * Certain results in this release include references to non-GAAP financial measures on a consolidated, continuing operations basis. Additional information regarding these measures and reconciliations of GAAP to non-GAAP historical results can be found below. Management is unable to reconcile, without unreasonable efforts, the Company’s forecasted range of adjusted EPS or capital expenditures to a comparable GAAP range or amount because management is not able to predict the timing or occurrence of events or transactions that management believes are not representative of the Company’s underlying business performance or the timing or occurrence of future investment activity, which are necessary to calculate forward-looking adjusted EPS from continuing operations and capital expenditures, respectively.  Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter Consolidated Results (comparison versus prior year) 
 Air Products also reported fiscal 2026 fourth quarter GAAP results, including operating income of $633 million, operating margin of 17.8 percent, and EPS # of $2.62. This compares with operating income of $17 million, operating margin of 0.5 percent, and EPS # of $0.02 in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2025. Both periods reflect significant business and asset action charges. Prior-year charges included project exit decisions and the initial impairment of two China coal gasification plants. Current-year charges primarily reflect updated estimates associated with the sale process for these plants, including one sold in October 2026. The non-GAAP financial measures discussed below exclude these charges, as well as other items, as described in the "Reconciliations of Non-GAAP Financial Measures" section of this release. 
 On a non-GAAP basis, fourth quarter adjusted operating income * of $856 million increased five percent on higher volumes and pricing actions, partially offset by higher costs. The higher volumes were primarily driven by on-sites, including a significant asset sale associated with an early contract termination in the Americas, and contributions from new assets. Adjusted operating margin * of 24.1 percent decreased 150 basis points, primarily due to higher costs. Adjusted EPS * of $3.61 increased six percent. 
 Fourth quarter sales of $3.6 billion increased 12 percent on nine percent higher volumes and one percent each from higher pricing, higher energy cost pass-through, and favorable currency. 
 Chief Executive Officer Eduardo Menezes said, "Our fiscal 2026 results demonstrate the progress we are making to improve our core industrial gas business, strengthen execution, and allocate capital with discipline. We delivered 12 percent adjusted EPS growth, reduced capital expenditures by $1.8 billion, and expanded our industrial gas project backlog through new business wins. These results reflect the dedication of our employees, whose commitment to safety and focus on serving our customers with excellence continue to drive our performance. As we move through fiscal 2027, we will maintain a disciplined approach to capital allocation while focusing on expanding our industrial gas project backlog and delivering continued operating income growth." 
 Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter Results by Business Segment 
 • Americas sales of $1.6 billion increased 27 percent from the prior year on 26 percent higher volumes and one percent higher pricing. Operating income of $469 million increased 20 perc ent primarily due to higher on-site volumes, including a significant asset sale associated with an early contract termination and a new asset contribution. The benefit from volume growth and lower depreciation expense were partially offset by higher incentive compensation, product distribution and dislocation costs, and fixed-cost inflation. Operating margin of 28.7 percent decreased 170 basis points, primarily due to higher costs. 
 • Asia sales of $909 million increased four percent from the prior year on three percent higher volumes and two percent favorable currency, partially offset by one percent lower energy cost pass-through. Volume growth was driven by on-sites, including new assets, and helium. Operating income of $250 million increased 10 percent and operating margin of 27.5 percent improved 150 basis points, pr imarily due to lower depreciation due to certain gasification assets held for sale and higher volumes. 
 • Europe sales of $856 million increased eight percent from the prior year on six percent higher energy cost pass-through, two percent higher pricing, and one percent higher volumes, partially offset by one percent unfavorable currency. Operating income of $241 million increased two percent , driven by higher on-site volumes and higher pricing, net of higher power costs. These benefits were partially offset by higher costs, including fixed-cost inflation. Operating margin of 28.2 percent decreased 190 basis points, which included an approximate 200-basis-point headwind from energy cost pass-through. 
 • Middle East and India equity affiliates' income of $97 million increased six percent from the prior year, primarily from an affiliate in Saudi Arabia. 
 • Corporate and other sales of $122 million decreased 34 percent from the prior year. Operating loss was $108 million compared to $49 million in the prior year, driven by lower sale of equipment activity and higher costs. 
 Outlook 
 Air Products expects full-year fiscal 2027 adjusted EPS guidance * in the range of $14.15 to $14.50. For the fiscal 2027 first quarter, Air Products' adjusted EPS guidance * is $3.35 to $3.45. 
 Air Products expects capital expenditures * to be in the range of $3.0 billion to $3.5 billion for full-year fiscal 2027.

 About Air Products 
 Air Products (NYSE: APD) is a world-leading industrial gases company in operation for more than 85 years. Serving energy, environmental, and emerging markets, the Company is generating a cleaner future by providing essential industrial gases, related equipment, and applications expertise that help customers improve productivity, efficiency, and sustainability across a wide range of industries, including energy, chemicals, electronics, metals, manufacturing, food, medical, and space. 
 Air Products is focused on profitably growing its core industrial gases business, accelerating growth in areas of competitive strength, and extending its reach through disciplined opportunities that create long-term value. As the leading global supplier of hydrogen, Air Products develops, engineers, builds, owns and operates some of the world's largest hydrogen projects and has extensive capabilities in air separation technology, engineering, and plant operations. Through its sale of equipment businesses, the Company also provides specialized products such as turbomachinery, membrane systems, and cryogenic containers globally. 
 Air Products had fiscal 2026 sales of $13.0 billion from operations in approximately 50 countries. For more information, visit www.airproducts.com or follow us on LinkedIn, X, Facebook or Instagram.

 We present certain financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles ("GAAP") because they exclude items that management does not consider to be representative of our underlying business operations. When viewed together with our GAAP results, we believe these non-GAAP financial measures offer a more complete understanding of the factors and trends affecting our financial performance and support analysis of our results on a more consistent basis. 
 Readers are cautioned that non‑GAAP financial measures have inherent limitations and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the corresponding GAAP measures. Our definitions and calculations of non‑GAAP financial measures may differ from those used by other companies, which may limit comparability. 

 Non-GAAP Performance Measures 
 Management uses non-GAAP performance measures, including adjusted operating income, adjusted operating margin, and adjusted earnings per share ("EPS"), to assess our performance because these measures exclude items that management does not consider to be representative of our underlying business operations. We also use adjusted operating margin to assess operational efficiency, cost discipline, and overall profitability.

Non-service related components are recurring, non-operating items that include interest cost, expected return on plan assets, prior service cost amortization, actuarial loss amortization, as well as special termination benefits, curtailments, and settlements. The net impact of non-service related components is reflected within “Other non-operating income (expense), net” on our consolidated income statements. Adjusting for the impact of non-service pension components provides management and users of our financial statements with a more accurate representation of our underlying business performance because these components are driven by factors that are unrelated to our operations, such as volatility in equity and debt markets. Further, non-service related components are not indicative of our defined benefit plans’ future contribution needs due to the funded status of the plans. 
 Tax Reform Adjustment Related to Deemed Foreign Dividends 
 During the second quarter of fiscal year 2025, we recorded a net income tax benefit of $34.9 million ($0.16 per share) related to our intent to file a refund claim for the 2018 tax year. Our intent to pursue the refund claim was based on a review of several U.S. Tax Court cases regarding the U.S. taxation of deemed foreign dividends in the transition year of the U.S. Tax Cuts and Jobs Act. The benefit was net of a $67.8 million liability for an uncertain tax position. 
 In September 2026, a subsequent U.S. Tax Court decision provided additional guidance regarding the calculation of the refund amount. As a result, we recorded income tax expense of $14.2 million ($0.06 per share) during the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 to increase the related liability for an uncertain tax position.

As of 1 January 2026, all swaps were re-designated as cash flow hedges. The unrealized gain on swaps that remained de-designated during the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 was not material.

 Capital Expenditures (Non-GAAP) 
 Capital expenditures is a non-GAAP financial measure that management uses to evaluate our deployment of capital and assess alignment with our strategic priorities. Our calculation of this measure begins as the sum of cash paid for additions to plant and equipment, including long-term deposits, acquisitions (less cash acquired), investment in and advances to unconsolidated affiliates, and investment in financing receivables, each of which are reported on our consolidated statements of cash flows. 
 We then adjust this amount to exclude spending for additions to plant and equipment by our consolidated joint venture, NEOM Green Hydrogen Company (“NGHC”), to the extent such spending is funded by sources other than Air Products’ cash. These other funding sources include NGHC’s project financing, which is non‑recourse to Air Products, as well as equity contributions from the other joint venture partners. Management believes this adjustment provides a more useful view of the capital we deploy to support the ongoing growth of our business.

 Ross Stores Reports Strong Third Quarter Sales and Earnings Results 

 Updates Fourth Quarter and Fiscal 2026 Outlook 

 Dublin, California, November 19, 2026 -- Ross Stores, Inc. (Nasdaq: ROST) today reported financial results for the 13‑week quarter ended October 31, 2026. 

 Highlights: 
 • Total sales for the third quarter of fiscal 2026 increased a robust 12% versus last year, with comparable store sales up a strong 8%, primarily driven by customer traffic. 
 • Third quarter operating margin of 12.3% increased 70 basis points compared to last year, above the Company’s plan of 11.7% to 12.0%. 
 • Earnings per share for the third quarter of $1.91 exceeded our guidance of $1.75 to $1.83. 
 • During the quarter, opened 51 new stores, including 41 Ross and 10 dd’s DISCOUNTS, completing 2026 store opening plan of 115 new locations. 

 Jim Conroy, Chief Executive Officer, commented, “We are extremely pleased with our third quarter sales and earnings growth, particularly given the more challenging comparisons we faced. Results exceeded our expectations as our marketing initiatives continue to attract more customers while our compelling combination of great brands at exceptional values and an enhanced store experience continued to resonate. As a result, customer traffic remained the primary driver of our comparable store sales growth. We are encouraged by the broad-based strength of our results and the continued positive response from customers to our brands, values, and shopping experience.”

 Third Quarter and First Nine Months Results 

 Sales for the third quarter increased 12% to $6.3 billion, up from $5.6 billion in 2025. Comparable store sales rose a strong 8% for the quarter on top of a 7% gain last year. Net income was $606 million versus $512 million last year, while earnings per share were $1.91 compared to $1.58 per share in the prior year period. 

 For the first nine months of fiscal 2026, sales grew 15% to $18.5 billion, up from $16.1 billion in 2025. Comparable store sales for the nine-month period were up 11%. Net income was $2.1 billion versus $1.5 billion last year, while earnings per share were $6.59 compared to $4.61 per share in the prior year period. 

 Results for the first nine months of 2026 include approximately $253 million of IEEPA tariff refunds recognized in the second quarter, which benefited earnings per share by approximately $0.60. 

 Update on Shareholder Payouts 

 During the 2026 third quarter, a total of 1.4 million shares of common stock were repurchased for an aggregate price of $319 million under the Company’s two-year $2.55 billion authorization approved by its Board of Directors in March 2026. The Company remains on track to buy back a total of $1.275 billion in common stock during fiscal 2026. 

 In addition, the Company's Board of Directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.445 per common share, payable on December 31, 2026 to stockholders of record as of December 8, 2026. 

 Fiscal 2026 Guidance 

 Mr. Conroy commented, “Looking ahead to the holiday season, we are excited about the opportunities in front of us and confident in the plans we have in place for the remainder of the year. For the fourth quarter, we are maintaining our outlook for comparable store sales growth of 4% to 5%, on top of last year’s strong 9% gain. Earnings per share are expected to be in the range of $2.16 to $2.25 or growth of 8% to 13%.”

 Mr. Conroy continued, “Based on our year-to-date results and our updated fourth quarter guidance, we are increasing our 2026 fiscal year earnings per share projections to be in the range of $8.75 to $8.84. As a reminder, our full year guidance includes an approximate $0.60 earnings per share benefit from IEEPA tariff refunds recognized in the second quarter.” 

 Mr. Conroy concluded, “The market share gains we have achieved reinforce our confidence in the opportunities ahead, and we believe we are well positioned to continue strengthening our competitive position and driving sustainable, profitable growth over the long term.”

 About Ross Stores, Inc. 
 Ross Stores, Inc. is an S&P 500, Fortune 500, and Nasdaq 100 (ROST) company headquartered in Dublin, California, with fiscal 2025 revenues of $22.8 billion. Currently, the Company operates Ross Dress for Less ® ("Ross"), the largest off-price apparel and home fashion chain in the United States with 1,993 locations in 45 states, the District of Columbia, Guam, and Puerto Rico. Ross offers first-quality, in-season, brand name and designer apparel, accessories, footwear, and home fashions for the entire family at savings of 20% to 60% off department and specialty store regular prices every day. The Company also operates 384 dd's DISCOUNTS ® stores in 24 states that feature a more moderately-priced assortment of first-quality, in-season apparel, accessories, footwear, and home fashions for the entire family at savings of 20% to 70% off moderate department and discount store regular prices every day. Additional information is available at www.rossstores.com .

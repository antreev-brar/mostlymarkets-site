 Rocket Lab Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results: Posts Record Revenue, $1.2 Billion or 52% Sequential Quarterly Backlog Growth, Positive GAAP Operating Cash Flow, and Guides to Record Revenue and Gross Margin Expansion in Q4 2026 
 Long Beach, California. November 9, 2026 – Rocket Lab Corporation (Nasdaq: RKLB), a global leader in launch services and space systems, today shared the financial results for fiscal third quarter ended September 30, 2026. 

 Rocket Lab founder and CEO, Sir Peter Beck, says: “Q3 2026 was a defining quarter for Rocket Lab. We delivered a record $268 million in revenue ($34 million higher than Q2 2026) alongside a record backlog which has grown to $3.57 billion. Nearly $1 billion of that backlog comes from new launch contracts, signed in Q3 2026 alone, as well as $470 million signed for Space Systems. Our end-to-end space strategy is delivering exactly as designed: the world's most critical and ambitious missions increasingly rely on Rocket Lab for launch, spacecraft, and components, sometimes all on a single mission, and every part of our business is growing as a result.” 

 Highlights for the Third Quarter 2026, plus business updates since September 30, 2026. 
 • Record quarterly revenue of $268 million, a 73% increase year-on-year. 
 • Record backlog of approximately $3.6 billion, a 52% increase since end of Q2 2026. 
 • Positive GAAP operating cash flow of $7.2M. 
 • Approximately $1 billion in new launch contracts signed across Electron, HASTE, and Neutron, bringing Rocket Lab’s total launch backlog to 117 launches: a new record. 
 • Secured over $515M in Neutron launch contracts from constellation operators in Q3, growing the rocket’s backlog to 16 missions ahead of its debut and lifting the base launch price to $70 million for several of these missions as industry launch capacity tightens. With first launch now targeted for no earlier than 2027, critical milestones continue to be achieved across assembly, integration, testing, and delivery of first flight hardware to Launch Complex 3. 
 • Twelve Rocket Lab-built spacecraft successfully launched to orbit and now operating, including eight satellite platforms for Globalstar’s direct-to-device communications constellation, twin spacecraft conducting Mars science for NASA, the responsive space VICTUS HAZE mission for the United States Space Force, and a single spacecraft for Viasat’s NASA Communications Services Depot. 
 • More than 55 spacecraft now in development and build across Rocket Lab’s production complexes, including spacecraft for NASA, the Space Development Agency, USSF, and commercial constellation operators. 
 • Fully funded Rocket Lab’s pending acquisition of Iridium Communications through a completed ATM equity offering and received approval by Iridium lenders to maintain Iridium’s credit facility post-transaction, and obtained overwhelming approval from Iridium shareholders in favor of the acquisition.

 Fourth Quarter 2026 Guidance 
 For the fourth quarter of 2026, Rocket Lab expects: 
 • Revenue between $270 million and $290 million. 
 • GAAP Gross Margins between 34% and 36%. 
 • Non-GAAP Gross Margins between 39% and 41%. 
 • GAAP Operating Expenses between $162 million and $168 million. 
 • Non-GAAP Operating Expenses between $136 million and $142 million. 
 • Interest Income net $45 million. 
 • Adjusted EBITDA loss of between $14 million and $20 million. 
 • Basic Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding of 681 million, including approximately 41 million of Series A Convertible Participating Preferred Shares. 
 See “Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below for an explanation of our use of Non-GAAP financial measures, and the reconciliation of historical Non-GAAP measures to the comparable GAAP measures in the tables attached to this press release. Stock-based compensation is currently expected to range from $17 million to $19 million in Q4 2026.

 About Rocket Lab 
 Rocket Lab (Nasdaq: RKLB) is an end-to-end space company delivering rockets, satellites, and spacecraft components for commercial, government, and defense missions. Driven by its industry-leading small-lift rockets Electron and HASTE and its upcoming reusable Neutron medium-lift rocket, Rocket Lab delivers reliable and responsive launch for the world’s most important missions from constellation deployment to missile defense. Rocket Lab’s satellites and components have powered more than 1,700 missions in Earth orbit, as well as deep-space exploration of the Moon, Mars, and beyond. Learn more at www.rocketlabcorp.com .

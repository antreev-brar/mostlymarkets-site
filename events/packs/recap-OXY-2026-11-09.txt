 Occidental Announces 3rd Quarter 2026 Results 

 • Achieved $10.0 billion principal debt milestone; reduced principal debt by $1.8 billion 
 • Generated $4.1 billion of operating cash flow from continuing operations 
 • Delivered $2.8 billion of free cash flow before working capital from continuing operations with capital expenditures of $1.3 billion 
 • Produced 1,427 Mboed globally, exceeding the mid-point of guidance 
 • Midstream and marketing pre-tax adjusted income exceeded the high end of guidance 
 • Reported EPS of $1.61; adjusted EPS from continuing operations of $1.91 

 HOUSTON — November 9, 2026 — Occidental (NYSE: OXY) today announced results for the third quarter of 2026, including net income attributable to common stockholders of $1.6 billion, or earnings per diluted share (EPS) of $1.61, and adjusted income attributable to common stockholders of $2.0 billion, or adjusted EPS from continuing operations of $1.91. The difference between net income attributable to common stockholders and adjusted income attributable to common stockholders mainly reflects derivatives losses. 

 "We reached an important milestone in the third quarter, reducing principal debt to $10.0 billion after retiring $10.4 billion of debt since the beginning of this year," said Richard Jackson, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Our teams delivered another strong quarter, building on the operational and financial performance we have seen throughout 2026. With our stronger balance sheet, we are advancing our plan to add more than $4.0 billion in annual sustainable cash flow by 2030, providing a pathway to accelerate capital returns and deliver durable long-term value for our shareholders." 

 QUARTERLY RESULTS 
 Oil and Gas 

 Pre-tax income from oil and gas totaled $2.6 billion for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $2.8 billion for the second quarter of 2026. Excluding items affecting comparability, the decrease was primarily driven by lower realized crude oil prices, partially offset by higher domestic natural gas prices. Third quarter average WTI and Brent marker prices were $85.79 per barrel and $91.62 per barrel, respectively. Average worldwide realized crude oil prices decreased 11% from the previous quarter to $85.91 per barrel. Average worldwide realized natural gas liquids prices of $24.63 per barrel remained flat quarter to quarter. Average domestic realized gas prices improved by 209% to $1.61 per thousand cubic feet (Mcf), compared to negative $1.48 per Mcf in the previous quarter. 

 Total global production averaged 1,427 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (Mboed) for the third quarter of 2026, exceeding the mid-point of guidance, led by the performance of Permian and Rockies business units, offsetting a temporary curtailment of production at certain Gulf of America facilities due to a third-party gas pipeline constraint and maintenance activities. 

 Midstream and Marketing 

 Midstream and marketing reported pre-tax income of $55 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.3 billion in the previous quarter. Excluding items affecting comparability, midstream

 About Occidental 
 Occidental is an international energy company that produces, markets and transports oil and natural gas to maximize value and provide resources fundamental to life. The company leverages its global leadership in carbon management to advance lower-carbon technologies and products. Headquartered in Houston, Occidental primarily operates in the United States, the Middle East and North Africa. To learn more, visit oxy.com.

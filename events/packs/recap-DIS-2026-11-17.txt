 November 17, 2026 
 To Our Shareholders and the Broader Investment Community, 
 We closed fiscal 2026 with a strong fourth quarter. Consumers around the world, faced with endless options, chose Disney. We welcomed 4% more global guests at Experiences and increased engagement on our streaming platforms and across the ESPN networks (1) , trends that support strong revenue growth. Disney’s mission is to be the world’s leading entertainment company and to compound growth for shareholders over time. Our strong fiscal 2026 results, delivered amid a challenging macroeconomic environment, show we are succeeding on that mission. 
 Revenues increased 16% for the fourth quarter to $25.9 billion from $22.5 billion in Q4 fiscal 2025. Income before income taxes increased 71% to $3.5 billion from $2.0 billion in Q4 fiscal 2025. Total segment operating income (2) increased 43% to $5.0 billion from $3.5 billion in Q4 fiscal 2025. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) increased to $1.51 from $0.73 in Q4 fiscal 2025. Adjusted EPS increased to $1.77 from $1.11 in Q4 fiscal 2025. 
 Revenues increased 8% for the fiscal year to $102.3 billion from $94.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Income before income taxes increased 18% to $14.2 billion from $12.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Total segment operating income increased 12% to $19.7 billion from $17.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Diluted EPS decreased 18% to $5.63 from $6.85 in fiscal 2025. Adjusted EPS increased 18% to $7.02 from $5.93 in fiscal 2025. 
 We exceeded our fourth quarter total segment operating income and full year adjusted EPS guidance. In a pivotal year for the Company, we delivered approximately 10% total segment operating income growth and 16% adjusted EPS growth, excluding the 53 rd week. 
 As a reminder, our fourth quarter and fiscal 2026 results had one additional week of operations (the 53 rd week). We estimate that the 53 rd week contributed approximately $0.17 to full year adjusted EPS. See the table on page 3 for the estimated impact of the 53 rd week across segment revenues and segment operating income (3) . 
 We continue to expect double-digit growth in adjusted EPS in fiscal 2027, excluding the impact of the 53 rd week. Note that in Q4 fiscal 2027 we will lap the impact of the 53 rd week in Q4 fiscal 2026. Additional guidance points are included on pages 11 through 12.

 OUR STRATEGY 
 Our results in the quarter reflect our continued execution across our three strategic pillars: 
 1. Investing in IP and creativity that breaks through, builds connections, and endures 
 2. Using advanced technologies to power our storytelling and increase monetization and returns 
 3. Reaching more consumers in more seamless, engaging ways around the world with a One Disney operating model 
 INVESTING IN IP 
 Creativity and the pursuit of creative excellence will always be at the core of our company. The increased engagement we saw across our streaming platforms and networks in the fourth quarter, which directly supports our revenue growth, reflects the strength of our creative talent, our focused investment in storytelling, and our leading sports rights portfolio. 
 Our IP continued to deliver. Following its Disney+ premiere on September 23, Toy Story 5 attracted 20 million views in its first five days on the platform. August was the highest month ever for Marvel Studios viewing, fueled by the summer theatrical success of Spider-Man: Brand New Day (1) and the debut of the Avengers: Doomsday trailer. And during the quarter, Marvel viewership on Disney+ more than doubled compared to its historical baseline. Our successful re-release of Avengers: Endgame in late September also showed how anticipation continues to build for Avengers: Doomsday , which arrives in theaters December 18. 

 To put the scale of Marvel fandom in context, the 38 films of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) have now generated more than $35 billion at the global box office (2) . Marvel’s impact extends well beyond the screen as we build fandom in ways unique to Disney. Marvel is consistently one of our top consumer products brands in global retail sales (3) .  IMPROVING MONETIZATION AND RETURNS 
 We are focused on leveraging technology to expand our reach and increase the value we deliver to our fans. This focus supports our financial goals: consistent revenue, total segment operating income, and adjusted EPS growth, along with strong free cash flow generation. 
 We are pleased to announce that our Board of Directors has declared a dividend of $2.00 per share, payable in two installments of $1.00 per share on January 22, 2027 (record date December 16, 2026) and on July 13, 2027 (record date June 11, 2027). This represents a 33% increase over the $1.50 per share paid to shareholders during fiscal year 2026. 
 In fiscal 2026, total company revenue increased 8% to $102.3 billion, total segment operating income increased 12% to $19.7 billion, and adjusted EPS expanded 18% to $7.02.  The fourth quarter and full year were record periods for Experiences revenue and segment operating income, even without the contribution of the 53 rd week. Experiences revenue increased 9% and segment operating income increased 12% on the year, while we estimate the 53 rd week contributed approximately 2% to Experiences revenue growth and 2% to Experiences segment operating income growth on the year. 
 For the fourth quarter, Experiences revenue increased 15%, with the 53 rd week contributing approximately 7%. Domestic Parks & Experiences revenue increased 19% in the quarter, with the 53 rd week contributing approximately 8%. Domestic Parks & Experiences operating income increased 36% in the quarter, with the 53 rd week contributing approximately 15%. 
 Our continued growth in both guests and guest spending at Experiences once again highlights the differentiated strength of our diversified global portfolio. Per capita guest spending increased 4% across our domestic parks and 5% across our international parks in the fourth quarter, with strength at Disneyland Paris more than offsetting incremental weakness relative to the third quarter at our Hong Kong and Shanghai parks. It was another strong quarter for our largest site, Walt Disney World, where a steady run of new offerings, our IP, and a commercial strategy focused on domestic guests drove strong growth, aided by favorable comparisons to the prior-year quarter. 
 Guests are also increasingly using AI-powered tools to discover, plan, and book their travel, and we are exploring several new ways to meet them where they are. We want Disney to appear as a vacation option within the AI tools guests use for inspiration and planning, and we are integrating our offerings directly into those tools so guests can book more efficiently. On our own channels, we are using AI to simplify planning and booking, including a vacation planning experience now in beta on our Walt Disney World website, built in collaboration with Google. 
 Our investments in franchise IP during the fiscal year and anticipation for Avengers: Doomsday continued to drive strong consumer products sales in the quarter. Consumer Products revenue increased 12% in the quarter, with the 53 rd week contributing approximately 4%, led by games and licensing. 
 Our streaming strategy is built on two pillars: making our core streaming experience the best in the marketplace and connecting our services into a single ecosystem. We are executing this strategy from a position of strength, reflected by another quarter of double-digit Entertainment SVOD (SVOD) revenue growth. 
 SVOD revenue increased 19% to $6.1 billion from $5.1 billion in Q4 fiscal 2025, with 22% subscription revenue growth. We estimate that the 53 rd week contributed approximately 9% to SVOD revenue growth and 9% to subscription revenue growth in the quarter, while our growth in subscription revenue was driven by both volume and rate. We are encouraged by our continued strong SVOD revenue growth and plan to invest behind this success. In fiscal 2027 we expect to increase both content and marketing investment in support of our longer-term goals. 
 SVOD operating income increased $422 million to $824 million in the quarter with the 53 rd week contributing approximately $160 million. We achieved a double-digit SVOD operating margin of 11.5% for the full fiscal year, and we remain focused on expanding SVOD operating margin over time.

 Our app unification of Disney+ and Hulu is nearly complete.  ESPN plays an important role in the overall Disney portfolio by contributing live and on-demand offerings to Disney+, lifting engagement and supporting revenue growth, and encouraging users to upgrade to the Disney+, Hulu, ESPN Bundle. This fall, we increased the volume of sports content we offer Disney+ subscribers without the need for an ESPN subscription, continuing our simulcast of College GameDay, adding more live college football games, and airing select NFL games, which kicked off with the Dallas Cowboys at the Philadelphia Eagles on October 26. Following the expansion of ESPN content into Europe and select APAC markets this year, ESPN now reaches fans through Disney+ in approximately 100 markets. 
 Sports segment subscription and affiliate fee revenue increased 18% in the quarter to $3.4 billion from $2.9 billion in Q4 fiscal 2025, with advertising revenue growth of 20%. We estimate that the 53 rd week and NFL Transaction contributed 11% to segment revenue growth, 12% to subscription and affiliate fee revenue growth, and 9% to advertising revenue growth in the quarter. 
 In September, we named two leaders who will accelerate how we build, test, and deliver technology across the company: 
 Karandeep Anand was appointed Senior Executive Vice President and Chief Technology Officer, a newly created role at Disney, where he oversees enterprise technology, infrastructure, data and AI platforms, product, and engineering, while working with the segment technology teams to modernize how we build and deliver technology across the company. 
 Adam Smith was named Chairman of Direct-to-Consumer for Disney Entertainment and is responsible for Disney’s global entertainment SVOD business, while leading strategy, development, and continued innovation across our streaming platforms, proprietary advertising technology, and emerging technologies. 
 INCREASING REACH AND ENGAGEMENT 
 We are successfully executing on our strategy to reach more fans around the world across both digital and physical experiences. As AI accelerates the growth in content supply over time, we believe that the strength of our franchises and live sports offering, combined with the depth of our consumer relationships, will strengthen our competitive advantage. 
 The record fiscal fourth quarter at Disney Experiences was propelled by an 11% increase in global guests (1) (4% increase excluding the 53 rd week), driven by continued attendance strength at Walt Disney World and Disneyland Paris, and by increased capacity at Disney Cruise Line following the launches of the Disney Adventure and Disney Destiny earlier in the fiscal year. Our capacity expansion, operational excellence, and unmatched IP portfolio helped Disney Experiences set an all-time global guests record, up 5% for the full fiscal year (4% excluding the 53 rd week).

 We continued to see headwinds from lower international attendance at our domestic parks in the quarter, but as expected, those headwinds moderated from the year-over-year impact observed in the third quarter. Domestic parks attendance was up 11% in the quarter (3% excluding the 53 rd week). 
 Meanwhile, Disneyland Paris continued to deliver strong growth, benefiting from recent investment and expansion, including World of Frozen. In contrast, attendance at our Hong Kong and Shanghai parks worsened during the quarter and we expect this pressure to continue in fiscal 2027. We expect growth in

 Beyond our international parks, we are focused on bringing Disney+ to more households outside the U.S. We are pleased with the progress we saw in the quarter across EMEA, LATAM, and APAC, including healthy engagement growth, which we believe directly supports revenue growth. 
 Over the last two years we released nine of our most-watched international originals of all time on Disney+. Key local originals in Q4 included A Shop for Killers Season 2 (APAC) and Soy Luna: Let’s Roll Again (LATAM). And The Remarried Empress, our latest breakout hit from South Korea which debuted earlier this month, is our biggest local original premiere to date. We believe our local free-to-air collaborations are a meaningful competitive advantage – we now have more than 30 across the regions. Local content remains one of the strongest drivers of international subscriber acquisition and engagement, and over the next three years we plan to roughly triple our delivery of local originals. 
 We continue to see the power of ESPN and live sports in our results. Both the fourth quarter and the full fiscal year delivered the most consumption on the ESPN networks since fiscal 2015, with hours watched up 4% and 10%, respectively (1) . On the full year, the ESPN networks captured the largest share of sports viewership, both overall and among adults 18-49, even with the Winter Olympics and World Cup airing on competitor networks in the US (2) . Across digital and social, ESPN remained No. 1 in the U.S. Sports Category in September with a monthly record 237 million unique users, while reaching 96% of internet users 35-44 and 99% of internet users 25-34 (3) . 
 In total, we are reaching more consumers in more seamless, engaging ways around the world. We are also breaking down legacy processes and building new muscle to grow faster and deliver sustainable returns over time. 
 CAPITAL ALLOCATION AND OTHER MATTERS 
 Last quarter, we said we were mid-stream on an efficiency program to create incremental capacity to drive growth. We now expect the program to realize annual run-rate operating expense savings of approximately $3 billion by fiscal 2029. While these efficiencies will be used primarily to fund future growth, we do expect a portion to drop to the bottom line over time. 
 We repurchased $1.8 billion of Disney shares in the quarter and $9.0 billion across the full fiscal year. Combined with our 2026 dividend, we returned $11.6 billion to shareholders in fiscal 2026. 
 In the fiscal fourth quarter we closed the previously announced transaction to sell our 50% stake in A+E Global Media to an affiliate of co-owner Hearst Corporation. On a full-year basis, A+E contributed approximately $390 million in equity income to Entertainment segment operating income.

 In aggregate, these disclosure changes reflect management’s continued desire to simplify the structure of our businesses and position The Walt Disney Company for continued long-term growth.

 OTHER DEVELOPMENTS 
 On September 30, 2026, the Company sold its 50% interest in A+E Global Media (A+E) to an affiliate of co-owner Hearst Corporation for approximately $1.3 billion in cash. 
 On January 31, 2026, ESPN acquired NFL Network and certain other media assets owned and controlled by NFL Enterprises LLC, including the NFL RedZone channel’s pay TV distribution and NFL Fantasy (collectively the Specified Assets), from NFL Enterprises LLC in exchange for a 10% noncontrolling interest in ESPN (the NFL Transaction). Following the NFL Transaction, the Company has an effective 72% interest in ESPN and Hearst Corporation has an 18% interest. As of January 31, 2026, the operating results attributable to the Specified Assets are included in the Company’s consolidated results. 
 On October 29, 2025, the Company and FuboTV Inc. (FuboTV), a publicly-traded virtual multi-channel video programming distributor (vMVPD) company, combined certain Hulu Live TV assets with the FuboTV business (the Fubo Transaction). The Company has an approximate 70% interest in the combined operations. As of October 29, 2025, the operating results attributable to FuboTV are included in the Company’s consolidated results.

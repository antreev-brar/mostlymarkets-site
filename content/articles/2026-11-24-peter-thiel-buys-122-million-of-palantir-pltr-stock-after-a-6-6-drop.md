Peter Thiel, Palantir's ($PLTR) co-founder and a director, bought 850,100 shares for about **$122.3 million** on Friday, 20 November, according to a Form 4 filed with the SEC on Tuesday and reported by Investing.com. He paid weighted average prices of $142.91 to $144.61 a share, through PLTR Holdings II, an entity he controls, under a trading plan he adopted on 19 August.

The timing stands out. Palantir closed at $143.49 that day, down 6.6%, and it has since risen **12.3%** to $161.18, putting the new shares about $15 million ahead on paper. Because the buys ran under a plan set three months earlier, they show what Thiel was willing to pay in August rather than a reaction to last week's slide.

The stock is still down 9.3% this year and sits 22.9% below its October closing high of $209.05, a slide that deepened after its [third-quarter results](/news/2026-11-02/palantir-pltr-beats-q3-estimates-as-us-commercial-sales-overtake-government/) showed [new contracts growing just 6%](/news/2026-11-03/palantir-pltr-shares-slide-as-its-new-contracts-grow-just-6/). At about 70 times expected earnings, one insider purchase does not change what the price assumes about growth.

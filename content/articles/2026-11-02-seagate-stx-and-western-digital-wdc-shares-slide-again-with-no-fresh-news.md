## What happened

Shares of hard-drive maker Seagate Technology ($STX) **slid 5.4%** to $609.56 as of 11:15 a.m. ET on Monday, and rival Western Digital ($WDC) fell 4.5%, while the Nasdaq Composite was up about 0.9%. We found no company news, filing or analyst call on Monday that two outlets tied to the drop, so the cause is no clear news: the move extends a selloff in storage and memory stocks that has run for weeks.

The slide is broad. Sandisk ($SNDK) was down 2.8% and Micron ($MU) 1.9% at the same time. On Friday Seagate fell 8.0% and Western Digital 7.4% as profit-taking swept memory stocks the day Sandisk reported, 24/7 Wall St. reported. After the results, UBS cut its Seagate price target to $750 from $780 and kept a Neutral rating, citing uncertain exabyte shipments, the total storage capacity the industry sells, according to Investing.com and TheFly.

## Why it matters

The business has not cracked. Seagate's quarterly revenue reached $4.23 billion in the quarter to 2 October, up from $2.63 billion a year earlier, and its GAAP gross margin rose to 57.3% from 39.4%, its filing shows. The fear is the next leg: that hyperscalers' orders peak and new capacity, including Western Digital's move to the same high-density drive technology, brings prices down. That is the argument in our [story on why memory stocks have fallen while profits hit records](/news/2026-10-31/why-memory-stocks-have-fallen-for-four-months-while-profits-hit-records/).

## What the market is telling us

Seagate moved 5% or more on 71 of the last 252 sessions, so swings this size are common. Monday's drop leaves it down 13% from Thursday's close of $700.47 and about 44% below its 52-week closing high of $1,094.04 from 22 June, though it was still up 134% this year through Friday. A $1,000 stake five years ago would have been worth $7,237 at Friday's close.

We read Monday as more of the same, not new information: record margins are not stopping the selling, so the stock now trades on when investors think the cycle turns, not on the last quarter.

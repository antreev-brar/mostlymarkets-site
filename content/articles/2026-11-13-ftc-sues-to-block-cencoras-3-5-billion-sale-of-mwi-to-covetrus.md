The Federal Trade Commission sued on Friday morning to block Covetrus, owned by buyout firms Clayton, Dubilier & Rice and TPG, from buying MWI Veterinary Supply from Cencora ($COR) for **$3.5 billion**, Reuters reported, citing the agency's statement. The FTC called MWI Covetrus's closest competitor and argued the deal would leave a highly concentrated market and raise costs for vet clinics and pet owners, Semafor reported.

The suit ends a review that ran from the summer: the companies offered remedies in October that didn't satisfy regulators, MLex reported. For Cencora, the drug wholesaler that owns MWI, the stake is modest, as $3.5 billion is about 5% of its $67.4 billion market value. Its shares **rose 1.3%** to $353.10 by 10:30 a.m. ET, so investors aren't treating the loss of the sale as a blow.

What isn't known yet is whether the companies will fight the suit in court or drop the deal; a court challenge typically takes months, so Cencora's MWI unit stays on its books in the meantime.

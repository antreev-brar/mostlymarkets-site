Walt Disney ($DIS) is preparing thousands of job cuts, and some managers have been asked to find **up to 20%** in annual cost savings, Bloomberg News reported late on Monday, citing people familiar with the plans; Reuters and Variety carried the report. The savings could come from jobs or other spending, and the report did not say how many roles or which divisions. Disney has not commented.

It would be the fourth round of cuts in 2026 under Chief Executive Josh D'Amaro, and the largest, Variety and Reuters reported. D'Amaro wants to put part of the savings back into film and TV programming, technology, theme parks and cruise ships, according to Bloomberg, while Disney contends with weaker box-office revenue, streaming competition and slower international park attendance.

The stock market was shut on Thursday for Thanksgiving, after a week dominated by [tariff headlines](/news/2026-11-25/stock-market-today-the-dow-erases-a-1100-point-drop-as-tariffs-and-ai-fears-fade-by-the-close/). This list looks at mid-sized companies the front page rarely mentions, and at a lesson from the screen: a high operating margin on paper doesn't make a good business on its own.

Our screen ranked companies worth $5 billion to $30 billion by operating margin over the 12 months to their latest quarter, from SEC filings in our data, and left out any stock we listed in the past 14 days. We then judged each against its industry peers on growth, profit trends, cash and price. Two of our skips are losing money, and the third keeps 30 cents of each sales dollar while selling less each year.

```picks
skip: CPNG, IFF, STZ
like: NDSN
```

## 3 stocks we'd skip

### 1. Coupang ($CPNG)
**Operating margin: -3.4% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Coupang is South Korea's biggest online retailer.

1. Even in its best years it barely made money: its operating margin peaked at 1.9% in 2023, against a 13.1% median for the 16 consumer-internet companies in our data.
2. It has posted an operating loss in each quarter of 2026. The third-quarter loss of $450 million included $248 million of costs from a July fire at its Incheon warehouse, the Korea Times reported, after second-quarter charges for fines over its data breach.
3. Growth is fading: revenue rose 23.6% a year over five years but only 7.5% in the latest quarter, in the bottom quarter of its peers.

**The price:** 47.6 times expected earnings, against a 17.1 times peer median, even after the stock fell 42.9% from its 52-week high of 28 November 2025.
**What would change our mind:** Chairman Bom Kim said on the third-quarter call that Coupang aims to restore profitability in product commerce by the middle of next year; two profitable quarters would reopen the case.

### 2. International Flavors & Fragrances ($IFF)
**Operating margin: -2.6% in the 12 months to September 2026.** IFF makes flavors, fragrances and food ingredients for consumer-goods companies.

1. It booked an operating loss in three of the last four fiscal years (2022, 2023 and 2025), and its margin is 14.6 points lower than five years ago, in the bottom quarter of the 28 materials stocks we cover.
2. Its 16.5% a year of revenue growth over five years came mostly from the 2021 merger with DuPont's nutrition arm; over the past two years revenue grew 1.2% a year, against a 5.3% materials median.
3. What changed: on 25 November the European Commission fined it €81.9 million for sharing sensitive information with rivals Givaudan and Firmenich between 2019 and 2023, as part of €471.9 million in fines, FashionUnited and Swissinfo reported.

**The price:** 24.3 times expected earnings, against a 15.9 times materials median, with $6.5 billion of net debt; the stock is up 27.8% this year, so the market is already paying for a recovery.
**What would change our mind:** two quarters in a row with an operating margin back above its 6.7% of fiscal 2024.

### 3. Constellation Brands ($STZ)
**Operating margin: 30.2% in the 12 months to August 2026.** Constellation sells Modelo, Corona and Pacifico beer in the US, plus wine and spirits.

1. Its margin is in the top tenth of the 35 consumer-staples stocks we cover, but revenue fell 4.9% a year over the past two years, the weakest of the group.
2. Beer demand is flat: in the quarter to August, depletions (sales from distributors to stores) fell 0.6% while shipments rose about 5.5%, and it guided to organic sales growth of -1% to +1% for the year, according to its release and Vinetur.
3. It carries **$10.2 billion of net debt**, 3.7 years of operating profit, and members of the founding Sands family sold shares in mid-November, including 150,000 by director Richard Sands at $145, Investing.com and Kalkine reported from filings.

**The price:** 11.8 times expected earnings, the cheapest of these four and well below the 17.8 times staples median. The discount fits a business whose sales are shrinking.
**What would change our mind:** beer depletions turning positive in its next quarterly report, due in early January.

## 1 stock we like

### Nordson ($NDSN)
**Operating margin: 26.9% in the 12 months to July 2026.** Nordson makes precision equipment that dispenses adhesives, coatings and fluids, used in electronics, medical devices and packaging.

1. It keeps getting more profitable: its margin rose from 16.5% in fiscal 2020 to 26.9%, a 9.0-point gain over five years, in the top quarter of the 89 industrials we cover, where the median margin is 17.2%.
2. The profit is cash: free cash flow was 24.3% of revenue in the past 12 months, in the top tenth of its peers, and earnings per share grew 14.8% a year over five years, against an 11.3% median.
3. What changed: in its third quarter to July, sales rose 10% to a record $818 million, organic sales rose about 12%, backlog was up 35%, and it raised its full-year adjusted EPS outlook to **$11.80 to $12.00** from $11.30 to $11.80, its release and Zacks reported.

**The price:** 32.5 times trailing earnings, above its 29.7 times median since August 2024, and 25.4 times expected earnings against a 20.1 times peer median. We like the business more than the entry point.
**What could go wrong:** revenue grew only 5.6% a year over five years, below the 8.0% peer median; if the electronics orders behind the 35% backlog jump fade in its fiscal fourth-quarter report in December, the premium multiple has little to lean on.

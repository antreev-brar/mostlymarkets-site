Amphenol ($APH) reported third-quarter sales of $10.23 billion, up 65% year on year, beating the $9.44 billion analysts expected, according to Zacks consensus data carried by Yahoo Finance and Nasdaq.com. Adjusted earnings of 80 cents a share topped the 73-cent Zacks estimate and were up 74% from a year earlier. The results came out before the market opened on Wednesday, 28 October, and the shares rose 3.5% that day to close at $80.96, then added 1.4% on Thursday to $82.08.

```highlights

- **Sales:** $10.23 billion vs analyst estimates of $9.44 billion (65% year on year, 37% organic, an 8% beat)
- **Adjusted EPS:** $0.80 vs analyst estimates of $0.73 (a 10% beat); GAAP EPS $0.79
- **Orders:** $12.17 billion, a book-to-bill ratio of 1.19
- **IT datacom sales:** up 117% year on year, 48% of total sales
- **Q4 guidance:** sales of $10.3-10.5 billion, adjusted EPS of $0.81-0.83
- **Market value:** $202 billion at Thursday's close
```

Per-share figures reflect the two-for-one stock split Amphenol completed in early September. Before the split, the company had guided to adjusted earnings of $1.40 to $1.42 a share for the quarter, or 70 to 71 cents on the new share count.

## What stood out

The engine is IT datacom, Amphenol's business selling connectors, cables, and related parts into data centers. Sales there rose 117% from a year earlier, or 77% excluding acquisitions, and the market now makes up 48% of the company's revenue, according to the release and call coverage by Yahoo Finance and TradingKey.

Orders point to more growth ahead. Amphenol booked $12.17 billion of new orders, up 99% year on year, for a book-to-bill ratio of 1.19. That means it took in $1.19 of new business for every dollar it shipped, Benzinga and Investing.com reported.

Acquisitions added to growth, but most of the gain was organic: sales rose 37% on that basis. Both sales and adjusted EPS came in above the top of the company's own guidance, Amphenol said in the release.

## The outlook

For the fourth quarter, Amphenol expects sales of $10.3 billion to $10.5 billion and adjusted earnings of 81 to 83 cents a share. At the midpoint that would be about 62% sales growth from the $6.44 billion it reported a year earlier.

The company also raised its 2026 outlook to sales of $36.9 billion to $37.1 billion and adjusted earnings of $2.82 to $2.84 a share. The forecasts exclude deals that have not closed yet. Alongside the results, Amphenol raised its quarterly dividend 20% to 15 cents a share and approved a new $4 billion, three-year share buyback.

## Our take

This was a clean quarter: an 8% sales beat, a 10% profit beat, and orders running well ahead of shipments. The modest share reaction suggests investors expected strength, since the stock still sits 7.4% below its 52-week closing high of $88.62 from 6 October. Next quarter we'd watch whether book-to-bill stays above 1 and how much of IT datacom's growth comes without help from acquisitions.

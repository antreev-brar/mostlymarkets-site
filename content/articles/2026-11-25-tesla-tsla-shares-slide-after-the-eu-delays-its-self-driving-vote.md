## What happened
Shares of Tesla ($TSLA) **slid 5.7%** to $362.80 by 10:30 a.m. ET on Wednesday after the European Union postponed its vote on approving Tesla's supervised self-driving software across the bloc. A European Commission spokesperson told Reuters the committee meeting, set for 1 December, will now take place on **15 December**, according to reports carried by Investing.com and TheNextWeb.

The committee has discussed Tesla's request twice, in June and October, without voting, Reuters reported. Approval needs at least 15 member states that hold 65% of the EU's population. The drop came on a bad day for the market: the Nasdaq Composite was down 3.5% at the same time, which CNN tied to doubts about AI spending, new tariff proposals and hotter inflation. IBTimes and Invezz also pointed to unease over the planned merger talks with SpaceX.

## Why it matters
Tesla's case for its valuation leans on self-driving software it can sell as a subscription, and Europe is the biggest market where it isn't yet allowed bloc-wide. Countries can still approve it one by one: Italy became [the first big EU market to do so](/news/2026-11-20/italy-approves-teslas-tsla-supervised-self-driving-system-the-first-big-eu-market/) last week, after the Netherlands. France has refused to recognize the Dutch approval, and Germany has called the name "Full Self-Driving" misleading, Reuters reported, so a 15 December vote is no sure thing.

The other open question is SpaceX: Tesla and SpaceX [named directors to negotiate a merger](/news/2026-11-24/tesla-tsla-and-spacex-name-five-directors-to-negotiate-a-merger/) on Monday, and no terms have been set.

## What the market is telling us
Tesla's shares have moved 5% or more on 18 of the last 252 sessions, and the last drop this big was on 20 November, when they fell 7.6%. At Tuesday's close of $384.68, the stock was down 14.5% this year and 21.5% below its 16 December 2025 high. We read today's drop as mostly the market's and partly Europe's: a two-week delay changes little for Tesla's sales, and the next test is the 15 December vote itself.

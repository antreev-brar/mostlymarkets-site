OpenAI filed a countersuit against Apple ($AAPL) in a California court on Friday, accusing it of stealing trade secrets and of "coercing and threatening" suppliers that work with OpenAI, the Financial Times reported. CNBC and Investing.com carried the report, which Reuters relayed; the suit's specific trade secrets and suppliers have not been named.

It escalates a fight over AI hardware that Apple started in July, when it sued OpenAI, the device company io Products and two former Apple employees, alleging that confidential engineering details were taken to help OpenAI build consumer devices. OpenAI denies that and has asked the judge to dismiss Apple's suit. The supplier claim matters most for Apple: it turns a case about what ex-employees took into one about how Apple treats the component makers both companies use. Apple's shares closed 0.8% higher at $358.54 in a broad tech rally covered in [our market wrap](/news/2026-11-27/stock-market-today-memory-stocks-lead-a-black-friday-rally-as-the-nasdaq-gains-2-3/).

The claims are OpenAI's allegations, not court findings. The next step is Apple's response in court, and a ruling on OpenAI's request to dismiss the original suit.

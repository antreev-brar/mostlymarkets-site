Regeneron Pharmaceuticals ($REGN) reported third-quarter revenue of $5.12 billion on Friday morning, up 36% year on year. Adjusted earnings rose 70% to **$20.07 a share**, far above the $16.24 analysts expected, according to LSEG data cited by Reuters. The stock soared 13.3% to $927.89 at the close, its biggest one-day gain since September 2022 and a 52-week high.

- **Revenue:** $5.12 billion, up 36% year on year; estimates ranged from about $4.5 billion to $4.6 billion
- **Adjusted EPS:** $20.07 vs the $16.24 LSEG consensus (a 24% beat), up 70%; GAAP EPS $18.45, up 35%
- **Dupixent global sales (recorded by partner Sanofi):** a record $6.5 billion, up 35%; US sales above $5 billion for the first time

- **Libtayo global sales:** a record $550 million, up 51% at constant currency
- **Market value:** $96 billion at Friday's close

## What stood out

**Dupixent did most of the work.** Global sales of the eczema and asthma drug, which Regeneron shares with Sanofi, rose 35% to **$6.5 billion**, the release showed. Sanofi books the sales and Regeneron takes a share of the profit, so faster growth at this size flows almost straight into Regeneron's earnings, which is why EPS grew twice as fast as revenue.

*Verdict: the main engine is accelerating, not maturing.*

**The eye-drug problem is easing.** Eylea HD, the longer-lasting version of Regeneron's macular-degeneration drug, sold a record $699 million in the US, up 62%. Investors had worried that cheaper copies of the original Eylea would shrink the franchise, and Eylea HD's growth shows patients moving to the newer version.

**Cancer is a third leg.** Libtayo, its immunotherapy, set a record at $550 million, up 51% at constant currency.

## The outlook

Regeneron does not give an EPS forecast. Analysts moved their targets up after the report: JPMorgan raised its price target to $1,050 from $950 and kept an overweight rating, Raymond James raised its to $1,020, and Citigroup raised its to $825 from $740 while staying at neutral, according to Investing.com and TradingView.

## Our take

We were impressed by the size of the beat: 24% on EPS, against the 2% to 4% that is typical, with all three main products at records. On the other hand, much of the gain depends on one partnered drug, and Regeneron's operating margin has fallen 17.1 points over five years, by our data, in the bottom quarter of our drugmaker group. Overall, a strong quarter that resets what Regeneron can earn.

Even after the jump, the stock trades at 14.3 times forward earnings, against an 18.1 times median for our drugmakers, and Regeneron has cut its share count 9.1% in two years. The question for the fourth-quarter report in early 2027 is whether Dupixent's growth holds above 30%; at that pace, today's price still looks undemanding.

The five biggest moves among companies worth $10 billion or more on Tuesday 17 November were all drops, measured close to close, and three were business-software stocks falling with no company news. Two had news of their own: JD.com's results and Nike's investor day.

**Salesforce ($CRM)**, -9.5%: the business-software maker had its worst day since 30 May 2024 in our data, with no company news, as funds cut tech holdings by the most since 2006 in Bank of America's November survey, Bloomberg and CNBC reported, and software sold off ahead of Anthropic's IPO, Benzinga reported. It is down 26.6% this year. [Full story](/news/2026-11-18/wall-street-is-betting-ai-will-empty-softwares-seats-3-numbers-will-decide-it/)

**JD.com ($JD)**, -7.7%: the Chinese online retailer's third-quarter revenue rose 3.5% to 309.6 billion yuan, short of estimates, as Chinese shoppers stayed cautious, Investing.com and the South China Morning Post reported. It was the stock's biggest fall since April 2025. [Full story](/news/2026-11-17/jd-com-jd-misses-q3-revenue-estimates-at-309-6-billion-yuan-as-shoppers-hold-back/)

**Intuit ($INTU)**, -7.2%: the TurboTax and QuickBooks owner fell in the same software sell-off, with no news of its own. At $320.79 it is down 51.6% this year, more than half below its December high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-17/salesforce-mongodb-and-sap-shares-slide-what-happened/)

**ServiceNow ($NOW)**, -6.5%: the workflow-software company fell with its peers, Benzinga and MLQ reported, and is down 39.4% this year after its record fall on 29 October. [Full story](/news/2026-11-18/wall-street-is-betting-ai-will-empty-softwares-seats-3-numbers-will-decide-it/)

**Nike ($NKE)**, -6.3%: the sportswear maker gave up an early gain of as much as 6% after its investor day warned that sales may fall for two more years before growth returns in fiscal 2029, Yahoo Finance and FX Leaders reported. It was its biggest fall since 1 April. [Full story](/news/2026-11-17/nike-nke-targets-5-billion-of-added-sales-and-a-12-to-14-margin-by-2031/)

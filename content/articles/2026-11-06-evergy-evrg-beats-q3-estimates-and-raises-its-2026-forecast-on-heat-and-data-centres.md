Evergy ($EVRG) reported third-quarter adjusted earnings of **$2.58 a share** before the open on Thursday, up from $2.03 a year earlier and 12% above the $2.30 Zacks consensus, helped by a hot summer and growing demand. The Kansas and Missouri utility raised its 2026 forecast and signed a letter of intent with Digital Realty ($DLR) for a 1.4-gigawatt data-centre expansion. The shares **rose 1.4% to $87.41**, on a day the S&P 500 fell 4.4%.

## What stood out

**Weather did a lot of the lifting.** The release credited warmer weather first, then recovery of grid investments through rates, growth in weather-adjusted demand and higher revenue from large customers, partly offset by higher operating and depreciation costs. Evergy and the Southwest Power Pool, its regional grid, both set peak demand records, Chairman and Chief Executive David Campbell said in the release.

*Verdict: a beat that is partly luck, which is why the forecast raise matters more than the quarter.*

**The data-centre pipeline keeps growing.** Evergy signed amendments to three existing large-customer contracts worth about **250 megawatts** of extra load, plus the Digital Realty letter of intent. Campbell said the deals "demonstrate the sustained interest from large customers in locating and expanding within our Kansas and Missouri service territories."

## The outlook

Evergy raised its 2026 adjusted EPS range to **$4.35 to $4.50**, from $4.14 to $4.34, which it said reflects results ahead of plan and warmer weather. It kept its growth target of 6% to 8% or more a year through 2030, measured from the original $4.24 midpoint, so this year's weather windfall does not raise the base. It also raised its dividend 4% to $0.7225 a quarter.

## Our take

We were impressed by the large-customer momentum: a 1.4-gigawatt prospect is big for a utility of Evergy's size. On the other hand, much of the beat came from heat that won't repeat, and its net debt of $15.5 billion, 9.9 years of operating profit by our data, means building for data centres will need more borrowing and favorable rate cases. Overall, a solid quarter that strengthens the growth story.

Evergy is up **20.6% this year** and within 1% of its July high, and at 19.4 times forward earnings it trades about at the 19.0 utility median by our data, so the market is paying for the plan but not yet for the Digital Realty project. The next test is whether the letter of intent becomes a signed service agreement.

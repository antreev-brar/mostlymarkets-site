Texas Pacific Land ($TPL), one of the biggest landowners in the Permian Basin oil field, agreed on Monday to buy EagleRock Land in an all-stock deal valued at about **$3 billion**, the companies said in a release filed with the SEC, as Investing.com and Reuters reported. Each EagleRock Class A share becomes 0.0831 Texas Pacific Land shares, worth about $23.56 at Texas Pacific Land's five-day average price to 20 November.

The deal is worth about a sixth of Texas Pacific Land's $18.4 billion market value, and its holders would keep about **86%** of the combined company. The companies expect about $45 million a year of benefits, $20 million of it from corporate cost cuts, and a combined holding of about 1 million acres of surface land plus about 220,000 barrels a day of capacity to handle the salty water that comes up with oil and gas.

Texas Pacific Land rose 2.5% to $274.00 before the open, as of 7:00 a.m. ET, after falling 3.7% on Friday; the stock is down 6.9% this year. The deal still needs the usual approvals and is expected to close in the first half of 2027.

The Securities and Exchange Commission's investigation of Nvidia ($NVDA) is examining potential accounting issues, Reuters reported on Friday afternoon, citing two people briefed on the matter. Reuters could not determine when the inquiry began or which accounting questions it covers; the SEC declined to comment and Nvidia did not immediately respond.

It widens a probe the Wall Street Journal first reported on Tuesday as a look at [whether Nvidia's disclosures misled investors](/news/2026-11-17/sec-probes-nvidia-nvda-disclosures-and-muddy-waters-says-it-is-short/), two days before California's attorney general [served the company a subpoena](/news/2026-11-19/california-subpoenas-nvidia-nvda-over-china-chips-and-what-investors-were-told/). Questions about the numbers themselves matter more than questions about disclosure, because investors have been valuing Nvidia on its reported earnings. The stock was **down 9.0%** at $132.83 as of 2:15 p.m. ET, 44.5% below its 6 October closing high, in a sell-off across AI stocks; Investing.com said it was unclear how much of the drop was due to the report.

An SEC inquiry is not a finding of wrongdoing and can end without action. The next thing to watch is whether Nvidia responds publicly, and whether it says the SEC has contacted it.

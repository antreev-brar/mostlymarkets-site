Alibaba ($BABA) unveiled Qwen4-Max, its new flagship AI model, late on Sunday New York time, saying it cuts the average cost of a task by **more than 60%** against its predecessor, Qwen3.8-Max, and that its training added security checks to make the code it writes safer, Reuters, The Star and Startup Fortune reported. Developers pay $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens, The Next Web reported. It is the first Qwen flagship that Alibaba will not release with open weights, though smaller Qwen4 models will be.

Alibaba says the model leads on coding tests, but the cost figure comes from its own benchmark, and independent rankings put it behind models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Moonshot AI, Trending Topics reported. The bet is on price: cheap, capable models are how Alibaba sells more cloud computing at home while its access to Nvidia ($NVDA) chips is in doubt ([our story](/news/2026-11-13/alibaba-baba-shares-slide-on-fears-over-its-access-to-nvidia-chips/)).

**F5 ($FFIV)**, -20.4%: The maker of software and hardware that keeps big websites and apps running was the worst stock in the S&P 500 after it forecast revenue growth of only 8% to 9% for its new fiscal year and a lower gross margin as memory parts cost more, and Piper Sandler cut the stock to Neutral. [Full story](/news/2026-10-28/f5-ffiv-shares-plunge-after-a-soft-forecast-and-a-piper-sandler-downgrade/)

**Vertiv ($VRT)**, +16.5%: The maker of power and cooling equipment for data centers was the best stock in the S&P 500 and had its best day since February after its organic orders more than doubled and it raised its 2026 sales forecast to $14.45 billion to $14.85 billion. [Full story](/news/2026-10-28/vertiv-vrt-shares-soar-as-its-orders-more-than-double/)

**Oracle ($ORCL)**, +10.7%: The database and cloud company had its best day since May after its investor day set a fiscal 2030 revenue target of $225 billion or more and capped new money from bond and share sales at $20 billion through fiscal 2030. [Full story](/news/2026-10-28/oracle-orcl-shares-soar-after-it-sets-a-225-billion-revenue-target/)

**Alphabet ($GOOGL)**, -10.8% after hours: Google's parent fell in late trading after third-quarter revenue of $127.58 billion came in just under estimates and search revenue missed, even though Google Cloud sales more than doubled to $30.71 billion. The move is as of 5:53 p.m. ET. [Full story](/news/2026-10-28/alphabet-googl-q3-cloud-sales-double-stock-falls-11-after-hours/)

**Sandisk ($SNDK)**, +7.7%: The flash-memory maker led a rally in memory stocks after South Korea's SK hynix posted its first quarter with more than 100 trillion won in revenue and said demand for memory will outrun supply through 2030; Micron ($MU) rose 5.9%. [Full story](/news/2026-10-28/sandisk-sndk-and-micron-mu-shares-jump-after-sk-hynixs-record-quarter/)

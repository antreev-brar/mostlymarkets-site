AppLovin ($APP) is among several companies in talks to buy Criteo, the French advertising-technology company, Reuters reported on Tuesday afternoon, citing two people familiar with the matter. The price and terms were not clear, and a deal could be announced before the end of the year or not at all, Reuters said; Seeking Alpha and MarketScreener carried the report.

At a market value of about **$1 billion**, Criteo would be less than 1% of AppLovin's $132.2 billion in our data, so the money matters less than the direction. AppLovin, whose software places ads inside mobile apps, has grown on that engine, with third-quarter sales up 55% ([our recap](/news/2026-11-05/applovin-app-soars-31-as-q3-sales-rise-55-and-its-forecast-tops-estimates/)); Criteo places ads for retailers and brands across the web. Criteo has been weighing a sale since private-equity firms Vista Equity Partners and Quinti Capital approached it, Reuters and Pulse 2 reported.

AppLovin closed **down 3.9%** at $398.94 on Tuesday, when the Nasdaq fell 0.7%, and was up 0.5% before the open on Wednesday.

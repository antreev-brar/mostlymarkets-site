Hewlett Packard Enterprise ($HPE) reports fiscal fourth-quarter results after the close on Monday, 23 November, the first of the AI server makers to report since the S&P 500 fell into a correction. The stock closed Friday at $40.91, **down 10.1%** on the day and 44.3% below its 9 October high, though still up 70.3% this year.

Last quarter, HPE's revenue rose 34% to a record $12.2 billion and adjusted earnings of $1.11 a share beat the roughly 93 cents expected, its release and StorageNewsletter showed. It raised its full-year earnings forecast to $3.75 to $3.85 a share, and the stock rose 5.0% on 3 September in our data.

## What analysts expect

Analysts expect adjusted earnings of **$1.28 a share**, up from 62 cents a year earlier, on revenue of $14.4 billion, against $9.68 billion a year ago, according to the Zacks consensus cited by TradingView and Yahoo Finance. Both sit inside HPE's own forecast of $13.9 billion to $14.8 billion and $1.20 to $1.30 a share. Part of the year-on-year jump comes from Juniper Networks, the networking company HPE now owns.

## What to watch

- **AI systems orders.** AI systems revenue was almost $1.6 billion last quarter, the release said. After Nvidia's $130 billion forecast and the sell-off since, new orders and the backlog will show whether customers are still buying.
- **Networking margins.** The Juniper-led networking business earned a 22.0% operating margin on $2.9 billion of revenue last quarter, HPE said, as StorageNewsletter reported. Holding that margin is the main path to better overall profit for a company whose operating margin was 6.2% over the last 12 months in our data.
- **Memory costs and the 2027 outlook.** Citi cut its HPE target to $80 from $92 last week, citing worries about peak AI demand and memory costs, GuruFocus and Invezz reported. Any comment on fiscal 2027 margins will matter more than the quarter. Options imply a move of about 14% either way, TipRanks and BigGo Finance reported.

## How the stock has done

HPE is down 26.3% over the past month, including an 11.8% drop on 5 November when AI hardware stocks sold off. At 8.8 times expected earnings, it trades well below the 16.2 times median of the hardware makers we cover. Dell reports a day later, on Tuesday; [our breakdown of Dell](/news/2026-11-22/dell-technologies-dell-is-53-below-its-high-before-tuesdays-report-is-the-business-broken/) covers the same fears.

Eli Lilly ($LLY) will sell its obesity drugs Zepbound and Foundayo through the Hims & Hers ($HIMS) telehealth platform, the two companies said on Friday, and Hims & Hers shares **rose 8.1%** to $26.00. Patients prescribed the drugs on the platform can buy them there at $299 a month for Zepbound and from $149 for Foundayo, the same self-pay prices Lilly charges on its own LillyDirect site, Reuters and the companies' release said.

The deal gives Hims & Hers the branded drugs of both big makers: it already sold Novo Nordisk's ($NVO) Wegovy, TIKR reported. Hims also committed never to sell compounded copies of Lilly's drugs, the release said. Leerink Partners analyst Michael Cherny called the deal "a clear positive," according to TIKR.

The stock had fallen 9.8% on Thursday, and is still down 19.9% this year and 37.4% below its high of a year ago. Neither company said how the revenue from each sale will be split, which decides how much the deal adds to Hims & Hers' profit.

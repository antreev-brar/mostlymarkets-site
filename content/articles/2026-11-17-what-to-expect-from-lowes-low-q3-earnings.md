Lowe's ($LOW) reports its fiscal third quarter, the three months to October, before the open on Wednesday, 18 November, a day after Home Depot ($HD) beat estimates and still fell 2.5%. Lowe's **fell 1.99%** to $220.46 on Tuesday alongside its rival, but the stock is up 22.8% from its 5 October closing low of $179.50 in our data.

Last quarter was mixed. Adjusted EPS of $4.40 beat the $4.22 consensus, helped by an 11-cent tariff refund, but comparable sales, those at stores open a year or more, **rose only 0.2%** against the 0.8% analysts expected, according to LSEG data cited by Reuters. Lowe's cut its fiscal 2026 forecast to the bottom of its old ranges: about $92 billion of sales, flat comparable sales and adjusted EPS of about $12.25, its release showed. The stock fell about 3% before the open on 19 August, then closed up 2.0%.

## What analysts expect

The Zacks consensus is **$2.86 a share** on an adjusted basis, down from $3.06 a year earlier, on revenue of $22.38 billion, up 7.5%, Zacks and Nasdaq's calendar show; Earnings Whispers has $2.85. That matches management's own August forecast for third-quarter adjusted EPS about 7% below last year's, with comparable sales in line with the flat full-year outlook, Hudson Labs and Earnings Whispers reported.

Almost none of that sales growth is from existing stores. Lowe's closed its $8.8 billion purchase of Foundation Building Materials, a supplier to professional builders, on 9 October 2025, so last year's quarter held only its final three weeks. The debate this report settles: **is buying pro customers lifting sales while the do-it-yourself shopper keeps shrinking the profit?** Sales up 7.5% with earnings down 7% means thinner margins.

## What to watch

- **Comparable sales.** Anything below zero would put the flat full-year forecast at risk; Home Depot's 2.2% on Tuesday ([our recap](/news/2026-11-17/home-depot-hd-tops-q3-estimates-and-names-cfo-richard-mcphail-ceo/)) sets the bar.
- **The $12.25 forecast.** It already sits at the old range's low end; a further cut would say the August reset wasn't enough.
- **Pro against DIY.** Management credited professional and online sales for August's growth; another quarter of weak DIY demand would leave the acquisitions doing all the work.

## How the stock has done

Lowe's is down 8.6% this year and up 12.5% over the past month, 23.3% below its 13 February closing high of $287.39. It trades at 17.7 times forward earnings, below the 19.7 times median of our consumer peers and Home Depot's 22.1 times.

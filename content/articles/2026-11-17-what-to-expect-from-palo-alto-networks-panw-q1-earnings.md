Palo Alto Networks ($PANW) reports its fiscal first quarter, the three months to October, after the close on Wednesday, 18 November, Earnings Whispers and TIKR reported. The cybersecurity company's stock **fell 2.2%** to $309.29 on Tuesday as software stocks [led a market rout](/news/2026-11-17/stock-market-today-salesforce-leads-a-software-rout-as-the-dow-drops-849-points/), 5.8% below its close the day after its last report.

Last quarter, revenue **rose 34%** to $3.41 billion, above the $3.35 billion analysts expected according to FactSet, and adjusted EPS of $1.02 beat the $0.98 consensus, Finviz and Zacks reported. Next-generation security ARR, the yearly value of its newer subscription products, rose 63% to $9.10 billion, its release showed. But it posted a GAAP loss of $0.35 a share, and the stock fell 9.3% on 2 September in our data, as SiliconANGLE and TS2 pointed to margins and the GAAP loss.

## What analysts expect

The consensus is **$0.97 a share** on an adjusted basis, up 4.3% from $0.93 a year earlier, on revenue of $3.31 billion, up 33.6%, Zacks reported; Yahoo Finance's data shows the same. Both sit inside the company's guidance of $0.96 to $0.98 and $3.30 billion to $3.31 billion.

The debate this report settles: **how much of the growth is bought?** The CyberArk and Chronosphere deals flatter the 34% growth rate; CyberArk closed only in February. Management also said fiscal 2026 ARR included a nine-figure lift from one AI-model customer's move onto Chronosphere that ends this quarter, Zacks and Earnings Whispers reported. For the full year it guided ARR growth of 22% to 23%, about a third of last quarter's pace.

## What to watch

- **ARR.** The company guided $9.54 billion to $9.56 billion; a figure at the top shows demand holding as the Chronosphere boost fades.
- **The fiscal 2027 forecast.** Revenue of **$14.10 billion to $14.20 billion** and adjusted EPS of $4.16 to $4.19; a cut after a 10.9% fall in a month would test the stock.
- **GAAP profit.** A second GAAP loss would revive September's margin worry. Fortinet ($FTNT) [plunged 20.1%](/news/2026-10-29/fortinet-ftnt-shares-plunge-after-it-cuts-its-service-revenue-forecast/) on 29 October after cutting a forecast, and Palo Alto fell 3.5% with it.

## How the stock has done

Palo Alto closed at $309.29 on Tuesday, up 67.9% this year but down 10.9% over the past month and 26.3% below its closing high of $419.91 from 6 October. It **fell 9.6%** on 5 November, the day the Nasdaq [sank 5.5% on midterm AI fears](/news/2026-11-05/stock-market-today-nasdaq-sinks-5-5-into-a-correction-on-midterm-ai-fears/). It trades at 73.9 times forward earnings, against a 25.6 times median for its software peers in our data.

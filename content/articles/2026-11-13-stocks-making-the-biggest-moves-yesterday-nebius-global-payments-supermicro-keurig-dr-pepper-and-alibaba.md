All five of the biggest moves among companies worth $10 billion or more on Thursday 12 November were drops, measured close to close. Three of them trace back to the same place: the AI buildout, on the day Nvidia's co-founder resigned as its chief executive.

**Nebius Group ($NBIS)**, -16.8%: the AI cloud company's third-quarter revenue rose 571% to $980.8 million, above estimates, but it said 2026 sales would land at the low end of its $3 billion to $3.4 billion range as data-center delays push revenue into 2027, TheStreet and Benzinga reported. It closed at $128.64, its worst day since 1 July. [Full story](/news/2026-11-13/nebius-nbis-q3-sales-jump-571-but-it-trims-its-2026-outlook-and-plunges-17/)

**Global Payments ($GPN)**, -12.0%: the payments processor lost its takeover premium after Reuters reported that buyout firms CD&R and GTCR had dropped talks to take it private, Investing.com and TipRanks reported. It was the stock's worst day since April 2025, two days after it closed at a 52-week high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-13/global-payments-gpn-plunges-12-as-buyout-firms-drop-takeover-talks/)

**Supermicro ($SMCI)**, -11.3%: the AI-server maker fell after Bloomberg reported that federal prosecutors in Manhattan are examining whether its products were smuggled into China by entities linked to Alice Huang, the former executive at the center of the week's Nvidia reports, a story Benzinga and Yahoo Finance carried. [Full story](/news/2026-11-13/supermicro-smci-shares-plunge-on-a-report-of-a-doj-smuggling-inquiry/)

**Keurig Dr Pepper ($KDP)**, -7.4%: the soft-drink and coffee company launched a $2 billion sale of new shares to pay down debt before it splits into two companies, Investing.com and Benzinga reported, diluting existing holders. It was the stock's worst day since August 2025. [Full story](/news/2026-11-13/keurig-dr-pepper-kdp-shares-slide-after-a-2-billion-stock-sale-to-cut-debt/)

**Alibaba ($BABA)**, -7.1%: the Chinese e-commerce and cloud company fell on fears that Washington will cut its access to advanced AI chips, after the Nvidia scandal put its chip supplier in the spotlight and a report said US curbs on remote chip access are near, Bloomberg and Proactive Investors reported. [Full story](/news/2026-11-13/alibaba-baba-shares-slide-on-fears-over-its-access-to-nvidia-chips/)

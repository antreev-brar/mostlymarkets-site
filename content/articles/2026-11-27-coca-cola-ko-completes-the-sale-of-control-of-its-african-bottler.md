Coca-Cola HBC, Coca-Cola's Swiss-based bottling partner, said on Friday it had completed its purchase of 75% of Coca-Cola Beverages Africa for a combined **$2.6 billion**, the price set when the deal was signed. Coca-Cola ($KO) sold 41.52% of the 66.52% it owned, and the Gutsche family investment group sold its whole 33.48%, according to Coca-Cola's announcement of the deal in October 2025 and the completion notice.

The sale moves another big bottler off Coca-Cola's books, which the company has called a significant step in selling the bottling plants it owns or controls, Dow Jones reported at signing. The deal valued all of the African bottler at **$3.4 billion**. Coca-Cola keeps 25%, and Coca-Cola HBC has an option to buy that stake within six years, the companies said. Coca-Cola HBC said the deal adds 14 African markets and makes it the second-largest Coca-Cola bottler by volume.

What isn't known yet is how much cash Coca-Cola itself receives and what it does with it: neither company has broken out its share of the price. Coca-Cola's shares were down 0.4% at $97.19 in premarket trading as of 6:15 a.m. ET.

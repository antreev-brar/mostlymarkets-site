**Zscaler ($ZS)**, +23.0%: the security-software company had the biggest weekly gain in our coverage, mostly in two jumps. It rose 7.8% on Wednesday, when PHP rushed out emergency fixes for two critical flaws in the language behind an estimated 70% of websites and security stocks rallied, Investing.com and TIKR reported. It added 7.3% on Friday after an Evercore ISI survey of resellers found security budgets firming into year-end, 24/7 Wall St reported. [Full story on Friday's security rally](/news/2026-10-23/why-crowdstrike-crwd-stock-is-soaring-today/)

**Hasbro ($HAS)**, +18.9%: the toy and games maker had its best week in six years, Stocktwits reported, most of it from an 11.2% jump on Tuesday after its third-quarter results. Revenue rose 15%, Magic: The Gathering topped $600 million in a quarter for the first time, and Hasbro raised its 2026 outlook and doubled its share buyback target for the year, the company said in its results release.

**Lam Research ($LRCX)**, -12.8%: almost all of the chip-equipment maker's loss came on Thursday, when it sank 11.8% after warning that short supplies of clean-room space and parts will keep limiting chipmakers into 2027. KLA and Applied Materials slid with it. [Full story](/news/2026-10-22/lam-research-lrcx-kla-klac-and-applied-materials-amat-shares-slide-what-happened/)

**Meta ($META)**, -9.4%: the social media company lost 7.0% on Thursday after reporting it spent $34.91 billion on data centers and equipment in the quarter, turning free cash flow negative, and unveiled nonvoting shares it could sell to raise money. [Full story](/news/2026-10-22/meta-meta-shares-slide-after-free-cash-flow-turns-negative/)

**Moderna ($MRNA)**, -8.2%: the vaccine maker gained almost 13% in the first three sessions of the week, then plunged 17.6% on Thursday when Phase 3 results for its melanoma vaccine with Merck appeared online two days early. The results showed a 42% cut in the risk of the cancer returning or death, below the 49% an earlier, smaller study had shown. [Full story](/news/2026-10-22/why-moderna-mrna-stock-is-sinking-today/)

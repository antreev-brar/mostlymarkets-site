Novartis ($NVS) said on Monday that Leqvio, its cholesterol drug given as an injection twice a year, **met the main goal** of ORION-4, a Phase III trial in people with heart disease caused by clogged arteries, according to its release, as Reuters and Seeking Alpha reported. Added to standard treatment, Leqvio produced a statistically significant reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events, the trial measure that groups events such as heart attacks and strokes, compared with a placebo.

It is the first outcomes trial of an siRNA drug, a medicine that silences the gene behind a disease-causing protein, in this group of patients. Until now Leqvio's label rested on how far it lowers LDL cholesterol; ORION-4 is the first trial to test whether the drug itself prevents cardiovascular events. Novartis had said in August that it expected ORION-4 to read out in 2027, so the result came early.

Novartis gave no figure for the size of the reduction. Its US shares rose 1.2% to $152.31 before the open, as of 6:10 a.m. ET. Full results are due in the first half of 2027.

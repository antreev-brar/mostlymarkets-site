Private-equity firm TPG and payments company Corpay () agreed late on Thursday to take Bill Holdings private for **$63.25 a share in cash**, a fully diluted equity value of about $6 billion, Investing.com and Private Equity Wire reported. The price is 31% above Bill's 60-day average through 16 November, the last full trading day before takeover reports surfaced.

Corpay will invest $1.1 billion for roughly a third of the company, with an option to buy all of it later, the same setup it used with TPG to buy AvidXchange, which closed in October 2025. Bill, which sells bill-payment and invoicing software to small businesses, agreed to the sale after activist investors Starboard Value and Elliott Management pushed for one. For Corpay, which was up 44.3% this year at Thursday's close of $434.29, it adds a second accounts-payable business on the same buy-later terms.

The deal needs shareholder and regulatory approval and is expected to close in the first half of 2027. Corpay's stock had not traded on the news by 5:25 a.m. ET.

Pershing Square Chief Executive Bill Ackman said on X on Wednesday that the board of Nvidia ($NVDA) is "not acting in the best interest of shareholders nor in a manner consistent with their fiduciary duties" over Jensen Huang's exit, Seeking Alpha and Investing.com reported. He argued the directors ruled out Huang's return as chief executive before the facts were established, a breach of their duty of care.

It adds a well-known activist voice to the pressure on Chair Harvey Jones, who said on Tuesday's results call that the board does not intend to bring Huang back ([our results story](/news/2026-11-17/nvidia-nvda-tops-q3-estimates-and-guides-to-130-billion-in-its-first-report-without-huang/)). Huang said the same day he would be honored to return, and he can nominate two directors under Nvidia's rules ([our story](/news/2026-11-17/jensen-huang-can-nominate-two-nvidia-nvda-directors-filings-show/)). Nvidia **closed down 2.9%** at $150.96 on Wednesday, giving up an early gain on its $130 billion forecast, CNBC reported.

The board's search for a permanent chief executive, run by Heidrick & Struggles, is the next marker.

Amgen ($AMGN) reported third-quarter revenue of **$10.4 billion** after the close on Tuesday, up 8% from a year earlier and ahead of the $10.06 billion analysts expected, according to Investing.com's consensus. Adjusted earnings rose 14% to $6.45 a share, against the $5.76 expected. The shares closed down 0.9% at $461.26 before the report, slipped after hours, and were up 0.1% at $461.90 before the open on Wednesday, by 6:03 a.m. ET in our data.

## What stood out

The growth came from the newer drugs, not one blockbuster. Repatha sales grew **37%**, mostly on volume, Evenity, an osteoporosis drug, 39% to $751 million, and the asthma drug Tezspire 43% to $538 million, Amgen said in its release, as GuruFocus and Securities.io reported. Imdelltra, a lung-cancer drug, more than doubled to $406 million.

That offset the older franchises that are losing ground: sales of Prolia and Xgeva, the bone drugs now facing copies, and the psoriasis pill Otezla declined, the same reports said. Amgen's gross margin, 67.1% over the last two years in our data, is below the 72.6% median of its drugmaker peers, so mix shift toward newer, higher-priced drugs matters.

## The outlook

Amgen raised its 2026 adjusted earnings forecast to **$22.90 to $23.70** a share, from $22.30 to $23.50, and set revenue at $38.9 billion to $39.7 billion, according to its release. The midpoint of the new profit range, $23.30, sits above the $23.02 analysts had expected for the year, Securities.io reported.

On obesity, there was no new data on MariTide, its monthly weight-loss injection; Amgen said two more late-stage studies in people with type 2 diabetes start this quarter.

## Our take

A clean beat and a raise, carried by five growing drugs rather than one. But the stock is up 40.9% this year and sits 1.0% below its 52-week closing high, so the quarter mostly confirmed what was priced in, which is why the shares barely moved. The next big test is MariTide's late-stage data, which decides whether Amgen gets a seat in the obesity market that Eli Lilly ($LLY) and Novo Nordisk ($NVO) now share.

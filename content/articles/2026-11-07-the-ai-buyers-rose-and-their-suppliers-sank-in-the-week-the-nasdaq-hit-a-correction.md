On Tuesday, Democrats swept Congress and the Texas governor's race, and by Thursday investors had decided what that meant: fewer AI data centers, built later. The Nasdaq Composite **sank 5.5%** that day, its worst since April 2025, and closed the week down 3.4% in our data, its fourth weekly loss in a row, Reuters reported.

But the selling had a clear shape. The companies that sell chips, memory, storage and power into data centers lost a quarter or more of their value in five days, while Amazon ($AMZN) and Microsoft ($MSFT), which buy all of it, ended the week higher. On Friday a strong jobs report added a second worry: a Federal Reserve rate hike in December.

## The week in eight charts

### The Nasdaq fell into a correction on Thursday

The Nasdaq ended Thursday more than 10% below its 6 October record close, the definition of a correction, Reuters and CNBC reported, and the S&P 500 lost **4.4%** that day ([our market wrap](/news/2026-11-05/stock-market-today-nasdaq-sinks-5-5-into-a-correction-on-midterm-ai-fears/)). Over the week the S&P 500 fell 2.7% and the Dow 0.9%, by our count from last Friday's closes.

Lumentum ($LITE) **fell 35.9%** for the week, Seagate ($STX) 28.5% and Micron ($MU) 22.0%, and most of it came on Thursday ([our movers of the week](/news/2026-11-06/stocks-making-the-biggest-moves-this-week-applovin-lumentum-seagate-marvell-and-western-digital/)). The trigger was Texas, where the governor-elect wants to keep new data-center permits paused, in a state Wells Fargo estimates holds about 27% of planned US capacity ([our narrative](/news/2026-11-05/wall-street-is-betting-the-midterms-will-slow-the-ai-buildout-3-numbers-will-decide-it/)).

### The AI buyers rose while their suppliers sank

```chart
{"kind": "compare", "tickers": ["AMZN", "GOOGL", "MSFT", "NVDA", "MU"], "range": "1w", "end": "2026-11-06", "highlight": "AMZN",
 "title": "Amazon gained in the week its suppliers sank", "subtitle": "Change since 30 October, %"}
```

Amazon **rose 7.9%** this week, Alphabet ($GOOGL) 1.8% and Microsoft 1.7%, while Nvidia ($NVDA) fell 8.8%. A delayed data center is a sale lost or pushed back for the supplier, but money that stays in the bank, for now, for the buyer. We think that split is the clearest sign the market is pricing a slower buildout, not a broken AI business.

Health insurers dragged the Dow lower for a second day on Wednesday as UnitedHealth ($UNH) **sank 10.2%** on a federal plan to widen Medicare billing audits, while Nvidia ($NVDA) gave up its early gain to close down 2.9% despite a forecast above estimates. The Dow fell 561.25 points, or 1.1%, to 50,198.17 and the S&P 500 slipped 0.2% to 7,323.63, its fifth straight loss. The Nasdaq Composite rose 0.4% to 24,429.89 as investors waited for Anthropic's stock-market debut, Yahoo Finance reported.

## The audit rule hits insurers again

UnitedHealth closed at $349.91, its **biggest one-day drop since 27 January**, and Humana ($HUM) lost 13.0%, the S&P 500's worst performer. Elevance Health ($ELV) fell 7.6% and Centene ($CNC) 7.0%. The cause is a proposed rule from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services that would extrapolate audit errors across whole Medicare Advantage contracts back to 2018, as [our story on the slide](/news/2026-11-18/unitedhealth-humana-and-centene-shares-slide-what-happened/) set out.

UnitedHealth's high share price gives it an outsize weight in the price-weighted Dow, so its fall did more damage there than in the S&P 500. It has now lost 15.1% since Monday's close of $412.02. We weighed what the rule could cost it in [our breakdown](/news/2026-11-18/unitedhealth-unh-lost-14-in-two-days-on-an-audit-rule-is-the-business-broken/).

## Nvidia's beat was not enough

Nvidia rose before the open on its [$130 billion fourth-quarter forecast](/news/2026-11-18/nvidias-130-billion-forecast-lifts-futures-after-software-led-a-fourth-straight-loss/), about $5 billion above the LSEG consensus, then turned lower to close at $150.96, CNBC reported. It was the company's first report since Jensen Huang left as chief executive, and the board said on the call he would not return. The neocloud companies that rent out Nvidia chips fell with it: CoreWeave ($CRWV) **lost 4.1%**, CNBC and TechBuzz reported.

Security software went the other way after two days of selling. CrowdStrike ($CRWD) rose 8.0% and Zscaler ($ZS) 6.8%, and Palo Alto Networks ($PANW) jumped 9.9% ahead of its results ([our story](/news/2026-11-18/palo-alto-networks-crowdstrike-and-rubrik-shares-jump-what-happened/)). After the close Palo Alto reported quarterly revenue of $3.44 billion against the FactSet consensus of $3.31 billion and agreed to buy AI startup Applied Compute for $4.5 billion in stock, MarketScreener and Reuters reported. Its shares were **up another 9.0%** at 4:15 p.m. ET.

## Retailers rally, metals and oil slide

Results lifted the retailers. TJX Companies ($TJX) rose 8.0% after its [Marmaxx stores steadied](/news/2026-11-18/tjx-companies-tjx-q3-marmaxx-steadies-shares-jump-6-before-the-open/) and Target ($TGT) 4.0% on its [best sales growth in five years](/news/2026-11-18/target-tgt-beats-q3-estimates-as-traffic-jumps-5-and-raises-its-forecast/). Take-Two ($TTWO) added 6.2% as [GTA VI preorders](/news/2026-11-18/take-two-ttwo-shares-jump-as-gta-vi-preorders-top-1-5-billion-a-day-before-launch/) topped an estimated $1.5 billion a day before launch.

Gold **fell 3.5%** to $3,728.90, its lowest in about 14 months, after Fed Governor Christopher Waller backed a half-point hike in December and the dollar firmed, Invezz reported. Freeport-McMoRan ($FCX) lost 7.0% and Newmont ($NEM) 6.0% ([our story](/news/2026-11-18/agnico-eagle-freeport-mcmoran-and-newmont-shares-slide-what-happened/)). WTI crude dropped 3.8% to $82.74 after US crude stockpiles rose 3.4 million barrels, CNBC and Oil & Gas 360 reported.

## The Fed minutes and what's next

Minutes of the October meeting showed almost all officials expect more rate hikes, and many saw the AI buildout as an inflation risk ([our story](/news/2026-11-18/fed-minutes-show-almost-all-officials-expect-more-rate-hikes-and-flag-ai-as-an-inflation-risk/)). The two-year Treasury yield edged up to 4.80% while the 10-year fell 0.035 percentage point to 5.07%. Anthropic was set to price its offering on Wednesday evening and start trading on Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT, after marketing shares at $70 to $78, HeyGoTrade and TradingKey reported.

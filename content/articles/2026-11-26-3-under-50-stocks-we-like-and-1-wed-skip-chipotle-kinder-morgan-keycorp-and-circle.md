With the US market shut for Thanksgiving, we went back to the screen that keeps producing debt-fuelled growers: stocks under $50 a share. Third-quarter results, the last of which came from Circle on 4 November, split this batch cleanly. Chipotle ($CMG), Kinder Morgan ($KMI) and KeyCorp ($KEY) grew while their cash and returns held up; Circle ($CRCL) grew its total sales only because a one-off token presale covered an **8% fall** in its main income line.

Our screen took the stocks under $50 a share with positive free cash flow and ranked them by revenue growth in the 12 months to September 2026, from SEC filings and Wednesday's closes. We set aside the leader, Keurig Dr Pepper, whose 43% growth is the JDE Peet's purchase and which we put on our skip list on 8 November. We judged the rest on long-run growth, margins, cash, returns and debt against their own industries.

```picks
like: CMG, KMI, KEY
skip: CRCL
```

## 3 stocks we like

### 1. Chipotle Mexican Grill ($CMG)

**Revenue growth: 7.9% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Chipotle runs fast-casual burrito restaurants and plans to open 350 to 370 more this year, Nation's Restaurant News reported.

1. **Profit per share has compounded.** Earnings per share grew **35.4% a year** over five fiscal years, in the top quarter of 35 consumer stocks we cover, against a median of 13.6%. Wider margins and a 7.8% cut in the share count over two years did much of it.
2. **The customers came back.** Transactions rose 0.8% in the September quarter, the third straight quarter of more visits after comparable sales fell 1.7% in 2025, the release and Nation's Restaurant News reported. Same-store sales rose 2.1%, and management guided to about 2% for the fourth quarter, Zacks reported.
3. **It's still a cash business.** Free cash flow was 12.6% of revenue over two years, above the 9.4% median for its group, and return on equity was 56.1%, in the top quarter.

**The price:** 35.0 times trailing earnings, right on its own two-year median of 34.9, and 29.5 times forward earnings, above the restaurant peers McDonald's (21.3) and Yum (26.0).

**What could go wrong:** costs. Operating margin fell to 13.7% from 15.9% in the quarter on beef, freight and bigger portions, and EPS of $0.27 missed the $0.29 analysts expected. Another quarter like it would test the earnings record.

### 2. Kinder Morgan ($KMI)

**Revenue growth: 11.7% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Kinder Morgan runs natural gas pipelines and storage, and charges fees to move and store the gas.

1. **The cash is steady.** Free cash flow was **16.8% of revenue** over two years, in the top quarter of 25 energy stocks we cover, against a 9.3% median, because its fees don't move with the gas price.
2. **The backlog grew by half in a quarter.** The project backlog rose to **$14.4 billion** at the end of September from $9.7 billion in June, with a record $13.2 billion in gas projects, the release and Oilprice.com reported. Adjusted EBITDA rose 9% in the quarter.
3. **The margin is wide.** Operating margin was 29.0% over the last 12 months, against a 22.5% median for energy stocks.

**The price:** 19.6 times trailing earnings, below its own two-year median of 22.4.

**What could go wrong:** debt and one-offs. Net debt is $30.5 billion, 5.7 years of operating profit, and return on capital is 6.8%, in the bottom quarter. Management warned that weather, a contract buyout and wide gas price spreads helped 2026 and may not repeat, Beancount's review of the call reported.

### 3. KeyCorp ($KEY)

**Revenue growth: 25.1% in the 12 months to September 2026.** KeyCorp is a Cleveland-based regional bank that lends to businesses and households.

1. **Most of the jump is a 2024 base effect, and that is the point.** In late 2024 KeyCorp sold about $3.0 billion of bonds yielding 1.5% at a **$915 million loss** and reinvested at 5.5%, its 10-K says. That loss depresses the starting year, and the higher yield is now showing up: net interest income rose 9.8% in the September quarter and the margin widened to 2.95%, the release said.
2. **Record revenue, rising returns.** Third-quarter revenue was a record $2.04 billion, up 8.4%, and EPS of $0.51 beat the $0.46 Zacks consensus. Return on equity is 11.9%, above the 10.9% median of 15 banks we cover.
**The price:** 1.2 times book value and 9.7 times forward earnings, against a bank median of 10.1.

**What could go wrong:** the long run. EPS grew 3.7% a year over five years, in the bottom quarter of its peers, and the share count rose 12.1% in two years after Scotiabank bought a 14.9% stake in 2024. Growth now has to come from loans.

## 1 stock we'd skip

### Circle Internet Group ($CRCL)

**Revenue growth: 25.7% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Circle issues USDC, a dollar-backed digital token, and earns interest on the reserves behind it.

1. **Its main income is falling.** Reserve income fell **8% to $658 million** in the September quarter as the yield on its reserves dropped 0.61 points to 3.5%, the release said. Total revenue grew 17% only because of a presale of its ARC token, CoinDesk and The Block reported.
2. **Rate cuts hit it directly.** Average USDC in circulation grew 8%, too slowly to offset lower rates, and its share of the stablecoin market fell to 27%, The Block reported.
3. **Thin margins, many more shares.** Operating margin was 11.3% over the last 12 months against a 26.8% median for 46 payments and finance peers, and diluted shares rose 266% in two years around its 2025 listing.

**The price:** 47.9 times forward earnings against a peer median of 15.9, even after a 63.7% fall from its March high.

**What would change our mind:** reserve income growing again in the December quarter, which needs USDC circulation to grow faster than rates fall.

On Saturday the Financial Times reported that a US government memo describes Nvidia ($NVDA) Chief Executive Jensen Huang as having had a romantic relationship with Alice Huang, a Megaspeed executive whom US intelligence believes was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security, according to accounts of the report by Investing.com and Seeking Alpha. The memo, which has not been published, circulated shortly before President Trump's May summit in Beijing, the FT reported.

It raises the stakes of a briefing already on the calendar. In a letter dated 4 November, House committee chairs Brian Mast, John Moolenaar, and Rick Crawford asked Jensen Huang to brief them in private on **18 November** about his dealings with Alice Huang and Nvidia's protections against espionage, and to accept or offer another date by 11 November. The chairs did not say he knowingly shared secrets. Nvidia declined to comment on the memo and pointed to its earlier statement that Huang talks with "thousands of customer executives every year." We covered the first reports [here](/news/2026-10-31/singapore-disputes-part-of-wsj-report-on-chinese-executive-close-to-nvidia-nvda-ceo/).

The stock trades on Monday for the first time since the report. Nvidia reports results on Tuesday 17 November.

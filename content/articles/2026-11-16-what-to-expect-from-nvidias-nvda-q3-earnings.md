Nvidia ($NVDA) reports its fiscal third quarter, the three months to 25 October, after the close on Tuesday, 17 November, with a call at 5 p.m. ET, the company said. It is the first report since Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive on 12 November, so interim Chief Executive Colette Kress, who stays on as finance chief, presents, Invezz and Fortune reported. The stock goes in at **$156.14**, its lowest close in a year.

Last quarter, revenue rose 106% to $96.2 billion, and Kress said revenue should grow about 70% in fiscal 2028, against the 44% analysts had expected, CNBC and Fortune reported. The stock **jumped 8.7%** the next day and peaked at $239.24 on 6 October. Since then it has lost 34.7%, in our data.

## What analysts expect

Analysts polled by LSEG expect revenue of about **$109.2 billion**, Invezz reported. The Zacks consensus is $2.49 a share on an adjusted basis, Alphabeta Stock reported, matching the figure in Nasdaq's data. That compares with revenue of $57.0 billion a year earlier, in Nvidia's filings, so the consensus implies growth of about 92%. Nvidia guided to $108 billion, plus or minus 2%, and a gross margin of about 74.0%, its August release showed, so analysts sit just above the middle of its range.

The debate this report settles: **did customers move orders to AMD ($AMD) and Broadcom ($AVGO) while Nvidia's leadership was in doubt?** Both rivals jumped the day Huang quit ([our story](/news/2026-11-13/broadcom-avgo-and-amd-shares-jump-after-nvidias-ceo-resigns/)), and the quarter itself closed before he did. Only the forecast can show it.

## What to watch

- **The fourth-quarter forecast.** A revenue guide that keeps rising from $108 billion would say demand held through the leadership crisis; a flat one would back [last week's bet](/news/2026-11-13/wall-street-is-betting-nvidias-loss-is-amd-and-broadcoms-gain-tuesday-will-test-it/) that rivals gained.
- **The 70% fiscal 2028 forecast.** Kress repeating or raising it answers the SemiAnalysis cut to 2028 AI-chip spending that hit chip stocks on Monday ([our debate story](/news/2026-11-16/bulls-vs-bears-on-ai-spending-semianalysis-cut-28-morgan-stanley-says-the-cycle-is-intact/)).
- **Cash.** Free cash flow fell to $21.4 billion last quarter from $48.6 billion the quarter before while sales rose, in Nvidia's filings; another fall would feed the charge that it is financing its own customers ([our narrative](/news/2026-11-16/wall-street-is-betting-ais-1-trillion-money-loop-breaks-at-the-neoclouds-nvidias-report-will-test-it/)).

## How the stock has done

Nvidia **fell 4.1%** on Monday and is down 27.1% over the past month and 16.3% this year, 34.7% below its closing high of $239.24 from 6 October. At 12.9 times forward earnings it trades at about three-fifths of the 21.7 times median for the 25 chipmakers we track ([our breakdown](/news/2026-11-06/nvidia-nvda-is-down-22-from-its-high-is-the-business-broken/)). $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $5,170 today.

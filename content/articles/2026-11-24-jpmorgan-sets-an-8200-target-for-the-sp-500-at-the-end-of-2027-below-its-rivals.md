J.P. Morgan's strategists, led by Dubravko Lakos-Bujas, forecast on Tuesday that the S&P 500 will end 2027 at **8,200**, Bloomberg and Reuters reported. That is about 14% above Tuesday's close of 7,201.09, but below the 8,471 average of strategists' forecasts compiled by Bloomberg and the 8,700 set by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley.

The call comes as the S&P 500 has fallen on eight of the last nine days, the Associated Press reported, as AI stocks swung on Nvidia's leadership crisis ([our wrap](/news/2026-11-24/stock-market-today-chip-stocks-give-back-mondays-rally-while-the-dow-closes-above-50000/)). The strategists argued that the recent unwinding of momentum trades had improved the near-term odds for stocks, according to Reuters. In August the same team had set an 8,000 target for the end of 2026, CNBC reported, a level the index now sits 10% below.

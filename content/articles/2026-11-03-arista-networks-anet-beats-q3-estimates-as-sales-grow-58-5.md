Arista Networks ($ANET) reported third-quarter revenue of **$3.66 billion** after the close on Tuesday, up 58.5% from a year earlier and ahead of the $3.31 billion analysts expected, according to Zacks data cited by the Associated Press. Adjusted EPS of $1.20 beat the Zacks consensus of $1.08, and the shares were up 9.5% at $186.00 by 7:59 p.m. ET, after closing up 3.5% at $169.91.

## What stood out

The beat was big against Arista's own forecast, too: it had guided to about **$3.3 billion** for the quarter, the release said. Arista sells the Ethernet switches that connect AI servers inside data centers, and its product revenue rose 67% to $3.19 billion. "Arista registered another momentous quarter with the highest year-over-year revenue growth since our IPO and record profitability, surpassing even my high expectations," Chief Executive Jayshree Ullal said in the release.

Growth this fast usually costs margin, and it didn't here. The adjusted operating margin edged up to 49.1%, against a median of 16.9% for its peers in our data.

## The outlook

Arista expects fourth-quarter revenue of **$3.9 billion to $4.0 billion**, up 57% to 61% from a year earlier, against about $3.6 billion analysts had expected, Benzinga and Investing.com reported. On the call, management gave a first look at 2027: revenue growth of 38% to 40%, or about $18.5 billion at the midpoint, with AI networking sales expected to roughly double to about $7 billion, according to call summaries from TradingKey and SimpleTechTrend.

## Our take

This is the cleanest AI-hardware quarter of the season so far: a beat of about 11% on both lines, a Q4 guide above estimates and a 2027 number that keeps growth near 40%. The bar is high, though: at 36.6 times forward earnings, the stock is priced at nearly twice the peer median, so the 2027 guide is now the number it has to hit. It reports again early next year; our [preview](/news/2026-11-02/what-to-expect-from-arista-networks-anet-q3-earnings/) set out what was expected going in.

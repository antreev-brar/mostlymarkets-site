## What happened
Shares of database and cloud company Oracle ($ORCL) **jumped 16.7%** to $118.09 in the first minutes of trading on Monday, as of 9:34 a.m. ET. Two things lifted it: Oracle's statement after Friday's close that its OpenAI data-center contract is fully funded, reported by Reuters and Seeking Alpha, and the rally in AI stocks after Nvidia ($NVDA) reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive, reported by Bloomberg and CNBC ([our Nvidia story](/news/2026-11-23/nvidia-nvda-shares-soar-29-before-the-open-after-it-reinstates-jensen-huang/)).

Oracle said the build for OpenAI was paid for after a final capital raise this month ([our brief](/news/2026-11-20/oracle-orcl-says-its-openai-data-center-deal-is-fully-funded-after-an-8-drop/)). The shares were up more than 4% before the open on that statement alone, GuruFocus and Seeking Alpha reported, then rose further when the wider AI rebound began.

## Why it matters
The worry behind Oracle's slide was money: it is paying for its AI build with borrowed money and new shares, and it raised another $20 billion in stock and bonds on 2 November. The statement answers the question of whether the OpenAI build can be finished without more of that.

It doesn't answer the other worry. About half of Oracle's $664 billion order backlog is tied to OpenAI, by S&P's estimate, and a funded build is only as good as the customer that rents it ([our breakdown](/news/2026-11-21/oracle-orcl-has-a-664-billion-backlog-and-no-free-cash-is-the-business-broken/)).

## What the market is telling us
Oracle's shares moved 5% or more on 42 of the last 252 sessions; Friday's 8.5% fall was its biggest drop since an 8.8% fall on 2 November. It closed Friday down 48.1% this year, at $101.19, 59.2% below its 1 June high of $248.15. Even after Monday's jump, the stock is worth less than half its June price, which tells us the market has priced a financing problem that Oracle now says it has solved; its fiscal second-quarter results, expected in mid-December, will show whether the cash burn is shrinking.

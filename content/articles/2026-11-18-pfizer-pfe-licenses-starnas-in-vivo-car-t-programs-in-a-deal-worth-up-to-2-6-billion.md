Pfizer ($PFE) agreed on Wednesday to license three in vivo CAR-T programs for autoimmune diseases from China's Starna Therapeutics, paying **$330 million upfront**, with up to $2.3 billion more in development and sales milestones plus royalties, Reuters and Investing.com reported. Pfizer gets the rights outside Greater China, with an option to add that region.

CAR-T therapy reprograms a patient's immune cells to attack a target; the in vivo kind does it inside the body with an injected treatment, skipping the costly step of removing and engineering cells in a lab. It is Pfizer's first move into the field, Endpoints News and Contract Pharma reported. The upfront payment is small against Pfizer's market value of about $162 billion.

None of the three programs has been cleared to start US clinical trials yet. Pfizer said it will lead development outside Greater China and make the first trial supplies at its US plants. The shares were down 0.7% before the open, as of 9:15 a.m. ET, in our data.

Newsmax sued Fox () in federal court in California on Wednesday to block or unwind its **$22 billion** takeover of Roku, which closed on 6 November, Reuters and Bloomberg reported. Newsmax says the combined company holds more than 70% of free, ad-supported streaming TV, and asks the court to halt the integration unless Fox sells either Tubi or The Roku Channel.

Newsmax argues that Fox's promises to regulators, including keeping other companies' programming on Roku for three years, can't be enforced because they were never put in a consent decree, Reuters and The Daily Beast reported. Fox called the complaint "wholly without merit" and said the deal passed a rigorous regulatory review, TheWrap reported. A forced sale of Tubi would take apart the streaming bet behind the deal ([our story on Roku's last quarter](/news/2026-11-02/roku-roku-beats-q3-estimates-but-the-fox-deal-sets-its-price/)).

Fox shares closed down 1.6% at $65.63. An earlier Newsmax antitrust suit against Fox's cable-news business was dismissed by a Florida judge, TheWrap reported. The suit was filed on Wednesday, so no judge has yet ruled on the request to halt the integration.

Tesla ($TSLA) has applied to Germany's Federal Cartel Office to buy assets of EMAG, a maker of factory machines, according to a filing dated **23 November** that Reuters reported on Friday. The filing describes an asset purchase, not a takeover of the whole company, and gives no price or timing, Reuters and TeslAnt reported.

EMAG builds machines for precision metal parts, electrochemical machining and laser processing, the filing shows. Its machines also cut the strain-wave and planetary gears used in robot joints, TipRanks and FourWeekMBA reported, which ties the deal to Optimus, the humanoid robot Tesla has made central to its pitch to investors. Tesla's shares rose **1.9%** to $387.35 in premarket trading as of 6:30 a.m. ET, in line with the wider rebound in tech stocks.

What isn't known yet is the price, which assets are included and whether Tesla will comment. The next step is the cartel office's decision on the filing.

On Tuesday, Bank of America's November survey showed fund managers made their biggest cut to tech holdings since 2006, Bloomberg and CNBC reported. Over the past six months the market's strongest large stocks have not been chipmakers or software companies: **five of the 12** best performers on our screen make laboratory and life-science tools.

We ranked the large US stocks in our universe on their price return over the six months to Tuesday's close, from our price files, leaving out stocks we listed in the last 14 days. Then we asked whether each rise rests on a business that compounds: five-year growth in sales and earnings per share, margins, cash and debt, from SEC filings in our fundamentals data, judged against each company's industry. We passed over Bio-Techne, up 59.7%, whose price now tracks Merck KGaA's pending takeover, Seeking Alpha and Zacks reported.

## 3 stocks we like

### 1. Regeneron ($REGN)
**Six-month return: 52.0% to 17 November 2026.** Regeneron develops antibody drugs, including the eye treatment Eylea, and shares the profits of the eczema drug Dupixent with Sanofi.

1. **The latest quarter beat by a wide margin.** Revenue rose **36%** to about $5.1 billion in the third quarter, as Regeneron's share of Sanofi collaboration profits jumped 68% to $2.72 billion, its release, Zacks and Benzinga reported. Adjusted EPS of $20.07 beat the $16.08 Zacks consensus.
2. **It grows faster than its peers.** Sales rose 10.5% a year over the last two years, in the top quarter of the 18 drugmakers we cover, whose median is 4.6%.
3. **The Eylea switch is working.** US sales of the newer Eylea HD rose 62% to $699 million as patients moved from the original, whose sales fell 41% to $400 million, the release showed.

**The price:** 12.4 times expected earnings, against a 17.8 times median for its drug peers, though 21.1 times trailing earnings is near the top of its 12.3 to 21.5 range since October 2024.

**What could go wrong:** Combined US Eylea sales still fell 1% in the quarter, and its operating margin is 17.1 points lower than five years ago, in the bottom quarter of its peers. A drop in Eylea HD growth would leave it leaning on Dupixent alone.

### 2. Agilent Technologies ($A)
**Six-month return: 55.8% to 17 November 2026.** Agilent makes instruments, consumables and services for drug, diagnostic and chemical labs.

1. **Profit compounds faster than sales.** Earnings per share grew **14.7% a year** over five years, against an 8.8% median for the 65 health-care stocks we cover, as its operating margin rose 5.4 points to 22.2%.
2. **Growth is picking up.** Core revenue grew 7.3% in the quarter to July, above the top of its forecast, and Agilent raised its fiscal-year sales outlook to $7.49 billion to $7.51 billion, its release and Barchart reported. About 1.1 points of its 28.3% adjusted margin came from tariff refunds, Pulse 2 reported.
3. **Each dollar invested earns more.** Return on capital of 13.6% is in the top quarter of its peers, whose median is 8.5%, with net debt of just 1.3 years of operating profit.

**The price:** 33.9 times trailing earnings, above its 29.1 median since August 2024, and 26.2 times expected earnings against a 19.4 peer median.

**What could go wrong:** It reports after the close on Monday, 23 November, and guided to fourth-quarter revenue of $1.98 billion to $2.0 billion without any tariff refunds. A weak 2027 outlook from a stock 5.1% below its high would hit the premium first.

### 3. Allegion ($ALLE)
**Six-month return: 48.0% to 17 November 2026.** Allegion makes locks, door hardware and electronic access systems.

1. **Steady compounding.** Earnings per share grew **17.0% a year** over five years, against an 11.3% median for the 89 industrials we cover, and return on capital of 19.5% beats their 13.4% median.
2. **It beat and raised in October.** Third-quarter organic revenue rose 7.2% and adjusted EPS 13.0% to $2.60, against the $2.49 Zacks consensus, and Allegion lifted its 2026 adjusted EPS forecast to $9.05 to $9.10, its release, Zacks and Quiver Quant reported.
3. **More profitable every year.** Its operating margin of 21.5% is 6.3 points higher than five years ago, while it turned 17.0% of sales into free cash flow over two years, above the 12.7% industrial median.

**The price:** 23.3 times trailing earnings, near the top of its 17.2 to 24.9 range since October 2024, but 19.4 times expected earnings, below the 20.9 peer median.

**What could go wrong:** International sales shrank 0.2% organically, and the quarter included $19.9 million of tariff refunds, the release said. A slowdown in non-residential building would hit its Americas business, which grew 9.1%.

## 1 stock we'd skip

### Thermo Fisher Scientific ($TMO)
**Six-month return: 53.4% to 17 November 2026.** Thermo Fisher makes lab equipment and supplies, and runs drug-testing and manufacturing services.

1. **The rise is ahead of the earnings.** Earnings per share grew only **2.1% a year** over five years, in the bottom quarter of the 65 health-care stocks we cover, and its operating margin is 6.8 points lower than five years ago.
2. **The quarter was good, the year is slower.** Third-quarter revenue rose 9% to $12.14 billion with 6% organic growth, but the company raised its full-year organic growth forecast only to 4%, its release and BioSpectrum reported.
3. **It carries the most debt of the four.** Net debt of $35.6 billion is 4.2 years of operating profit, and return on capital of 7.4% trails the 8.5% median of its peers.

**The price:** 36.2 times trailing earnings, near the top of its 22.9 to 38.1 range since November 2024, and 26.2 times expected earnings against a 19.4 peer median.

**What would change our mind:** A 2027 forecast in late January with organic growth back above 6%.

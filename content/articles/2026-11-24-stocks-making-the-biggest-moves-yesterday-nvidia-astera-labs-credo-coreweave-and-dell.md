**Nvidia ($NVDA)**, +31.5%: the AI-chip leader closed at $165.05 on Monday, its biggest move of the past year by our data and its best day since 2003, after its board voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as chief executive 11 days after he resigned. The gain added about $952 billion to its market value, the biggest one-day dollar gain any company has had, according to Seeking Alpha and 24/7 Wall St., though the stock still sits 31.0% below its 6 October closing high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-23/nvidia-nvda-shares-soar-29-before-the-open-after-it-reinstates-jensen-huang/)

**Astera Labs ($ALAB)**, +23.2%: the maker of chips that connect AI processors inside servers closed at $222.12, its biggest gain of the past year and its biggest since August 2025, with no news of its own; Bloomberg, CNN and Reuters tied the chip rebound to Huang's return. Even after Monday, it is 54.0% below its 30 June high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-23/tsmc-intel-and-marvell-shares-jump-what-happened/)

**Credo Technology ($CRDO)**, +22.6%: the connectivity-chip maker, which sells the cables and links that wire AI servers together, closed at $122.46, also its biggest gain of the past year, with no company news. It remains 59.5% below its June high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-23/tsmc-intel-and-marvell-shares-jump-what-happened/)

**CoreWeave ($CRWV)**, +22.3%: the AI cloud company, which rents out Nvidia chips, closed at $48.33 a session after it ended at a 52-week low, and rose with Nebius Group ($NBIS), up 20.3%, as the AI sell-off reversed. CoreWeave is still down 32.5% this year and has more riding on Nvidia's financing questions than most, as we laid out in [why Nvidia's crisis has moved from its boss to its books](/news/2026-11-23/nvidias-crisis-has-moved-from-its-boss-to-its-books-what-the-evidence-says/).

**Dell Technologies ($DELL)**, +20.5%: the AI-server maker closed at $334.91 the day before its third-quarter results, with no news of its own, as the builders of Nvidia-based servers rallied together. It reports after the close today; rival Hewlett Packard Enterprise ($HPE), which rose 17.4% on Monday, is [sliding before the open](/news/2026-11-24/hpe-hpe-slides-11-5-before-the-open-as-it-sees-ai-server-margins-falling-to-14/) after forecasting thinner AI-server margins. [Full story](/news/2026-11-23/why-dell-technologies-dell-stock-is-soaring-today/)

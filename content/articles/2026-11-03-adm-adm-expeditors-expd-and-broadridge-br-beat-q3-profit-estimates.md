Three more companies reported before the open on Tuesday, and all three beat on profit. Archer-Daniels-Midland ($ADM) earned an adjusted **$1.77 a share**, nearly double a year earlier, and raised its 2026 forecast, but its shares fell 2.4% before the open after sales missed. Expeditors International ($EXPD) grew EPS 62% on a freight surge, and Broadridge Financial ($BR) beat on adjusted EPS and kept its year's targets.

## ADM ($ADM): biofuel margins came back

The crop processor's adjusted EPS of $1.77 was up from $0.92 a year earlier and above the $1.60 S&P Global consensus cited by StockStory, while GAAP EPS was $1.70. Segment operating profit rose to **$1.4 billion** from $845 million, and the release credited stronger biofuel and ethanol margins, demand for soybean meal and policy support for biofuels. Revenue of $22.34 billion missed the $22.51 billion forecast, StockStory reported.

ADM raised its 2026 adjusted EPS forecast to about $5.50 to $5.80, from $5.15 to $5.60, and said it expects operating profit to improve on a year earlier in every segment in the fourth quarter, Seeking Alpha and GuruFocus reported. The new floor sits above the $5.43 consensus GuruFocus cited. No outlet gave a cause for the pre-market drop to $73.96 as of 9:29 a.m. ET; the sales miss was the only headline number below forecasts.

## Expeditors ($EXPD): freight volumes and prices both jumped

The freight forwarder's diluted EPS **rose 62% to $2.65**, against the $2.24 Zacks consensus reported by the AP, on revenue up 42% to $4.1 billion, the company said. Net earnings rose 55% to $345 million and airfreight tonnage grew 13%, with a lower tax rate and share count adding to the gain. Revenue beat the $3.62 billion consensus StockStory cited by 13%. Expeditors holds an investor day on 18 November. The shares were down 0.3% at $200.00 in early pre-market trading, after closing Monday at a 52-week high.

## Broadridge ($BR): recurring revenue grew 11%

The financial-communications and trading-software company's adjusted EPS **rose 3% to $1.56** in its fiscal first quarter, the three months to September, above the roughly $1.41 consensus Chartmill cited. Total revenue of $1.67 billion rose 5%, and recurring revenue, the fee income from its long-term contracts, grew 11% to $1.08 billion, the release said. GAAP EPS fell 41% to $0.83, and the adjusted operating margin narrowed to 13.8% from 15.8%. Broadridge kept its fiscal 2027 targets of 6% to 8% recurring revenue growth and 8% to 12% adjusted EPS growth.

## What they say together

All three beat on profit, but only one big number moved the price: ADM's sales miss. The sharper read is in Expeditors' 13% rise in airfreight tonnage: air is the costliest way to ship, so the rise says customers were paying up for speed. Expeditors' investor day on 18 November is the next place to test whether that lasts.

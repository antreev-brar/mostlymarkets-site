Bristol Myers Squibb ($BMY) said at about 7 a.m. ET on Wednesday that its psoriasis pill Sotyktu **met its main goal in one of two** late-stage trials in systemic lupus, the company's release and The Fly reported. In POETYK SLE-1, which enrolled 510 adults, more patients on Sotyktu reached a standard lupus response measure (SRI-4) after 52 weeks than on placebo. In the 513-patient SLE-2, the response on Sotyktu was similar, but more placebo patients responded, so it missed.

A mid-stage trial had shown significantly higher response rates than placebo, BioPharma Dive reported, so a split Phase 3 result makes the case to regulators harder to argue. Bristol said safety matched what it has seen in psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis, and the stock was barely moved by 7:27 a.m. ET.

The company gave no effect sizes or filing date. It said it will review the full Phase 2 and Phase 3 data and talk to health authorities, so the detailed numbers are the next thing to watch.

Arista Networks ($ANET), which makes the network switches that link AI servers inside data centers, reports third-quarter results after the close on Tuesday, 3 November. It reports after its shares **fell 20.8% in a month**, including an 8.3% drop on 30 October during a sell-off in AI-infrastructure stocks.

Last quarter, Arista's revenue rose 37.7% to $3.04 billion, ahead of the roughly $2.83 billion analysts expected, and adjusted EPS of $1.02 beat the $0.89 forecast, Newsquawk and Seeking Alpha reported. It also guided third-quarter revenue to about $3.3 billion, well above the $2.94 billion the Street had then. The shares closed up 3.6% the next day, 5 August, and went on to a 52-week closing high of $216.74 on 9 October.

## What analysts expect

Analysts expect revenue of about **$3.33 billion**, up 44% year on year, and adjusted EPS of $1.08, according to MarketBeat's survey, with Seeking Alpha's preview citing the same figures. That sits at the top of Arista's own guide of $1.06 to $1.08 a share, so a beat alone has been assumed: Seeking Alpha counts eight straight quarters of beats on both lines.

## What to watch

- **The fourth-quarter guide.** In August the guide, not the beat, moved the stock; a December-quarter forecast well above consensus is what would answer the October sell-off.
- **The operating margin.** Arista guided a 48% to 49% adjusted operating margin; slippage would suggest it is paying up for components or discounting to its biggest cloud buyers.
- **What its cloud customers are building.** After Amazon's spending worries hit the group on 30 October, as Finviz reported, any comment on 2027 orders from the largest AI customers matters more than the quarter.

## How the stock has done

The stock is up 25.3% this year, but at $164.14 it sits 24.3% below its October high, and it moved 5% or more on 33 of the last 252 sessions.

French drugmaker Sanofi agreed on Tuesday to buy Insmed for **$175 a share** in cash, valuing the US biotech at about $41.0 billion, the companies said, as CNBC and the Wall Street Journal reported. The price is about 17.5% above Insmed's Monday close and about 46% above its 18 November close, the day before Bloomberg reported Sanofi was weighing a bid, Fierce Pharma and RTE reported. Insmed rose about 14% before the open, CNBC said.

Sanofi is buying Brinsupri, a treatment for the chronic lung disease bronchiectasis that launched in the US last year, and Arikayce, for a lung infection, as it looks for growth beyond its eczema drug Dupixent, Fierce Pharma reported. We read it as a sign that drugmakers facing patent cliffs are paying up for drugs already on the market rather than early-stage science.

The deal needs Insmed shareholder and regulatory approval and is expected to close in the first half of 2027, and Sanofi expects it to add to earnings per share by the end of 2028.

## What happened
Shares of Chinese e-commerce and cloud company Alibaba ($BABA) **sank 7.1%** on Thursday on fears that Washington will cut its access to advanced AI chips, after the scandal that ended Jensen Huang's run as Nvidia's ($NVDA) CEO put Alibaba's chip supplier in the spotlight, Bloomberg and Proactive Investors reported.

Alibaba rents Nvidia chips through Megaspeed International, a Singapore company whose former executive had a relationship with Huang and is reportedly suspected by US intelligence of working for China, Bloomberg reported. On the same day, The Information reported that the Commerce Department is finalizing a rule to block Chinese companies from reaching advanced AI chips remotely, through data centers abroad, a report 24/7 Wall St. and Yahoo Finance carried. The shares closed at $94.36, down 7.1% from the previous close, on 2.7 times their usual volume, in our data.

## Why it matters
Renting chips in other countries has been how Chinese cloud companies get Nvidia's best hardware without buying it, since US export rules stop direct sales. A rule closing that route would limit the computing power Alibaba can sell through its AI cloud. Baidu ($BIDU) fell 2.7% on the day, in our data.

Washington is now looking at the sales themselves: Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick has agreed to testify to the Senate Banking Committee about chip exports to China, CNBC and Euronews reported, with no date set. The rule itself has not been published, so its reach is not known yet.

## What the market is telling us
Alibaba's shares moved 5% or more on 14 of the last 252 sessions. The last move at least this big in the same direction was on 21 August 2026, when the stock fell 8.6%. The stock is down 35.6% this year, and at $94.36 it sits 46.7% below its 52-week closing high of $177.18 from 22 January 2026. $1,000 invested in Alibaba five years ago would be worth $566 today.

The stock had already lost 15.1% in the past month, so the market is pricing a real chance the remote route closes. Before Friday's open it was down another 1.7%, at $92.71.

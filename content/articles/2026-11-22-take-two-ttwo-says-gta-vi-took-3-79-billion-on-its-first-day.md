Take-Two Interactive ($TTWO) said on Friday that Grand Theft Auto VI took an estimated **$3.79 billion** in worldwide retail sales on its first day, 19 November, and sold 32.5 million copies before release, CNN and PC Gamer reported. The company called it the biggest entertainment launch in any medium. GTA V took about $800 million on its first day in 2013.

The figure is what shoppers spent, not what Take-Two books: stores and console makers such as Sony ($SONY) and Microsoft ($MSFT) take a cut, The Escapist and Make Tech Easier noted. It is more than twice the roughly $1.5 billion of preorders Sensor Tower estimated before launch, which lifted the stock on Wednesday ([our story](/news/2026-11-18/take-two-ttwo-shares-jump-as-gta-vi-preorders-top-1-5-billion-a-day-before-launch/)). The shares **rose 0.6%** on Friday as the S&P 500 fell 3.7%, but they remain 18.0% lower this year in our data, so investors are still waiting to see how much of the sales Take-Two keeps.

The first answer comes with Take-Two's next quarterly report, the first to include the launch.

Block ($XYZ), the company behind Square's payment terminals and Cash App, reports third-quarter results after the close on Tuesday, 3 November. In August it guided **gross profit of $3.13 billion**, up 18% year on year, the number that matters most to Block's investors because it strips out the pass-through cost of bitcoin it sells.

Last quarter, reported on 5 August, gross profit rose 25% to $3.17 billion and Cash App's grew 31% to $1.97 billion, the shareholder letter and Reuters show. Block raised its 2026 gross profit forecast to $12.51 billion from $12.33 billion. But bitcoin gross profit fell 31% after Cash App cut some fees, The Block reported, and the shares closed down 6.2% the next day.

## What analysts expect

Analysts expect adjusted EPS of about **$1.03** on revenue of $6.6 billion, according to the Zacks consensus cited by Yahoo Finance; Block's own guide was $1.02 of adjusted EPS and $875 million of adjusted operating income, a 28% margin on gross profit. Our calendar's $0.68 consensus is on a different basis and isn't comparable.

## What to watch

- **Cash App gross profit growth.** It was 31% last quarter; holding near that pace is what keeps the full-year guide credible.
- **Bitcoin.** A second quarter of falling bitcoin gross profit would show the fee cuts are costing more than they bring in.
- **The 2026 range.** A raise above $12.51 billion of gross profit, or a first look at 2027, is what would move a stock that has been range-bound since August.

## How the stock has done

Block is up 18.4% this year and at $77.08 sits 9.2% below its 27 August closing high. It moved 5% or more on 25 of the last 252 sessions, and $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $310 today.

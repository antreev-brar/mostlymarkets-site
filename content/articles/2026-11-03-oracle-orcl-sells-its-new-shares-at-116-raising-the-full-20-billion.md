Oracle ($ORCL) said late on Monday that it had priced **$10 billion** of new common stock at $116 a share, alongside $10 billion of senior notes, Reuters and RTTNews reported, citing its pricing release. The share price is 2.7% below Monday's close of $119.22 and 11.2% below Friday's, by our count.

It completes the $20 billion raise Oracle filed for on Monday morning, which sent the stock **down 8.8%** that day ([our story](/news/2026-11-02/oracle-orcl-shares-slide-after-it-files-to-raise-20-billion-in-stock-and-bonds/)). At its investor day on 28 October, Oracle said $20 billion was the most it would raise from bond and stock markets through fiscal 2030, so it has now used its whole allowance in one week, FourWeekMBA noted. New shares sold at a discount dilute existing holders, and the money pays for data centers Oracle is building for AI customers.

By 6:15 a.m. ET the stock was down 1.2% at $117.76 before the open, close to the 52-week closing low of $114.99 it hit on 24 July. The next test is whether Meta ($META), which is also [tapping the bond market](/news/2026-11-02/meta-meta-plans-a-bond-sale-of-at-least-20-billion-bloomberg-reports/), has to pay more than Oracle did.

## What happened
Shares of AI chipmaker Nvidia ($NVDA) **soared 29.1% to $162.00** before the open on Monday, as of 9:15 a.m. ET, after its board reinstated Jensen Huang as president, chief executive and a director, effective immediately. The board voted unanimously, Nvidia said in a release on Monday morning, 11 days after Huang resigned on 12 November.

The board said it acted after "a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang" and with input from "senior levels of the United States government," CNN and Reuters reported. Colette Kress, the interim chief executive, goes back to being chief financial officer, and Harvey Jones stays as chair. The board's own investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort will run to its conclusion, the company said.

It is a reversal in a week. On 16 November a person familiar with the matter told CNBC the board was not considering bringing Huang back. President Donald Trump urged a rehire overnight ([our brief](/news/2026-11-23/trump-urges-nvidia-nvda-to-rehire-jensen-huang-and-the-shares-rise-8-before-the-open/)), and after the decision wrote on Truth Social that the board had made "the correct and proper decision," Reuters reported.

## Why it matters
The leadership question was one of two weights on the stock; the other, scrutiny of Nvidia's China sales and its books, is still open ([our analysis](/news/2026-11-23/nvidias-crisis-has-moved-from-its-boss-to-its-books-what-the-evidence-says/)). California's attorney general says his probe covers possible criminal violations, and Huang faces a closed-door House interview on 1 December. Huang's return removes the search for a successor; it doesn't close any of those inquiries.

## What the market is telling us
Nvidia closed Friday at $125.53 after a **14.0% fall**, its biggest one-day move in a year, and 47.5% below its 6 October record of $239.24. It has moved 5% or more on 10 days in the past year and is down 32.7% this year. Even at $162, the stock would sit about a third below its high, so the bounce undoes the panic over Huang's exit, not the worries about the investigations ([our Daily](/daily/2026-11-23/)).

Amazon ($AMZN) said on Monday it has signed an agreement to buy the technology behind Decart's Optimization Stack, the Israeli startup's software for speeding up AI models, and to hire its three co-founders and research team. Amazon gave no price; Bloomberg reported the deal is worth about **$7.5 billion**, Investing.com and Seeking Alpha said, confirming the talks [first reported on Saturday](/news/2026-11-21/amazon-amzn-nears-a-deal-for-ai-startup-decart-worth-up-to-7-5-billion/).

Amazon said the aim is to let customers train and run AI models faster and more cheaply on Trainium, its own AI chip, and that the two companies have worked together on Trainium since 2025. That is the point of the deal: every model that runs well on Trainium is one fewer reason for cloud customers to rent Nvidia's ($NVDA) chips. At $7.5 billion it is small next to Amazon's $2.77 trillion market value, but it is the price of buying software skill rather than building it.

Amazon shares were up 3.7% at $255.95 at 10:15 a.m. ET, with the rest of tech. The deal still needs regulatory clearance, Amazon said, and it has not said when it expects to close.

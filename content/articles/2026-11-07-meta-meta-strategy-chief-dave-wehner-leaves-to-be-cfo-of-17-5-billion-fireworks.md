Dave Wehner, Meta's ($META) chief strategy officer, is leaving to become chief financial officer of Fireworks, a startup that runs AI models for other companies, he said on X, CNBC reported on Saturday. Neither company has said when he starts.

Wehner is one of Meta's longest-serving senior executives. He joined Facebook in 2012, ran its finances from 2014 to 2022, and moved to strategy when Susan Li became CFO in November 2022, according to CFO Dive and CNBC. Fireworks raised about **$1.5 billion at a $17.5 billion valuation** in July, CNBC and Quartz reported, so he is leaving a $1.58 trillion company for a far smaller one that sells AI computing to other companies.

The timing makes the exit more visible than its effect on the numbers. Meta's capital spending of $34.91 billion outran its operating cash flow last quarter, and S&P has turned its outlook on Meta's debt to negative ([our breakdown](/news/2026-11-01/meta-meta-now-spends-more-than-it-makes-in-cash-is-the-business-still-strong/)). Li remains CFO, and nothing in the move points to a change in Meta's spending plans.

Meta shares closed at $610.15 on Friday and trade again on Monday.

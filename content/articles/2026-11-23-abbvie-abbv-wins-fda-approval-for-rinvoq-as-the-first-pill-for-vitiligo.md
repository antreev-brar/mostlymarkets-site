The Food and Drug Administration approved AbbVie's ($ABBV) Rinvoq on Monday for nonsegmental vitiligo, the most common form of the skin-depigmenting disease, making it **the first systemic therapy** approved in the US for the condition, AbbVie said in a release confirmed by Investing.com and HCPLive. The once-daily 15 mg pill is cleared for adults and children 12 and older.

The approval rests on two Phase 3 trials called Viti-Up. At 48 weeks, **25% and 23%** of patients on Rinvoq regained at least 75% of the pigment on their face, against 6% and 7% on placebo, the release said. Until now, patients relied on creams and light therapy, so Rinvoq starts with no approved pill to compete against.

The approval adds another immune-disease use to Rinvoq, which AbbVie already sells for conditions including eczema and rheumatoid arthritis. The shares edged up **0.8%** to $288.45 on Monday in a broad market rally. The next test is uptake: how quickly dermatologists prescribe a daily pill for a condition that has mostly been treated on the skin.

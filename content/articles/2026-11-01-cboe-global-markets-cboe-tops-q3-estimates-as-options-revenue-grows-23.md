Cboe Global Markets ($CBOE) reported record third-quarter net revenue of $712.2 million on Friday morning, up 18% year on year and a little above the $707.3 million analysts expected, Investing.com reported. Adjusted earnings rose 36% to **$3.63 a share**, beating the $3.43 average in Nasdaq's earnings calendar ($3.42 by Investing.com's count). The stock jumped 6.8% to $335.53 at the close, on a day the Nasdaq fell 2.9%.

## What stood out

**Index options are the engine.** Cboe earns a fee on every contract traded on its exclusive products, chiefly options on the S&P 500 and the VIX volatility index. Index options volume rose 24% to **6.1 million contracts a day**, the release showed, and options net revenue grew 23% to $469.5 million, two-thirds of the total.

*Verdict: the highest-margin part of the business grew fastest.*

**Turbulence helps.** Trading volumes rise when markets swing, because investors buy options to hedge or to bet on the moves. 247 Wall St credited record volumes in options that expire the same day they are traded, known as 0DTE options.

**The franchise got longer.** Cboe extended its license to list options on S&P indexes for 25 years, through 2051, according to call coverage by GuruFocus and BigGo. Those products are exclusive to Cboe, so the license is the core of its moat.

## The outlook

Cboe raised its 2026 forecast for organic net revenue growth to the high teens, from mid to high teens, and set adjusted operating expenses at $830 million to $845 million, the release said. It also closed the sale of Cboe Australia in the quarter.

## Our take

We were impressed that growth came from Cboe's own index products, where it has no direct competitor, and that the license extension removes the biggest long-term risk to them. On the other hand, the revenue beat was only 1%, and a business that feeds on volatility will see volumes cool if markets calm down. Overall, a strong quarter with a better long-run picture than before it.

By our data, Cboe's operating margin rose 11.8 points over five years, in the top quarter of our financials group, while revenue grew only 6.6% a year over five years. At 23.4 times forward earnings, against a 17.0 times median for our group, the price assumes high-teens growth continues into 2027. The fourth-quarter report in February will show whether volumes held up as the year ended.

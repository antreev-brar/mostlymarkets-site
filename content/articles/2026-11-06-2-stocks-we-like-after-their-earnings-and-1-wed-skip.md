Results season puts a fresh quarter next to the long-run record, and the two don't always agree. Of the big companies we recapped in the last seven days, Arista Networks and Vertex Pharmaceuticals reported quarters that strengthen a strong record, while Shopify's showed growth cooling at a price that still assumes it won't.

The screen: companies worth more than $10 billion that reported in the seven days to Friday 6 November and that we recapped, leaving out the stocks in our lists of the last 14 days. For each, we started from the quarter, then checked five-year growth, margins, cash and valuation against peers from SEC filings.

```picks
like: ANET, VRTX
skip: SHOP
```

## 2 stocks we like

### 1. Arista Networks ($ANET)

**Third-quarter revenue: $3.66 billion, up 58.5%.** Arista makes the Ethernet switches that connect AI servers inside data centers.

1. **The quarter beat its own forecast and Wall Street's.** Revenue came in about 11% above the $3.31 billion analysts expected, in Zacks data cited by the Associated Press, and Arista guided fourth-quarter revenue to $3.9 billion to $4.0 billion, its release said ([our recap](/news/2026-11-03/arista-networks-anet-beats-q3-estimates-as-sales-grow-58-5/)). On the call it pointed to 2027 revenue growth of 38% to 40%.
2. **It has compounded for five years.** Revenue grew 31.2% a year over five fiscal years, in the top tenth of the 31 hardware makers in our coverage, whose median is 5.6%, and EPS grew 40.6% a year.
3. **It keeps the profit.** Its operating margin was **43.8%** in the 12 months to September 2026, against a peer median of 18.1%, and it turned 42.2% of sales into free cash.

**The price:** 45.9 times trailing earnings, below its median of 52.0 times since November 2024; the stock closed Friday 0.5% above its pre-results close after Thursday's chip rout took back the post-earnings gain.

**What would change our mind:** Arista sells into the same data centers the midterm debate is about. A cut to the 2027 growth outlook when it reports early next year would mean the buildout is slowing where it matters for this stock.

### 2. Vertex Pharmaceuticals ($VRTX)

**Third-quarter revenue: $3.44 billion, up 12%.** Vertex dominates treatment of cystic fibrosis and is adding drugs for pain, sickle cell disease and kidney disease.

1. **The core franchise is renewing itself.** Its newer once-daily pill Alyftrek brought in $706 million, nearly three times a year earlier, while total cystic fibrosis sales grew 8%, the release showed, and Vertex raised its 2026 revenue forecast to $13.15 billion to $13.25 billion ([our recap](/news/2026-11-02/vertex-vrtx-tops-q3-sales-estimates-and-raises-its-2026-forecast/)).
2. **It is the most profitable drugmaker in our group.** Its operating margin was **33.2%** in the 12 months to September 2026, first of eight drugmakers with comparable data, whose median is 19.6%, and its gross margin was 85.9% over two years.
3. **Growth has lasted.** Revenue grew 14.1% a year over five fiscal years against a peer median of 8.1%, and it cut its share count 2.2% over two years.

**The price:** 29.8 times forward earnings against a drugmaker median of 18.2; the premium pays for a business that rarely misses.

**What could go wrong:** adjusted EPS slipped to $4.76 from $4.80 as Vertex spent on new launches, and EPS grew 8.3% a year over five years, below the peer median. US regulators are due to rule on its kidney drug povetacicept on 30 November; a rejection would leave the spending without the sales.

## 1 stock we'd skip

### Shopify ($SHOP)

**Third-quarter revenue: $3.74 billion, up 32%.** Shopify sells the software and payments behind online stores.

1. **The number that drives revenue slowed.** Gross merchandise volume, the value of everything sold on its stores, grew 29%, down from 32% in the second quarter and about $900 million short of the $120.1 billion expected, Investing.com and 24/7 Wall St. reported ([our recap](/news/2026-11-03/shopify-shop-q3-gmv-and-subscriptions-miss-and-the-stock-sinks-15/)). Subscription revenue also missed, at $836 million.
2. **Its margins trail its peers.** The gross margin was 48.0% over two years, in the bottom quarter of our IT services and internet group, whose median is 58.3%, because more of its sales come from low-margin payments.
3. **Its operating margin is ordinary.** At **14.6%** over 12 months, it sits below the peer median of 15.6%, even after widening 9.6 points over five years.

**The price:** 71.7 times forward earnings, four times the peer median of 18.0, and 72.0 times free cash flow. The stock closed Friday 4.7% below its pre-results close.

**What would change our mind:** five-year revenue growth of 31.6% a year is real, and the 22% free-cash-flow margin in the quarter is rising. GMV growth back above 30% in the holiday quarter, reported in February, would show the slowdown was a blip.

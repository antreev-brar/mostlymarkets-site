Elon Musk said in a post on X on Saturday that Google has expressed interest in renting more computing capacity from SpaceX, Reuters and Stockanalysis reported. "Not sure who will buy more, but Google has expressed interest in renting more compute from us," he wrote, giving no size, price or timing; neither company commented.

Google, part of Alphabet ($GOOGL), already pays SpaceX **$920 million a month**, about $11 billion a year, for roughly 110,000 Nvidia ($NVDA) chips and related hardware from October to June 2029, under a deal announced in June. SpaceX has said its compute agreements with Google and Anthropic are together worth more than $70 billion, and it plans to spend more than $74 billion on new supercomputer sites in Texas and Arizona.

A bigger contract would suggest Google wants more capacity than it can build itself, a point in favor of the [AI spending story](/news/2026-11-26/wall-street-is-betting-the-tech-rout-is-nearly-over-4-numbers-will-decide-it/) after a month of doubts about it. Until SpaceX or Google announces an amended contract, it is one sentence from Musk, not a deal.

Global Payments ($GPN) **jumped 12.0%** to $93.53 as of 2:15 p.m. ET on Friday after Bloomberg News reported that private equity firm GTCR is weighing a takeover of the payments processor worth about $50 billion including debt. GTCR is lining up other buyout firms to help pay for it, while Clayton, Dubilier & Rice, its partner on an earlier joint approach, has dropped out, Bloomberg reported, citing people familiar with the matter. No decision has been made, and neither company responded to Reuters right away.

GTCR already owns about 16% of Global Payments, a stake it received when it sold its 55% of Worldpay to the company last year, so it knows the business from the inside. The report reverses most of the [12.0% fall on 12 November](/news/2026-11-13/global-payments-gpn-plunges-12-as-buyout-firms-drop-takeover-talks/), when Reuters reported that CD&R and GTCR had ended their talks; even after Friday's jump, the stock sits below its 10 November closing high of $101.59.

What isn't known yet is the price per share, who GTCR's new partners are, and whether the board will engage. Until a bid is confirmed by the company, this is one outlet's report of a plan under consideration.

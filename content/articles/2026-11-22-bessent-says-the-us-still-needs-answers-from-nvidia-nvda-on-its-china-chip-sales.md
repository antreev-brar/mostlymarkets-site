Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday, 22 November, that the administration still needs information on Nvidia's ($NVDA) chip sales to China and wants to find out who led compliance at the company, Reuters reported from his interview on CBS's "Face the Nation." "We do need answers," he said, adding that officials have spoken to Nvidia's interim management, lead directors and lawyers.

The pressure has a date on it. On 15 November Bessent gave Nvidia's board about 10 days to answer questions on export-control compliance, Fortune and Unite.ai reported, a window that closes around Wednesday ([our brief on his call for an outside CEO](/news/2026-11-15/bessent-says-nvidia-nvda-should-hire-an-outside-ceo-after-huang/)). It adds to an SEC probe of [Nvidia's accounting](/news/2026-11-20/sec-probe-of-nvidia-nvda-is-examining-its-accounting-reuters-reports/) and a California inquiry that [may cover criminal violations](/news/2026-11-21/californias-nvidia-nvda-probe-covers-possible-criminal-violations-bonta-says/).

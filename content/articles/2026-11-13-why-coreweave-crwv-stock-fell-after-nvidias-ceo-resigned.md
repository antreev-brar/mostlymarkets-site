## What happened
Shares of AI-cloud provider CoreWeave ($CRWV) **slid 6.9%** on Thursday, after Nvidia ($NVDA) said co-founder Jensen Huang had resigned as chief executive. CoreWeave rents out Nvidia chips by the hour, and Nvidia is both its main supplier and a shareholder, so the leadership shock hit it harder than most of the AI trade, TipRanks and Investing.com reported.

The fall was worst early. The stock traded as low as $50.17, down 17.4% from Wednesday's close in our data, then recovered as Nvidia itself pared a loss of as much as 14%. It closed at $56.60, down 6.9%, on 1.4 times its usual volume. TheStreet and Yahoo Finance both tied the drop to worries that a change at the top of Nvidia could slow the chip supply and capacity deals CoreWeave depends on.

## Why it matters
Thursday undid part of Wednesday. CoreWeave had **jumped 24.7%** on Wednesday after its third-quarter report on Tuesday evening: revenue rose 168% year on year to about $3.66 billion, its contracted backlog reached about $132.5 billion, and it raised its 2026 revenue forecast to $13.0 billion to $13.3 billion, the company said in its release.

That report eased the fear we laid out in our [narrative on the neoclouds](/news/2026-11-07/wall-street-is-betting-the-neoclouds-are-ais-weak-link-coreweaves-report-will-test-it/): that the companies renting out AI chips on borrowed money are the weakest link in the build-out. Thursday shows the other side of that bet. CoreWeave's fortunes are tied to one supplier, and a shock at Nvidia lands on it with leverage.

## What the market is telling us
CoreWeave's shares moved 5% or more on 97 of the last 252 sessions. The last move at least this big in the same direction was on 5 November 2026, when the stock fell 9.8%. The stock is down 21.0% this year, and at $56.60 it sits 59.0% below its 52-week closing high of $137.98 from 6 May 2026.

A stock that moves 5% on more than a third of its sessions was always going to swing on Nvidia news; the test is Nvidia's own report on Tuesday 17 November, which will show whether its customers' orders held up through the change. Before Friday's open CoreWeave was up 0.5% at $56.86, as of 7:15 a.m. ET.

Marvell Technology ($MRVL) agreed on Monday morning to buy Lumentum ($LITE), the maker of lasers for AI data-center networks, in an all-stock deal the companies valued at about **$115 billion** including debt. Lumentum holders get 4.5 Marvell shares each, worth $1,132 at Marvell's close on 28 October, a 23.9% premium, the companies said. Marvell holders will own about 67% of the combined company, which targets more than $1 billion a year in cost savings.

Those numbers predate the leak. Marvell **fell 22%** in the two sessions after Bloomberg reported the talks on 28 October, as investors balked at the dilution, MarketScreener and Yahoo Finance reported; Friday's chip rout added to it. At Friday's $196.40 close, 4.5 Marvell shares were worth $883.80, 5.1% above Lumentum's close. As of 7:30 a.m. ET, Lumentum was up 3.0% before the open and Marvell was flat. Analysts said owning Lumentum would make Marvell a closer rival to Broadcom ($AVGO) and Coherent ($COHR) in AI networking ([our chip sell-off story](/news/2026-10-31/the-chip-index-fell-7-2-in-a-day-where-did-the-damage-land/)).

It needs shareholder and regulatory approval and is expected to close in the first half of 2028.

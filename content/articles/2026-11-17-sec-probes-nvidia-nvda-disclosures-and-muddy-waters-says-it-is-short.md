The Securities and Exchange Commission is investigating Nvidia ($NVDA), including whether the company's disclosures misled investors, the Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday afternoon, as relayed by Reuters and Investing.com. The probe follows Jensen Huang's resignation as chief executive last week. About an hour earlier, short seller Muddy Waters Research said it is betting against the stock, TheFly and TheStreet reported.

Muddy Waters' report questions ties between Nvidia's former head of global sales and a former Super Micro Computer ($SMCI) executive, at a time when US prosecutors have charged three people connected to Super Micro over server sales to China. The firm said no one involved has been charged and that it cannot confirm one person's identity, Crypto Briefing and MarketScreener reported; it does not accuse Nvidia of a crime.

Nvidia closed **down 0.4% at $155.46**, its lowest close in a year and 35.0% below its 6 October high, by our price data. Both items land on a company already searching for a [new chief](/news/2026-11-17/nvidia-nvda-hires-heidrick-for-its-ceo-search-and-talks-to-arms-rene-haas/) and that [beat forecasts after the close](/news/2026-11-17/stock-market-today-salesforce-leads-a-software-rout-as-the-dow-drops-849-points/). Its call with interim Chief Executive Colette Kress starts at 5 p.m. ET, the first chance for analysts to ask about the probe.

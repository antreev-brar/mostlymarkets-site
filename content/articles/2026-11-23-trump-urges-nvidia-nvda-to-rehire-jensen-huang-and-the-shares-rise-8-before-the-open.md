President Donald Trump wrote on Truth Social overnight that he hopes Nvidia's ($NVDA) board **rehires Jensen Huang**, who resigned as chief executive on 12 November. "It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A.," Trump wrote, as Investing.com and Fox Business reported. Nvidia rose 8.3% to $135.98 before the open, as of 6:30 a.m. ET, as chip stocks bounced after Friday's rout.

The post runs against the board. Chair Harvey Jones told investors on 17 November that Nvidia would not reinstate Huang, and the search firm Heidrick & Struggles is looking for a replacement, Forbes and Yahoo Finance reported. Huang remains under scrutiny: California's attorney general says his probe of Nvidia covers possible criminal violations ([our brief](/news/2026-11-21/californias-nvidia-nvda-probe-covers-possible-criminal-violations-bonta-says/)), and Huang has a closed-door House interview on 1 December.

Nvidia had fallen seven weeks in a row, its longest losing streak since 2008, and closed Friday 47.5% below its 6 October record. The board has not responded publicly to the post; whether it does, and how the stock holds once trading opens, is the next test ([our Daily](/daily/2026-11-23/)).

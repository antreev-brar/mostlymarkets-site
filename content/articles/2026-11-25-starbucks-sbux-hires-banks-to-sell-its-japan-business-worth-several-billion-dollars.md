Starbucks ($SBUX) has hired Citigroup and Morgan Stanley to prepare a possible sale of its Japan business, which could fetch **several billion dollars**, Bloomberg reported late on Tuesday, citing people familiar with the matter. Reuters, which carried the report, said in September that a majority-stake sale could value the unit at about $3 billion. Starbucks said it continually assesses the best structure for its customers and for shareholder value; the banks declined to comment.

Japan, with about 2,000 stores, is the company's biggest overseas market that it runs itself, Reuters reported. Selling it would swap direct ownership of a large, mature market for cash that Starbucks could put toward the US turnaround that has lifted the stock **26.3% this year** in our data. The shares closed at $106.38 on Tuesday, 3.2% below their November high, and slipped 0.3% after hours.

Starbucks has begun gauging interest from strategic and financial buyers, but deliberations are ongoing and may not end in a sale, Bloomberg's sources said. No timetable was reported.

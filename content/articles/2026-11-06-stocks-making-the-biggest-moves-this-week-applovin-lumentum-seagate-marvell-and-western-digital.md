One stock soared and four sank in the week to Friday 6 November, measured from last Friday's close to this Friday's close. The four losers all sell parts for AI data centers, and each did most of its damage on Thursday, when the Nasdaq had its biggest one-day drop in 19 months on fears that the Democratic sweep of Congress will slow the data-center buildout.

**AppLovin ($APP)**, +71.2%: the mobile-ad software company soared 31.0% on Thursday, its best day since November 2024, after third-quarter revenue rose 55% to $2.19 billion, ahead of the $2.07 billion analysts expected according to Bloomberg estimates cited by Yahoo Finance, and its fourth-quarter forecast also beat. It added 9.9% on Friday to close at $391.22. [Full story](/news/2026-11-05/applovin-app-soars-31-as-q3-sales-rise-55-and-its-forecast-tops-estimates/)

**Lumentum ($LITE)**, -35.9%: the laser maker fell 18.6% on Monday, the day it signed a $115 billion all-stock sale to Marvell, because its holders are paid in Marvell shares that kept falling. It lost another 18.7% in Thursday's rout and bounced 5.7% on Friday to $538.59. [Full story](/news/2026-11-02/lumentum-lite-and-marvell-mrvl-shares-slide-on-the-day-their-deal-is-signed/)

**Seagate Technology ($STX)**, -28.5%: the hard-drive maker fell 7.9% on Monday with no clear news, then 16.3% on Thursday as storage and memory stocks led the chip sell-off. It closed Friday at $460.99, down another 1.2%. [Full story](/news/2026-11-05/sandisk-micron-and-amd-shares-plunge-what-happened/)

**Marvell Technology ($MRVL)**, -28.1%: the chipmaker fell 12.7% on Monday as investors balked at issuing so many new shares to buy Lumentum, then 16.4% on Thursday. It rose 5.7% on Friday to $141.20. [Full story](/news/2026-11-02/marvell-mrvl-agrees-to-buy-lumentum-lite-for-115-billion-in-stock/)

**Western Digital ($WDC)**, -28.0%: Seagate's rival in hard drives fell 17.4% on Thursday, when investors bet a pause on new data centers in Texas, home to about 27% of planned US capacity by Wells Fargo's estimate, would cut demand for storage. It closed Friday at $263.78. [Full story](/news/2026-11-05/sandisk-micron-and-amd-shares-plunge-what-happened/)

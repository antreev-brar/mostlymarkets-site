The argument this week is whether the AI sell-off is pricing a real slowdown in orders or has run ahead of them. The S&P 500 ended Friday in a correction after its worst week since April 2025, and the stocks that build AI data centers fell hardest, even though Nvidia ($NVDA) guided to $130 billion of sales on Tuesday. On Tuesday after the close, AI server maker Dell ($DELL) gives the first order book since then, with its stock **52.8% below** its September high.

A day later the government's PCE inflation index, the Fed's preferred gauge, is expected to show prices rising faster again, which would firm up a rate hike on 9 December. The weekend added to Nvidia's legal troubles and gave bitcoin a lift back above $60,000. Markets close on Thursday for Thanksgiving and at 1 p.m. ET on Friday.

Nvidia's **seventh straight weekly loss** is its longest losing streak since 2008, CNBC and Stock Analysis reported, and it closed Friday at $125.53, 47.5% below its 6 October record in our data. Friday's 14.0% drop came after Reuters reported that the SEC is [examining its accounting](/news/2026-11-20/sec-probe-of-nvidia-nvda-is-examining-its-accounting-reuters-reports/) ([the week in eight charts](/daily/2026-11-21/)).

### Dell reports with a $95 billion backlog and a stock 53% off its high

```chart

 "notes": [{"date": "2026-09-02", "label": "AI forecast raised"}, {"date": "2026-11-05", "label": "AI hardware sell-off"}],
 "title": "Dell has given back its whole post-forecast rally", "subtitle": "Dell Technologies, daily close, $"}
```

Dell left its last quarter with a record **$95 billion backlog** after booking $60.9 billion of AI orders, and raised its forecast for AI server sales this fiscal year to $74 billion from $60 billion, it said in September, as CNBC and Constellation Research reported. The stock has since fallen to $278.02, down 39.8% in the past month alone, including a 14.9% drop on 5 November when [AI hardware sold off after the midterms](/news/2026-11-05/stock-market-today-nasdaq-sinks-5-5-into-a-correction-on-midterm-ai-fears/), 24/7 Wall St. and Yahoo Finance reported. Tuesday shows whether those orders are still turning into sales or customers are delaying them.

It comes out at 8:30 a.m. ET on Wednesday with the second estimate of third-quarter growth, CMC Markets and BigGo Finance reported. A hotter number would make a hike in December harder to argue against, even with stocks in a correction ([our story on the Fed minutes](/news/2026-11-18/fed-minutes-show-almost-all-officials-expect-more-rate-hikes-and-flag-ai-as-an-inflation-risk/)).

## What's next

All times ET. Markets are closed on Thursday, 26 November, and close at 1 p.m. on Friday.

- **Monday, 23 November:** Hewlett Packard Enterprise, Agilent ($A) and Jacobs ($J) report after the close.
- **Tuesday, 24 November:** Analog Devices, Medtronic, Best Buy, Dollar Tree and J.M. Smucker ($SJM) before the open; Dell, Autodesk ($ADSK), Workday ($WDAY) and HP ($HPQ) after the close.
- **Wednesday, 25 November:** Deere ($DE) before the open. October PCE inflation and the second estimate of third-quarter GDP at 8:30 a.m.
- **Friday, 27 November:** Black Friday, the first read on holiday spending, in a half-day session.
- **Later:** Jensen Huang's House interview on 1 December, the Fed's decision on 9 December and Micron's report on 17 December.

We think Dell's report matters most this week: it carries a $95 billion backlog into a stock that has lost almost 40% in a month, so a forecast that holds would show the sell-off has run ahead of the orders, and a cut would confirm the bears' case for the whole AI hardware chain.

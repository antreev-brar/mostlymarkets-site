Anthropic's shares fell below their **$83** IPO price on Tuesday morning for the first time since the AI lab listed last Thursday, CNBC, Forbes and Investing.com reported. The stock opened at $111.50 on its first day and closed it at $97.17, so Tuesday's dip erases the whole of the debut premium.

The slide came as the first analyst reports came in mostly positive. Canaccord Genuity started coverage at buy with a $135 target, Oppenheimer at outperform with $150 and Wedbush at outperform with $120, CNBC, Investing.com and TipRanks reported.

The gap between those targets and the price is the [debate we set out on Friday](/news/2026-11-20/bulls-vs-bears-on-anthropic-after-its-debut-can-growth-justify-the-price/): whether growth that slowed to 47% from 144% a quarter can carry an $83 price. Tuesday's drop also lands on a morning when Nvidia and the chip stocks gave back part of Monday's rally ([our story](/news/2026-11-24/amd-credo-and-monolithic-power-shares-slide-what-happened/)). Amazon ($AMZN) and Alphabet ($GOOGL), which together own more than a quarter of Anthropic, were down 0.7% and 1.3% as of 10:30 a.m. ET.

The next hard evidence on that growth is Anthropic's first quarterly report as a public company.

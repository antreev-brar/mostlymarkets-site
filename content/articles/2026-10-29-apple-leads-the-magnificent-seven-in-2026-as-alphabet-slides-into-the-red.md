Apple ($AAPL) is the best of the Magnificent Seven this year, up 28.3% after rising 1.5% on Thursday to a 52-week closing high of $348.91. Nvidia ($NVDA) is second, up 14.6%, followed by Microsoft ($MSFT) at 9.6% and Amazon ($AMZN) at 5.6%.

Thursday's big change came from Alphabet ($GOOGL). It fell 12.6% to $283.82, its worst day in at least five years, the day after its third-quarter report. Revenue rose 25% to $127.58 billion, but diluted earnings per share fell to $2.12 from $2.87 as other income swung to an $8.22 billion loss from a $12.76 billion gain a year earlier, the company said in its results release.

That leaves three of the seven below where they began the year: Meta ($META), down 5.4%, Alphabet, down 9.3%, and Tesla ($TSLA), down 10.3%.

What to watch: Amazon's results, due after Thursday's close, and Apple's on Monday, 2 November.

PDD Holdings ($PDD), the owner of the Pinduoduo shopping app in China and Temu abroad, reports its third-quarter results before the US market opens on Thursday, 19 November, with a call at 7:30 a.m. ET. It is Temu's first full quarter under the European Union's new duty of 3 euros per item on parcels worth up to 150 euros, which took effect on 1 July.

Last quarter, on 24 August, PDD said revenue grew 8% to 112.4 billion yuan, but net income attributable to shareholders **fell 13%** to 27.2 billion yuan as it "stepped up" spending on merchants and shoppers, its results release shows. Transaction-services revenue rose 13% to 54.7 billion yuan. The shares fell 1.5% that day in our price data.

## What analysts expect

FactSet's consensus puts revenue at **115.67 billion yuan**, up from 108.28 billion a year earlier, ad-hoc-news reported, and Tikr cites a similar figure of about 116 billion, up about 7%. FactSet's survey also points to lower profit than a year ago. PDD has missed revenue estimates in each of the last four quarters, Tikr notes.

## What to watch

- **Temu in Europe.** The duty raises the cost of exactly the cheap, single-item orders Temu built its business on; any comment on European order volumes is the first hard read on what it costs.
- **The spending.** Management said it stepped up its investment in merchants last quarter; another fall in profit would say that spending isn't paying back yet.
- **China's shoppers.** JD.com ($JD) [missed revenue estimates](/news/2026-11-17/jd-com-jd-misses-q3-revenue-estimates-at-309-6-billion-yuan-as-shoppers-hold-back/) this week as shoppers held back, and Alibaba ($BABA) reports the same morning ([our preview](/news/2026-11-18/what-to-expect-from-alibabas-baba-september-quarter-earnings/)).

## How the stock has done

PDD closed at $77.45 on Wednesday, **down 31.7% this year** and 5.1% over the past month, 36.9% below its 6 January high of $122.66. At 6.8 times forward earnings, about two-thirds of Alibaba's multiple, the price assumes profits keep shrinking.

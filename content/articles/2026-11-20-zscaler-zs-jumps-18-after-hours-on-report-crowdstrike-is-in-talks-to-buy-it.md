Zscaler ($ZS) **jumped 17.8%** to $229.56 in after-hours trading on Friday, as of 5:15 p.m. ET, after Bloomberg reported that CrowdStrike ($CRWD) is in talks to buy it, citing people familiar with the matter. CrowdStrike fell 6.7% after hours. The talks could produce a deal soon but may still fall apart, Bloomberg said; CrowdStrike declined to comment and Zscaler did not immediately respond, CNBC and Reuters reported.

At Friday's close Zscaler was worth about $32 billion and CrowdStrike about $235 billion, so the target is a seventh of the buyer's size. A deal would add Zscaler's cloud gateway, which inspects company traffic on its way to the internet, to CrowdStrike's software that guards laptops and servers. Both stocks had fallen more than 5% in Friday's regular session as the Nasdaq dropped 5.3%, and Palo Alto Networks' [bigger forecast this week](/news/2026-11-18/palo-alto-networks-panw-beats-q1-estimates-raises-its-forecast-and-buys-applied-compute/) had lifted the whole group.

No price or terms have been reported. CrowdStrike is due to report on 1 December and Zscaler on 2 December, both after the close, according to the Nasdaq earnings calendar.

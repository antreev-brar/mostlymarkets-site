Medtronic ($MDT) reports results for its fiscal second quarter, the three months to October, **before the open on Tuesday, 24 November**, with a call at 7:30 a.m. ET, MarketBeat and The Cerbat Gem reported. The shares closed Monday at $100.72, up 0.8%, and 5.6% higher over the past month.

Last quarter, the three months to July, revenue rose **13.7%** to $9.76 billion, and adjusted earnings were $1.45 a share. Medtronic raised its full-year forecast for adjusted earnings to $5.94 to $6.00 a share and for organic sales growth to 7.25% to 7.75%, the release said, and the stock rose 1.5% that day in our data.

## What analysts expect

Analysts expect adjusted earnings of **$1.33 a share**, down 2.2% from $1.36 a year ago, on revenue of $9.48 billion, up 5.8% from $8.96 billion, according to Seeking Alpha and MarketBeat. Medtronic itself guided to $1.32 to $1.34. It has beaten the consensus in each of its last eight quarters, MarketBeat said, though Seeking Alpha reported that estimates have been revised down going in.

## The one question

Was last quarter's jump the start of faster growth or a high point? Over five years Medtronic's revenue grew 3.8% a year, in the bottom quarter of the 19 medical-device makers we cover, so a forecast that keeps growth above 7% would be a real change for a slow grower. The full-year guidance also still includes its diabetes business, which Medtronic plans to separate; it said it would update the forecast if that happens before the fiscal year ends.

## What to watch

- **Organic revenue growth** against 5.8%: a clear beat would support the raised 7.25% to 7.75% range.
- **The full-year EPS range** of $5.94 to $6.00: a second raise, or a trim.
- **The diabetes separation timing,** which changes the base for next year's numbers.

## How the stock has done

Medtronic is up 4.8% this year and sits 4.4% below its 52-week closing high of $105.35. At 16.8 times expected earnings it trades a little below the 17.8 times median for device makers we cover. $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $888 today.

Amazon ($AMZN) is in advanced talks to buy Decart, an Israeli artificial-intelligence startup, in a deal worth more than $7 billion in cash, stock and deferred payments, rising to nearly $7.5 billion if Decart hits set targets, the Financial Times reported on Saturday, as carried by Reuters, and Israel's Calcalist reported. Decart's three founders would commit to stay for five years, with much of their payout held back until then, Calcalist said.

The price is about 1.75 times the roughly $4 billion Decart was valued at in May, according to Calcalist and FourWeekMBA, after a contest in which Nvidia ($NVDA) and Anthropic also held talks, Calcalist reported. Decart already works with AWS, Amazon's cloud arm. At $7.5 billion the deal is small for a company worth more than $2.5 trillion, but it shows the biggest AI buyers are still paying up for talent and software in a week when [the Nasdaq 100 had its worst week since 2020](/news/2026-11-20/stock-market-today-the-s-p-500-enters-a-correction-as-nvidia-sinks-14/) and Amazon fell 4.1% on Friday.

Neither company has commented. Calcalist said the deal could be signed within days.

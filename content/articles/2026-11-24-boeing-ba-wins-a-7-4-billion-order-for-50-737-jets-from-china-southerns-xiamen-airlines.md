Xiamen Airlines, a subsidiary of China Southern Airlines, signed an agreement with Boeing ($BA) on Tuesday to buy **50 737-8 jets**, China Southern said in a stock exchange filing, as Reuters reported. The jets carry a list price of about $7.4 billion, though the price actually paid will be lower, and the deal still needs approval from the relevant authorities.

It is Boeing's first major order from China in years, Bloomberg reported via The Edge Singapore, and it fulfills part of a commitment China made earlier this year to buy hundreds of Boeing aircraft; President Donald Trump said he expects China to buy 250 more.

Boeing closed up 4.2% at $162.39 on Monday and was little changed before the open, as of 6:10 a.m. ET. The stock is down 25.2% this year in our data and slid in October when Boeing delayed its 737 production ramp ([our story](/news/2026-10-27/boeing-ba-shares-slide-after-it-delays-its-737-production-ramp/)). The next question is whether Beijing approves the order, and whether more Chinese airlines follow.

Jacobs Solutions ($J), the engineering and consulting firm that designs water systems, transport projects and data centres, reported fiscal fourth-quarter adjusted earnings of **$2.20 a share** after the close on Monday, up 25.7% from a year earlier and above the $2.12 LSEG consensus, its release and Reuters showed. Adjusted net revenue rose 15.7% to $2.59 billion.

Its backlog, the work customers have signed for but Jacobs hasn't yet billed, rose 24.2% to $28.7 billion, the release said, faster than revenue, so more growth is already booked. The company forecast double-digit growth in adjusted EPS for fiscal 2027 at the midpoint of its range. The shares closed up 4.6% at $157.53 before the report, 1.7% below their 2 November closing high, and are up 18.9% this year in our data.

The after-hours reaction and the call will show whether the 2027 outlook clears what the stock already prices in.

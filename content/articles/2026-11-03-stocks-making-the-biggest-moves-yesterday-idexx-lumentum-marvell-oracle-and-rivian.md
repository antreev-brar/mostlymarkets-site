A buyout, a funding plan, an earnings beat and an analyst upgrade drove Monday's biggest moves among US companies worth $10 billion or more, and the biggest loss came on the day the buyout was signed.

**Idexx Laboratories ($IDXX)**, +21.9%: the maker of veterinary diagnostic tests closed at $671.64 after earning $4.01 a share against the $3.71 analysts expected, as recurring diagnostics revenue grew 11%, and raising its 2026 forecasts. [Full story](/news/2026-11-02/idexx-idxx-beats-q3-estimates-and-raises-its-2026-outlook/)

**Lumentum ($LITE)**, -18.6%: the laser maker had its worst day since April 2025, closing at $684.40, on the day it agreed to be bought by Marvell in an all-stock deal valued at about $115 billion. Because Lumentum holders are paid in Marvell shares, Marvell's own fall cut the value of the offer. [Full story](/news/2026-11-02/lumentum-lite-and-marvell-mrvl-shares-slide-on-the-day-their-deal-is-signed/)

**Marvell Technology ($MRVL)**, -12.7%: the chipmaker fell to $171.41 as investors weighed the cost of issuing 4.5 of its shares for each Lumentum share, and AI-networking peers Ciena and Astera Labs fell about 11% with it. [Full story](/news/2026-11-02/bulls-vs-bears-on-marvell-mrvl-is-lumentum-worth-a-third-of-the-company/)

**Oracle ($ORCL)**, -8.8%: the database and cloud company closed at $119.22 after filing to sell $10 billion of bonds and $10 billion of stock, five days after it said $20 billion was its funding limit through fiscal 2030. It has since [priced the new shares at $116](/news/2026-11-03/oracle-orcl-sells-its-new-shares-at-116-raising-the-full-20-billion/). [Full story](/news/2026-11-02/oracle-orcl-shares-slide-after-it-files-to-raise-20-billion-in-stock-and-bonds/)

**Rivian ($RIVN)**, +8.5%: the electric-truck maker closed at $13.19 after UBS upgraded it to Buy with a $17 target, betting the cheaper R2 will turn its car margins positive by early 2027. [Full story](/news/2026-11-02/rivian-rivn-shares-jump-after-ubs-upgrades-the-stock-to-buy-on-its-r2-ramp/)

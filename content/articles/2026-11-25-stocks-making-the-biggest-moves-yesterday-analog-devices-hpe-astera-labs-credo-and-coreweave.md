**Analog Devices ($ADI)**, -17.4%: the analog-chip maker closed at $312.95 on Tuesday, its biggest move of the past 252 sessions in our data, even though fiscal fourth-quarter revenue rose 42% to $4.37 billion. Its forecast implied almost no growth next quarter, and the drop pulled Texas Instruments ($TXN) down 11.3% with it. [Full story](/news/2026-11-24/analog-devices-adi-beats-q4-estimates-but-a-flat-q1-forecast-sends-shares-down-8/) and [our breakdown](/news/2026-11-24/analog-devices-adi-is-30-below-its-high-after-a-flat-forecast-is-the-business-broken/)

**Hewlett Packard Enterprise ($HPE)**, -16.3%: the server maker closed at $40.19, wiping out Monday's 17.4% gain, after Chief Financial Officer Marie Myers said the operating margin of its Cloud & AI segment will fall to about 14% in fiscal 2027 from 18.4% in the fourth quarter, Seeking Alpha and Investing.com reported. [Full story](/news/2026-11-24/hpe-hpe-slides-11-5-before-the-open-as-it-sees-ai-server-margins-falling-to-14/)

**Astera Labs ($ALAB)**, -13.9%: the maker of connectivity chips for AI servers closed at $191.30, giving back more than half of Monday's 23.2% jump on Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia ($NVDA). There was no company news; the Motley Fool and TIKR put it among the AI suppliers that fell harder than Nvidia's 5.9% as the rally unwound. It was its second fall this size in three sessions. [Our market wrap](/news/2026-11-24/stock-market-today-chip-stocks-give-back-mondays-rally-while-the-dow-closes-above-50000/)

**Credo Technology ($CRDO)**, -13.1%: the networking-chip maker closed at $106.46 after a 22.6% gain on Monday, with no company news, as Seeking Alpha and Yahoo Finance described a retreat across chip stocks after the record rally. It is down 26.0% this year. [Full story](/news/2026-11-24/amd-credo-and-monolithic-power-shares-slide-what-happened/)

**CoreWeave ($CRWV)**, -11.9%: the AI cloud provider closed at $42.60, taking back about half of Monday's 22.3% rebound, after HPE said its financing arm has not lent to "neocloud or high-risk customers", the label for specialist AI clouds such as CoreWeave, TipRanks and Stocktwits reported. The stock is down 40.5% this year and 69.1% below its 6 May high. Dell ($DELL) fell almost as far, 11.8%, before its results that evening. [Full story](/news/2026-11-24/coreweave-and-nebius-shares-slide-what-happened/)

Clorox ($CLX) said after the close on Monday that adjusted earnings per share **rose 82% to $1.55** in its fiscal first quarter, against the $1.06 I/B/E/S estimate, Reuters reported. Net sales grew 33% to $1.91 billion, against $1.88 billion expected, the release and Reuters said.

The jump is mostly a comparison effect: a year ago, retailers ran down their Clorox inventories after the company switched to a new enterprise software system, a drawdown Clorox says cut fiscal 2026 EPS by about 90 cents. The news that matters for the rest of the year is costs. Clorox kept its fiscal 2027 forecasts of 13% to 14% sales growth and $5.70 to $6.00 of adjusted EPS, but now expects EPS near the low end and a gross margin of about 41.5%, citing higher inflation, StockTitan and Reuters reported. Its first-quarter gross margin was 40.4%.

The shares closed up 2.2% at $89.90 and were up 2.3% after hours at $92.00 as of 4:29 p.m. ET. Analysts had been cutting their targets on growth worries before the report, Investing.com reported; the low-end guide is the first test of whether that caution was right.

**Nu Holdings ($NU)**, -17.0%: The Brazil-based owner of the digital bank Nubank had its biggest drop in a year after President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva won Sunday's runoff, a result investors had bet against, and Brazil's Bovespa index fell 13.88%, its worst day since March 2020. [Full story](/news/2026-10-26/nu-holdings-nu-and-mercadolibre-meli-shares-slide-after-lula-wins-in-brazil/)

**Fair Isaac ($FICO)**, -13.2%: The company behind the FICO credit score was the worst performer in the S&P 500 after Wells Fargo cut it to Equal Weight, because Fair Isaac is holding its mortgage score price at $10 for lenders who commit to its score, which removes the price increases the bank had expected in 2027. [Full story](/news/2026-10-26/why-fair-isaac-fico-stock-is-sinking-today/)

**Lumentum ($LITE)**, +8.2%: The maker of optical parts for AI data centers was the best performer in the S&P 500 after StoneX started covering the stock with a Buy rating and a $1,200 price target, Benzinga reported, on a day when Anthropic's IPO filing lifted chip and AI-hardware stocks across the board.

**MercadoLibre ($MELI)**, -7.8%: The online marketplace and payments company, whose biggest market is Brazil, fell with the rest of the country's stocks after Lula's win, as the real ended about 8% weaker against the dollar and shrank the dollar value of its Brazilian sales. [Full story](/news/2026-10-26/nu-holdings-nu-and-mercadolibre-meli-shares-slide-after-lula-wins-in-brazil/)

**DoorDash ($DASH)**, -6.1%: The delivery app fell after The Wall Street Journal reported it is in advanced talks to buy grocery-delivery rival Instacart; a source told Reuters the deal would value Instacart at about $17 billion and could be signed as soon as Wednesday. [Full story](/news/2026-10-26/doordash-dash-shares-slide-on-report-it-is-in-talks-to-buy-instacart/)

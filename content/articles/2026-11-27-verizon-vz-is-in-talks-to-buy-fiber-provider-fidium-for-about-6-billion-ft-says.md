Verizon ($VZ) is in talks to buy Fidium, a fiber internet provider, in a deal that could value it at **about $6 billion** including debt, the Financial Times reported on Friday, in a story relayed by Reuters and Bloomberg. Fidium, known as Consolidated Communications until private equity firm Searchlight Capital and Canadian pension manager BCI bought it in a $3.1 billion deal in 2024, sells fiber broadband in 21 states.

The deal would follow Verizon's takeover of Frontier Communications, and its biggest gain would come in northern New England, where Fidium has about 351,000 subscribing homes, the FT reported. Reuters reported it would support Verizon's goal of reaching as many as 50 million homes and businesses with fiber. At about $6 billion it is a modest purchase for a company we put on our [dividend list on 17 November](/news/2026-11-17/3-dividend-stocks-we-like-and-1-wed-skip-verizon-att-realty-income-and-international-paper/) for paying its dividend out of cash with room to spare. Its shares closed 0.6% higher at $47.54.

No deal is signed. The talks could produce one in the coming weeks, but another bidder could emerge, and Verizon and Fidium did not respond to Reuters' requests for comment.

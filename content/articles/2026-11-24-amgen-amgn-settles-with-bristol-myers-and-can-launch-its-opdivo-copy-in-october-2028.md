Amgen ($AMGN) said after the close on Tuesday that it had settled the US patent litigation with Bristol Myers Squibb ($BMY) and Ono Pharmaceutical over ABP 206, its biosimilar of the cancer immunotherapy Opdivo, and can launch it on **1 October 2028**, subject to FDA approval. The terms are confidential, Amgen said in its release; TheFly and Dow Jones also reported the deal.

The date matters on both sides. Opdivo brought Bristol Myers about $10 billion in sales in 2025, by the company's results, and Bristol Myers had told the SEC it expects patent exclusivity until 2028. The settlement turns a Delaware suit over seven patents, filed in September, into a known date, and Amgen said it lets ABP 206 launch before patents that could otherwise have been asserted against it expire. The FDA has accepted the application for review.

Both stocks had already risen in the regular session, Amgen 5.5% and Bristol Myers 3.6%, before the 4:01 p.m. ET release, and were little changed after hours as of 4:45 p.m. ET. Still unknown: whether other Opdivo biosimilars get the same date, and when the FDA rules on ABP 206.

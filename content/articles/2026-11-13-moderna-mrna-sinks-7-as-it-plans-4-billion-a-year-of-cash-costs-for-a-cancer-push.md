Moderna ($MRNA) **fell 7.0%** to $231.35 on Thursday after it told investors at its analyst day that cash costs would be about $4 billion in 2027 and $4 billion to $4.5 billion a year through 2030, mainly to fund cancer drugs, the company said in its release, as TIKR and PharmExec reported. Its earlier plan had put 2027 cash costs at $3.5 billion to $3.9 billion.

The vaccine maker still aims to break even on cash in 2028 and expects to end this year with about $8.1 billion to $8.3 billion of cash and investments, its release said. It will give formal 2027 guidance with its fourth-quarter results early next year.

The drop follows a run few large stocks match: Moderna is **up 684.5%** this year in our data, after it [swung to a third-quarter profit and raised its sales outlook](/news/2026-11-06/moderna-mrna-swings-to-a-q3-profit-and-raises-its-sales-outlook/) on 6 November. Thursday's fall came on less than half its usual volume, and the stock was flat before Friday's open.

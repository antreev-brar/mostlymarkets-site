On Saturday, 14 November, two of Nvidia's ($NVDA) best-known investors, Altimeter Capital's Brad Gerstner and Atreides Management's Gavin Baker, urged its board to run a "slow, measured, and deliberate" search for a new chief executive and to weigh "the potential return of Jensen Huang," CNBC and Seeking Alpha reported. The two firms disclosed about **$2.1 billion** of Nvidia stock between them as of 30 June. They did not call for Huang to be reinstated now.

Huang resigned as CEO, president and director on Thursday, 12 November, Nvidia said in an SEC filing, and Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress is interim CEO. The stock closed at $162.85 on Friday, **31.9% below** its 6 October record close, so the letter puts public pressure on the board before it names anyone.

What's next: Nvidia reports its third quarter after the close on Tuesday, 17 November, its first report without Huang, when Kress and the board will face questions on the search.

Allstate ($ALL) said on Wednesday that **some access to its systems had been restricted over the last 48 hours**, but that it was still settling claims, Reuters reported. The insurer's website and app had been down since Monday, leaving customers and agents unable to file claims or pay bills in the run-up to Thanksgiving, Newsweek and PYMNTS reported.

Allstate had not said what caused the outage or when service would be fully restored as of Wednesday, according to Newsweek. That is the open question for shareholders: a technical fault costs days of service, while a security breach can bring notification costs, regulators and lawsuits, as the company found after a separate incident it confirmed this summer.

The stock has not reacted. Allstate **rose 0.4%** on Wednesday to $271.39 and is up 30.4% this year, near its high close of $275.11 in August. The next test is whether service is back by Friday, when markets reopen after Thanksgiving.

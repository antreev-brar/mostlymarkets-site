Agilent Technologies ($A), which makes laboratory instruments and supplies for drugmakers, food testers and chemical companies, reports fiscal fourth-quarter results after the close on Monday, 23 November. Its stock closed Friday at $170.38, **6.1% below** its 28 October high and up 25.2% this year, a rare holdout while the S&P 500 sits in a correction.

Last quarter, revenue rose 8.1% to $1.88 billion, or 7.3% on a core basis that strips out currency moves and deals, and adjusted earnings were $1.62 a share, including a 6-cent benefit from tariff refunds, the release said. Agilent raised its full-year adjusted earnings forecast to $6.18 to $6.21 a share, TradingView and Nasdaq's copy of the release showed, and the stock rose 1.7% the next day in our data.

## What analysts expect

Analysts expect adjusted earnings of **$1.72 a share**, up 8.2% from a year earlier, on revenue of $1.99 billion, up 6.7%, according to the Zacks consensus cited by TradingView and Yahoo Finance; MarketBeat's figure is $1.73. That sits near the top of Agilent's own forecast of $1.71 to $1.74 a share and $1.98 billion to $2.0 billion, which leaves out any further tariff refunds.

## What to watch

- **Any view of fiscal 2027.** The year starts in November, and at 26.0 times expected earnings in our data, against an 18.8 times median for health care stocks, the stock needs it to point to growth faster than its five-year pace of 5.4% a year.
- **Life sciences and diagnostics.** The unit grew 11% to $746 million last quarter, the release said, the fastest of its segments; drugmaker spending on instruments drives it.
- **CrossLab.** Services and consumables grew 6% to $786 million. These recurring sales steady the business when instrument orders slow. Options imply a move of about 10% either way, TipRanks reported, large for a stock that moved 5% or more on only 2 of the last 252 sessions.

## How the stock has done

Agilent is down 2.0% over the past month and up 18.0% over the past year. Its trailing P/E of 33.5 times is above its 29.1 times median since August 2024, so the market is paying up for its steadiness heading into the report.

## What happened

Shares of police body-camera and Taser maker Axon Enterprise ($AXON) **sank 16.2%** to $320.70 as of 10:15 a.m. ET on Friday, after the company missed third-quarter estimates on Thursday evening and cut its 2026 sales outlook because federal drone-defense deployments were delayed.

Revenue rose **28%** from a year earlier to $909 million and adjusted earnings were $1.72 a share, the company said in its results release. Both fell short of analysts' estimates, Investing.com and GuruFocus reported. Axon lowered its full-year revenue growth forecast after delays to federal deployments of Dedrone, its drone-detection business, Reuters and Dow Jones reported.

On the same evening Axon said it would invest $1.2 billion more in drone maker Skydio, raising its stake to 37%, according to the release; Reuters and Dow Jones reported the deal.

## Why it matters

Investors have paid up for Axon because it grows like a software company: body cameras, Tasers and drones are sold with multi-year cloud subscriptions, which makes its revenue unusually predictable. A cut blamed on the timing of a few federal contracts dents that reputation, and the Skydio deal asks shareholders to fund a bigger bet on police drones in the same breath.

The quarter itself was not weak: revenue still grew 28%. The open question is whether the delayed Dedrone deals land in the first half of 2027 or slip further.

## What the market is telling us

Axon moved 5% or more on 44 of the last 252 sessions, and the last drop this size was on 15 September, when it fell 9.8%. Before Friday the stock was down 32.6% this year and 40.8% below its 19 August closing high of $645.72. On Friday it traded as low as $309.45, below its 52-week closing low of $345.94 from 10 April.

Our read: a 16% drop for a delay, not a lost contract, reflects how much growth the price still assumed, about 36 times forward earnings in our quote data. Fourth-quarter results early next year will show whether the federal deals have closed.

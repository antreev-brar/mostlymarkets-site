Alibaba ($BABA) reports its September-quarter results before the US market opens on Thursday, 19 November. The question is whether its cloud business is growing fast enough to pay for its AI spending, after the shares **fell 22.3%** in a month, including a 7.1% drop on 13 November on fears that Washington will cut its access to advanced chips.

Last quarter, on 20 August, Alibaba said revenue grew 9% to 268.95 billion yuan and its cloud unit's revenue from outside customers grew 45%, its results release shows. But profit fell 75% as capital spending rose 75% to 67.7 billion yuan, Bloomberg and AP reported. The shares closed up 1.3% that day and **sank 8.6%** the next in our price data.

## What analysts expect

FactSet's consensus puts revenue at about 270.1 billion yuan, CME Elite Group reported, up about 9% from a year earlier; other trackers cited by Longbridge sit within 0.2% of that. Profit estimates have been cut in recent weeks while revenue estimates held steady, and we couldn't confirm a single earnings-per-share consensus across two outlets, so we leave it out.

## What to watch

- **Cloud growth.** Bank of America expects it to speed up to 52% from 45%, Investing.com reported, and Guosen forecasts 51%. Anything below 45% would undercut the reason investors tolerate the spending.
- **Capital spending.** It rose 75% last quarter; another jump with no sign of a peak would test investors' patience after the stock's fall.
- **Chips.** Any comment on how much of its AI computing it can secure from Nvidia ($NVDA) and other suppliers, after the 13 November slide ([our story](/news/2026-11-13/alibaba-baba-shares-slide-on-fears-over-its-access-to-nvidia-chips/)).

## How the stock has done

Alibaba closed at $84.37 on Wednesday, **down 42.4% this year** and 52.4% below its 22 January high of $177.18. Its Chinese peers have set a low bar: JD.com ($JD) [missed revenue estimates](/news/2026-11-17/jd-com-jd-misses-q3-revenue-estimates-at-309-6-billion-yuan-as-shoppers-hold-back/) as shoppers held back, while Baidu ($BIDU) [grew its AI cloud 94%](/news/2026-11-16/baidu-bidu-ai-cloud-revenue-jumps-94-as-online-ads-fall-22/). At about 10.6 times forward earnings, the price assumes the spending earns little back.

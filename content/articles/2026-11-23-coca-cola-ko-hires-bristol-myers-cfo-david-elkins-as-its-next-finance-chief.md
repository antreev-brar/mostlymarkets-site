Coca-Cola ($KO) said after the close on Monday that David Elkins, the chief financial officer of Bristol Myers Squibb ($BMY), will become its finance chief on **2 April 2027**, according to its release and Reuters. He replaces John Murphy, CFO since 2019, who stays on as president to hand over and plans to retire in 2027; both will report to Chief Executive Henrique Braun.

Elkins, 58, has run Bristol Myers' finances since 2019 and earlier worked at Johnson & Johnson and AstraZeneca, the release said. At Coca-Cola he will also oversee global strategy and the company's shared-services arm. Bristol Myers said Kevin Dillon, its head of corporate finance and a former Pfizer executive, will succeed him on **1 March 2027**, that the departure is unrelated to its results, and that it reaffirmed its 2026 guidance.

Elkins takes over the finances of a $412 billion company whose shares are up 37% this year, near their 52-week high, so the bar is continuity rather than repair. Coca-Cola's shares were up 0.1% after hours as of 5:12 p.m. ET; Bristol Myers was down 0.6%. Coca-Cola has not yet set Murphy's retirement date.

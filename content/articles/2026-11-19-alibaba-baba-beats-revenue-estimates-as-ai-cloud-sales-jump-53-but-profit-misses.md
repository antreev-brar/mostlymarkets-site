Alibaba ($BABA) said before the US open on Thursday that September-quarter revenue **rose 10%** to 271.52 billion yuan, above the 269.81 billion analysts expected, according to LSEG data cited by Reuters. Revenue from AI Cloud and Compute Services rose 53% to 60.97 billion yuan, its release and Investing.com said, a touch above the 52% Bank of America had forecast ([our preview](/news/2026-11-18/what-to-expect-from-alibabas-baba-september-quarter-earnings/)).

Profit is the catch. Adjusted EPS of 8.10 yuan missed the 10.75 yuan expected, Investing.com reported, while investment gains tripled GAAP net income to 64.42 billion yuan. Free cash flow was an outflow of **34.47 billion yuan**, against 21.84 billion a year earlier, the release shows. The company said it is "targeting to achieve positive free cash flow" as AI sales ramp, but gave no date.

The shares were down 0.8% at $83.68 at 6:15 a.m. ET in our data, after a 42.4% fall this year that, as we argued in the preview, already assumed the spending would earn little back. What to watch: whether management puts a date on positive cash flow, and what it says about access to Nvidia ($NVDA) chips after the [13 November slide](/news/2026-11-13/alibaba-baba-shares-slide-on-fears-over-its-access-to-nvidia-chips/).

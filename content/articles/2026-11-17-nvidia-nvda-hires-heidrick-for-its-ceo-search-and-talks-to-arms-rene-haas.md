Nvidia ($NVDA) has hired executive search firm Heidrick & Struggles to find its next chief executive, and the firm has begun talking to outside candidates including Arm ($ARM) Chief Executive Rene Haas, the Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday, as Yahoo Finance and The Fly relayed. The Financial Times had earlier reported that Nvidia's board approached Haas. Haas ran Nvidia's computing products business until 2013 and has led Arm since 2022.

Arm **rose 4.6%** by 2:15 p.m. ET, recovering part of Monday's 8.1% drop, while Nvidia was down 1.0%. The report lands hours after former CEO Jensen Huang said he would "be honored" to return ([our story](/news/2026-11-17/jensen-huang-says-he-would-be-honored-to-return-as-nvidia-nvda-ceo/)), so the board is running a search while its founder says he is open to coming back.

What's next: Nvidia reports third-quarter results after the close tonight, and interim Chief Executive Colette Kress takes questions on the 5 p.m. ET call ([our preview](/news/2026-11-16/what-to-expect-from-nvidias-nvda-q3-earnings/)).

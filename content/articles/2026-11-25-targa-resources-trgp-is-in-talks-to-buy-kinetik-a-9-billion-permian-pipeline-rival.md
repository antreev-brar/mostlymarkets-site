Targa Resources ($TRGP) is in talks to buy Kinetik Holdings, a Blackstone-backed pipeline operator with a market value of about **$8.9 billion**, Bloomberg reported after the close on Wednesday, citing people familiar with the matter. A deal could come as soon as next week, though no decision has been made and Kinetik could stay independent, according to the report, which Reuters carried. Kinetik shares jumped about 11.5% after hours, Investing.com reported.

Both companies gather and process natural gas in the Permian Basin of West Texas and New Mexico, so a deal would fold a direct rival's network into Targa's in the same basin, Bloomberg reported. At about $61 billion in market value in our data, Targa would be buying a company roughly a seventh its size. Its shares closed up 0.8% at $285.53, up 54.8% this year, and were little changed after hours.

The price and how Targa would pay for it, in stock, debt or cash, aren't known yet, and the report said another bidder could still emerge.

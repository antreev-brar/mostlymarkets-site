The board of Nvidia ($NVDA) has approached Rene Haas, chief executive of Arm Holdings ($ARM), as a possible successor to Jensen Huang, the Financial Times reported on Friday, in a story Reuters relayed. Haas is one of several candidates, and there is no guarantee he will be offered the job or take it, the report said; Reuters said it could not immediately verify it.

Haas knows the company: before joining Arm in 2013 he spent seven years at Nvidia, rising to run its computing products business, and he has led Arm since 2022. The report came a day after Huang quit and finance chief Colette Kress became interim CEO ([our story](/news/2026-11-13/nvidia-nvda-shares-slide-after-jensen-huang-resigns-as-ceo/)). At 2:15 p.m. ET, Nvidia was **down 2.2%** and Arm down 4.8%.

Kress reports Nvidia's third-quarter results on Tuesday 17 November after the close, the first test of orders without Huang ([what Wall Street is betting on](/news/2026-11-13/wall-street-is-betting-nvidias-loss-is-amd-and-broadcoms-gain-tuesday-will-test-it/)).

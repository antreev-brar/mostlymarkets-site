## What happened
Shares of laser makers Lumentum ($LITE) and Coherent ($COHR) **sank 8.1%** by 10:15 a.m. ET on Monday, and data-center network switch maker Arista Networks ($ANET) fell 5.8%. The optical stocks were lower from before the open, TokenPost reported, and TIKR tied the fall to the turmoil at Nvidia ($NVDA), whose results come after the close on Tuesday; Reuters and CNBC said the same worry was pulling the whole Nasdaq lower.

None of the three had news of its own. Two Lumentum partners fell with them: Marvell Technology ($MRVL), which is buying Lumentum, was down 7.0%, and cable-chip maker Credo ($CRDO) 7.6%. Power-chip maker Monolithic Power ($MPWR) fell 6.7%. Research firm SemiAnalysis also cut its forecast for AI chip spending in 2028 by 28%, Investing.com and Futu News reported, a number that carries straight through to the lasers and switches that link those chips.

## Why it matters
These companies sell the plumbing between AI chips: every rack of Nvidia processors needs switches, lasers and cables to talk to the next, so their orders track AI chip orders closely. When investors doubt the chip spending, they sell the plumbing harder, because it is priced for growth. Lumentum's price now also hangs on Marvell's: each Lumentum share becomes 4.5 Marvell shares under the deal signed on 2 November. At 10:15 a.m., that was worth $656.37, so Lumentum traded **16.5% below** the value of the offer, against about 5% on 30 October, a sign investors are pricing in more risk that the deal changes or fails before it closes in 2028.

## What the market is telling us
Arista had been the exception: it closed Friday 9.0% below its 9 October record and up 6.3% over the past month, while Lumentum was down 31.4% over the month and Coherent 13.0%. Lumentum's shares fell 18.7% on 5 November, the last drop bigger than Monday's, and Coherent sits 48.9% below its June high. Monday says even the AI hardware stock that had held up is now trading on Nvidia's numbers; Tuesday's guidance for data-center sales will decide whether the selling was early or right.

## What happened
Shares of Hollywood studio and TV group Skydance ($SKYD) **rose 5.4%** to $6.54 by 10:30 a.m. ET on Tuesday after UBS upgraded the stock to neutral from sell. UBS cited Skydance's deal for Warner Bros. and cut its price target to $7 from $8, The Fly and GuruFocus reported.

The stock was also helped by filings showing director Gerry Cardinale, who runs investment firm RedBird Capital Partners and whom Stocktwits called the architect of the Warner Bros. deal, bought more shares on 19 and 20 November, Stocktwits and MarketBeat reported. Yahoo Finance reported he had also bought about $1 million of stock between 11 and 13 November.

## Why it matters
An upgrade paired with a lower target is a call that the selling has gone far enough, not a call for a rebound: UBS's $7 target sits only about 7% above Tuesday's price. Cardinale's buys are small beside the stake RedBird already holds, so they signal his view more than they move the share count. The stock's biggest question is unchanged: whether the Warner Bros. combination pays for itself.

The next test is Skydance's 2027 outlook, which Yahoo Finance said will come with its fourth-quarter results; no date has been set.

## What the market is telling us
Skydance's shares moved 5% or more on 22 of the last 252 sessions; the last gain this big was on 9 November, when the stock rose 8.6%. It closed Monday down 42.4% this year at $6.33, 51.8% below its 52-week closing high of $13.13 from 28 November 2025, and 18.5% lower over the past month. We read Tuesday as a floor being tested rather than a turn: the analyst who moved still sees little upside.

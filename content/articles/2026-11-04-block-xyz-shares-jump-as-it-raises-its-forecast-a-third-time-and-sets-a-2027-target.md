Block ($XYZ) reported third-quarter gross profit of **$3.24 billion** after the close on Tuesday, up 22% from a year earlier and above the $3.16 billion analysts expected, Seeking Alpha reported. Adjusted earnings of $1.13 a share beat the $1.02 Zacks consensus, and revenue of $6.72 billion topped the $6.62 billion Zacks expected. The shares, which closed down 1.4% at $76.00, were **up 8.6%** at $82.54 before the open on Wednesday, by 5:56 a.m. ET in our data.

## What stood out

Cash App did the heavy lifting. Its gross profit rose **27%**, driven by Cash App Borrow, its small-loan product, the Afterpay buy-now-pay-later business and the Cash App Card, according to Block's quarterly filing and CryptoBriefing. Square, the merchant side, grew 14%, while gross profit from bitcoin services halved to $52 million on lower fees.

Profit grew faster than sales. Adjusted operating income nearly doubled to a record **$923 million**, SiliconANGLE and BigGo reported, a 29% margin on gross profit. On a GAAP basis, net income rose 23% to $566 million, or $0.92 a share, according to its filing, a figure GuruFocus said fell short of estimates on that basis.

## The outlook

This is the third straight quarter Block has raised its 2026 forecast, SiliconANGLE reported: it now expects gross profit of $12.63 billion and adjusted earnings of $4.15 a share, according to GuruFocus and CoinCentral. For 2027 it guided adjusted operating income to **$4.58 billion**, well above the $3.51 billion target it set at its investor day in November 2025, SiliconANGLE reported.

## How the stock has done

Block was up 16.8% this year at Tuesday's close and 10.4% below its 52-week closing high of $84.85 from 27 August, in our data. At $82.54 before the open it would be within 3% of that high. The longer view is less kind: $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $301 today, a reminder of how far the 2021 fintech boom ran ahead of the profits.

## Our take

The quarter showed Block's bet on lending through Cash App turning into profit: operating income nearly doubled on 22% gross-profit growth. At 16.6 times forward earnings in our data, against a peer median of 17.2, the stock wasn't pricing in a 30% profit jump next year. What to watch: whether Cash App's 60 million users keep growing, and loss rates on Borrow as the lending book gets bigger.

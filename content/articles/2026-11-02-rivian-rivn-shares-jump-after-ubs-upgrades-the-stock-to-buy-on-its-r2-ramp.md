## What happened

Shares of electric-vehicle maker Rivian ($RIVN) **jumped 9.4%** to $13.31 as of 10:15 a.m. ET on Monday after UBS upgraded the stock to Buy from Neutral with a $17 price target. Analyst Joseph Spak called the stock's recent slide a buying opportunity ahead of the ramp-up of the R2, Rivian's cheaper mid-size SUV, Investing.com, TipRanks and Stocktwits reported.

Spak's case rests on margins. UBS expects Rivian to leave 2026 with a positive automotive gross margin, the profit on each car sold before overhead, and sees that measure turning positive in the first quarter of 2027 as R2 output rises, Investing.com reported. He also sees little risk that Rivian needs to raise more money, putting its available and targeted future capital at $13 billion. The stock had fallen 19% in the month before the call.

## Why it matters

The R2 is the car that has to make Rivian a volume carmaker: it is priced below its R1 trucks and is built to sell in larger numbers, and its cost per car will decide whether Rivian ever earns money on what it sells. UBS is the second broker in a week to turn positive, after Jefferies upgraded the stock to Buy with a $17 target the week before, TipRanks and Electric Vehicles reported. Spak still warned that the stock "is and will likely remain a volatile stock".

Other EV makers rose with it: Lucid ($LCID) was up 5.5% and Tesla ($TSLA) 1.8% at 10:15 a.m.

## What the market is telling us

Rivian's shares moved 5% or more on 41 of the last 252 sessions, so swings this size are routine. Through Friday the stock was down 38.3% this year and 45.8% below its 52-week closing high of $22.45 from 19 December 2025.

Even after Monday's jump, the stock would need to rise another 28% to reach UBS's target. The upgrade is a bet on one number, automotive gross margin, and Rivian's fourth-quarter report will be the first chance to see it turn positive.

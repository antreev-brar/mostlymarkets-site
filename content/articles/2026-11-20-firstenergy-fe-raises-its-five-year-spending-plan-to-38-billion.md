FirstEnergy ($FE) said on Friday that it will spend **$38 billion** on its grid from 2026 to 2030, up from the $36 billion it set out with its fourth-quarter results in February, Reuters and TipRanks reported. The Ohio-based utility also raised its target for annual growth in core earnings per share to 7% to 9%, from near the top of 6% to 8%.

For a regulated utility, a bigger spending plan usually means bigger profits: regulators let it earn a set return on what it invests in its network, and more than half of the February plan, over $19 billion, was for transmission lines. The February plan, already 30% larger than the one before it, was built to grow that investment base by about 10% a year through 2030, Utility Dive reported.

The shares were up 0.3% at $47.28 in premarket trading as of 9:03 a.m. ET, 9.2% below their 52-week high. The open question is the bill: how much of the extra $2 billion is paid for with new shares, and whether state regulators approve the rate increases that recover it.

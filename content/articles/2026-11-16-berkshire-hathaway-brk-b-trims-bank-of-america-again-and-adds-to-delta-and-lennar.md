Berkshire Hathaway ($BRK.B) cut its stake in Bank of America ($BAC) by about 6% to **455.9 million shares** in the third quarter, its 13F filing of US stock holdings showed after the close on Monday, according to CNBC and Reuters. It also sold 62% of its Capital One ($COF) shares, leaving 1.15 million, and raised its Delta Air Lines ($DAL) stake by about 12% to roughly 64.5 million shares.

In housing, Berkshire nearly doubled its Lennar ($LEN) holding to 26.0 million Class A shares, Benzinga and 24/7 Wall St. reported, and it disclosed a new stake in Bending Spoons, the owner of AOL. Bank of America remains one of its largest holdings, but the filing extends a run of sales in the bank's shares since 2024, and Bank of America closed down 1.8% at $50.08 on Monday before the filing came out.

The filing shows positions at 30 September, so it says nothing about trades since then.

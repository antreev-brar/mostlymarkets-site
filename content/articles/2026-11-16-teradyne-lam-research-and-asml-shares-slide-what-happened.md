## What happened
Shares of chip-testing equipment maker Teradyne ($TER) **sank 6.5%** by 10:15 a.m. ET on Monday, with etching-tool maker Lam Research ($LRCX) down 5.7%, inspection specialist KLA ($KLAC) 5.5% and lithography maker ASML ($ASML) 4.6%. Research firm SemiAnalysis cut its forecast for the silicon that goes into AI chips, Futu News and PANews reported, as the sell-off in AI stocks resumed a day before Nvidia ($NVDA) reports, Reuters and CNBC reported.

SemiAnalysis lowered its forecast for the total wafer area of AI accelerator chips shipped from 2026 to 2030 by **17%**, and cut its forecast for AI accelerator spending in 2028 by 28%, citing tighter capital markets and slower expansion at the frontier AI labs, Futu News, PANews and Investing.com reported. Both said Nvidia takes the biggest share of the cut. Applied Materials ($AMAT) fell 4.5% by the same time.

## Why it matters
Equipment makers sell the machines that turn wafers into chips, so a forecast of fewer AI chip wafers is a forecast of fewer tools for the factories that make them. The cut lands three days after Applied Materials [beat its estimates](/news/2026-11-13/applied-materials-amat-tops-q4-estimates-as-memory-chipmakers-spend-more/) on memory makers' spending, which was the evidence the bulls leaned on. SemiAnalysis is one research firm, not a customer: the orders that count come from TSMC, Samsung and the memory makers.

## What the market is telling us
ASML had been the steady one: it closed Friday 24.9% below its 30 June record, while Teradyne, Lam and KLA were each 48% to 50% below theirs. Teradyne's shares moved 5% or more on 68 of the last 252 sessions, and its last drop bigger than Monday's was on 5 November, when it fell 15.2%. Nvidia's report after the close on Tuesday 17 November is the next read on AI chip demand; until a chipmaker cuts its own spending plan, the forecast is a warning, not an order cancelled.

Amgen ($AMGN) reports third-quarter results after the close on Tuesday, 3 November, a session after its shares closed at $465.45, **0.1% below** the 52-week closing high they set on 30 October. The stock is up 42.2% this year and 15.5% in the past month.

Last quarter, reported on 4 August, revenue rose 10% to $10.1 billion and adjusted EPS of $6.29 beat the $5.60 consensus, the release and Barchart show. Repatha, its cholesterol drug, grew 37% to $953 million, and Evenity, for osteoporosis, grew 38% to $714 million. Amgen raised its 2026 forecasts, and the shares closed up 4.6% the next day.

## What analysts expect

Analysts expect adjusted EPS of about **$5.77 to $5.79**, against $5.64 a year earlier, on revenue of $10.05 billion to $10.08 billion, up about 5%, according to the consensus figures cited by Yahoo Finance and AlphaStreet.

## What to watch

- **Spending.** 24/7 Wall St. flags rising expenses as the main risk to profit, which management frames as investment in MariTide, its experimental obesity drug, and other late-stage programs. A cost guide above plan would test the margin story.
- **Repatha and the growth brands.** Repatha's new prescriptions rose more than 50% in the US last quarter, management said on the call; another quarter near 37% growth would keep it as the main engine.
- **The 2026 range.** Guidance is $22.30 to $23.50 of adjusted EPS on $38.2 billion to $39.4 billion of revenue; a move to the top would back up a stock trading at its high.

## How the stock has done

Amgen moved 5% or more on only 3 of the last 252 sessions, and $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $2,172 today. With the shares at a 52-week high going in, the guide carries more weight than the beat.

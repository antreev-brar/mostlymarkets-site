Anthropic plans to price its initial public offering above the **$70 to $78** range it set on 10 November after strong demand, the Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday, and people familiar with the deal told CNBC the same. The company was due to close its order books at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, CNBC reported. The top of the range would have raised **$64.4 billion** from 825 million shares and valued Anthropic at about $1.404 trillion, according to TechCrunch and Yahoo Finance.

Every dollar above $78 adds about $825 million to the raise, by our arithmetic. The deal is the test of whether public investors will still pay up for AI after a month in which Nvidia ($NVDA) fell to a one-year low, which is why we called it the story Wall Street is betting on ([our narrative](/news/2026-11-15/wall-street-is-betting-anthropics-ipo-can-rescue-the-ai-trade-3-numbers-will-decide-it/)). Alphabet ($GOOGL), an investor and cloud supplier, was down 0.8% at 12:15 p.m. ET.

What's next: the final price is due on Wednesday, with trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday 19 November, CNBC reported.

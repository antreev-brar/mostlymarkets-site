Tesla ($TSLA) Chief Executive Elon Musk said on X on Saturday that Nvidia ($NVDA) will lose some "critical" deals without Jensen Huang, because many of the supply arrangements Huang ran were a handshake with him rather than a written contract, Reuters and Calcalist reported. Musk named no deal and gave no evidence that any had been cancelled; Nvidia declined to comment, and its board has said customer commitments and its product roadmap will continue without interruption, the reports said.

It matters because the bet behind last week's rally in AMD ($AMD) and Broadcom ($AVGO) is that Nvidia's customers and suppliers drift away after Huang's exit ([our narrative](/news/2026-11-13/wall-street-is-betting-nvidias-loss-is-amd-and-broadcoms-gain-tuesday-will-test-it/)). Musk also said he would sign a petition to bring Huang back, the same day two large Nvidia investors asked the board to keep that option open ([our brief](/news/2026-11-14/two-nvidia-nvda-investors-ask-its-board-to-keep-jensen-huangs-return-on-the-table/)).

Nvidia reports its third quarter after the close on Tuesday, 17 November; its fourth-quarter revenue forecast is the first number that can show whether any orders have moved.

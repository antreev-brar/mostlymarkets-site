Tesla ($TSLA) sold **67,829** cars made at its Shanghai factory in October, including exports, up 10.3% from a year earlier, the China Passenger Car Association said on Tuesday, Reuters and CnEVPost reported. That was down 28.9% from 95,366 in September, and the lowest monthly total since February, CnEVPost noted.

October was the twelfth straight month of year-on-year growth, and the gain was faster than September's 5%. But China's whole market for electric and plug-in hybrid cars grew about 12% from a year earlier to roughly 1.8 million, CnEVPost reported, so Tesla's Shanghai output grew more slowly than the market around it, even as it had [its best October on record in Norway](/daily/2026-11-02/). Shanghai's first ten months came to 810,889 cars, up 21.4%.

The shares were down 0.4% at $402.36 before the open as of 6:12 a.m. ET, 10.2% lower this year. The CPCA's breakdown of Tesla's Chinese sales and its exports will show whether the October dip came from home demand or shipping timing.

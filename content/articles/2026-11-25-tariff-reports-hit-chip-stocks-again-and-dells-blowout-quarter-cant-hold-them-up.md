The AI hardware trade has now given back most of Monday's record rally in a day and a half. On Tuesday the chip stocks that led Monday fell hard, Nvidia ($NVDA) by 5.9% and Analog Devices ($ADI) by 17.4%, while the Dow rose 0.9% to 50,018.70 on health care and shoppers' stocks. After the close, Dell ($DELL) doubled its revenue and raised its forecast by $10 billion.

Overnight a new worry arrived on top. Reuters and the Wall Street Journal reported that the administration is preparing tariffs on 16 trading partners, Taiwan and South Korea among them, and Nasdaq 100 futures **fell 2.2%**. As of 6:16 a.m. ET in our data, Dell is down 8.0% and Nvidia 4.6%, with October PCE inflation due at 8:30 a.m. and a December rate hike almost fully priced.

## Yesterday and overnight in eight takes

### Reports of new tariffs knocked 2.2% off Nasdaq futures

The administration could announce new duties as soon as this week after a probe into excess factory capacity, Reuters reported, and the Journal said tariffs on China and dozens of other countries would rise. The duties would partly replace the global tariffs the Supreme Court struck down on 20 February, Reuters and Sourcing Journal reported. Taiwan and Korea make most of the chips and memory inside AI servers, which is why the chip stocks took the hit.

## What's next

All times ET. Markets close on Thursday, 26 November, for Thanksgiving and at 1 p.m. on Friday.

- **Wednesday, 25 November, before the open:** Deere ($DE) reported fourth-quarter net income of **$3.89 a share**, below the $4.07 Zacks expected, on sales down 2% to $12.1 billion, and forecast fiscal 2027 net income of $4.50 billion to $5.25 billion, its release said. The stock is down 3.9% before the open ([our preview](/news/2026-11-24/deere-de-reports-earnings-tomorrow-what-to-watch/); recap to follow).
- **Wednesday, 8:30 a.m.:** October PCE inflation. RBC expects headline and core prices up 0.3% on the month, taking the yearly rates to 3.8% and 3.3%, Coinotag and CoinCentral reported. Fed funds futures price a December hike at about 95% to near 100%.
- **This week:** the tariff announcement could come at any time, Reuters reported. Black Friday trading is a half day.
- **Later:** OPEC+ meets on 29 November, Huang's House interview is on 1 December and the Fed decides on 9 December.

We think the tariff report matters more for chips than PCE: Dell just showed AI demand and margins holding, yet the AI hardware stocks are falling for a second session, which says the market is pricing risk to the supply chain in Taiwan and Korea, not weaker orders.

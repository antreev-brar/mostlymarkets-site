TJX Companies ($TJX), the owner of T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods, reports its fiscal third quarter, the three months to 31 October, before the open on Wednesday, 18 November, its Business Wire notice said. The stock closed at $129.85 on Tuesday, **down 13.9%** from its close the day before its August report, in our data.

In August, comparable sales, those at stores open a year or more, **rose 4%**, above TJX's plan of 2% to 3%, and adjusted EPS rose 11% to $1.22, excluding tariff refunds, Just Style and Zacks reported. But Marmaxx, the T.J. Maxx and Marshalls division and its biggest, grew comparable sales just 1%, down from 6% the quarter before, and the third-quarter forecast came in below estimates, Reuters reported. The stock fell 4.2% that day in our data, and kept falling to a 52-week closing low of $122.84 on 16 September.

## What analysts expect

The Zacks consensus is **$1.33 a share**, up 3.9% from $1.28 a year earlier, on revenue of $15.81 billion, up 4.6%, Zacks and Börse Express reported; Nasdaq's data also shows $1.33. TJX guided to $1.36 to $1.38 including about 6 cents of expected tariff refunds, or $1.30 to $1.32 without them, so the basis matters: the consensus sits above the top of the adjusted range. It planned comparable sales up 2% to 3%.

The debate this report settles: **was Marmaxx's slowdown a fixable stumble?** Chief Executive Ernie Herrman said on the August call that the team was "convinced that the issues were self-inflicted and within our control," Reuters and TIKR reported, while Reuters also pointed to competition from Ross Stores and Burlington.

## What to watch

- **Marmaxx comparable sales.** Herrman expected a recovery by the holiday season; a figure back near 2% to 3% would back his diagnosis, another 1% would not.
- **Adjusted EPS against $1.32.** Beating the top of its own adjusted range matters more than a beat padded by refunds.
- **The full-year forecast.** TJX raised it in August to $5.31 to $5.36 a share, or $5.15 to $5.20 without refunds, and held comparable-sales growth at 3% to 4%.

## How the stock has done

TJX is down 15.5% this year and 11.9% over the past month, and sits 22.9% below its closing high of $168.41 from 12 June. Morgan Stanley and UBS both cut their price targets in the past week, to $166 and $184, while keeping buy-equivalent ratings, TradingView and GuruFocus reported. At 24.0 times forward earnings, it trades above the 19.7 times median of its peers in our data but below its own two-year median trailing multiple of 30.0 times.

## What happened
Shares of AI chipmaker Nvidia ($NVDA) **fell 7.4%** to $152.89 as of 2:15 p.m. ET on Tuesday, and memory maker Micron Technology ($MU) slid 10.9% to $630.83, a day after Nvidia's record one-day gain in market value when it reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive. The rest of the group fell with them: Marvell ($MRVL) was down 8.3%, AMD ($AMD) 8.3% and Arm ($ARM) 7.7%.

There was no new company news. CNBC and Investing.com described the move as the Huang comeback rally cooling, after Nvidia closed Monday up 31.5% at $165.05, adding more than $1 trillion in market value. Reuters, as cited by Archyde, put the fall down to profit-taking after that record day, weakness across chip stocks including Micron and Marvell, and renewed doubts about the returns on AI data-centre spending.

## Why it matters
Huang's return settled who runs Nvidia, not the questions that sank it. The SEC's inquiry, which Reuters reported on Friday now covers potential accounting issues, is still open ([our story on Nvidia's books](/news/2026-11-23/nvidias-crisis-has-moved-from-its-boss-to-its-books-what-the-evidence-says/)).

Micron is tied to the same question: whether the money Nvidia lends or guarantees to customers has been propping up memory orders. It rose 19.0% on Monday after Citi sized that risk at about 12% of DRAM demand ([our story](/news/2026-11-23/micron-mu-jumps-19-as-citi-sizes-the-nvidia-financing-risk-at-12-of-dram-demand/)); Tuesday's fall takes back more than half of that gain.

## What the market is telling us
Nvidia's shares have moved 5% or more on 11 of the last 252 sessions. At Monday's close of $165.05 the stock was down 11.5% this year and 31.0% below its 52-week closing high of $239.24 from 6 October; $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $5,199. Micron, which moves 5% or more far more often (77 of the last 252 sessions), was still up 148.0% this year at Monday's close.

We read Tuesday as the market pricing the probes back in after a day that priced only the chief executive. The next hard numbers are Micron's results on 17 December ([the memory-boom story](/news/2026-11-21/wall-street-is-betting-the-memory-boom-has-peaked-microns-17-december-report-will-test-it/)).

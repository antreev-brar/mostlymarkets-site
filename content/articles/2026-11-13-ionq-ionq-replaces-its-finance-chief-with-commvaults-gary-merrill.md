IonQ ($IONQ) said at 9 a.m. ET on Friday that Inder Singh, its chief financial officer and chief operating officer, is stepping down from his roles effective immediately, according to the company's release, as carried by StockTitan and Rallies. Gary Merrill, currently finance chief of software company Commvault, will become CFO in early December, and Thomas Austin, formerly chief accounting officer at Juniper Networks, starts now in a newly created chief accounting officer role.

Singh, a former Arm finance chief, took both jobs in September 2025, when he replaced Thomas Kramer, so IonQ will have had three finance chiefs in about 15 months. The change comes after a hard stretch for the stock: it **fell 9.4%** on Thursday to $30.86, is down 31.2% this year, and sits 57.2% below its 29 May closing high, in our data. The company framed the moves as preparing it for growth and progress toward profitability.

Merrill's first results as finance chief will be IonQ's fourth-quarter report. The shares were down 1.4% at $30.44 before the open, as of 9:15 a.m. ET.

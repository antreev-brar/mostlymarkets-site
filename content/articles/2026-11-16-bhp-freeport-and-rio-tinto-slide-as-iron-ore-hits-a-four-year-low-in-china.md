Freeport-McMoRan ($FCX) **fell 6.8%** on Monday, BHP Group ($BHP) 5.0% and Rio Tinto ($RIO) 3.5%, in our data, as iron ore and copper prices fell together. Iron ore futures in Dalian, China, dropped as much as 2.5% to 653 yuan a ton, the lowest since November 2022, on a weak winter outlook for steel demand, Bloomberg and the Motley Fool reported. Copper fell about 1.3% to near $6.00 a pound, Trading Economics and Market Index reported, after hitting its lowest in almost four months on Friday, ABC reported.

It was BHP's biggest one-day fall since 10 September. The stock is still up 25.1% this year but 23.4% below its 25 August high. A stronger dollar, at a 19-month high on bets the Fed raises rates, makes metals priced in dollars dearer for buyers abroad, TradingKey and Reuters noted.

The next read on Chinese demand is Thursday's meeting between President Trump and President Xi in Shenzhen ([our week ahead](/news/2026-11-15/the-week-ahead-nvidia-anthropics-ipo-the-fed-minutes-and-walmart/)).

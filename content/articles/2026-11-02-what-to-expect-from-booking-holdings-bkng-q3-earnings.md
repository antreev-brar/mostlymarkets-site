Booking Holdings ($BKNG), the owner of Booking.com, Priceline and Kayak, reports third-quarter results after the close on Tuesday, 3 November. Its shares **jumped 3.8%** on Monday as travel stocks rallied, IBTimes reported, but they are still down 28.2% this year and 30.0% below their January high.

Last quarter, reported on 4 August, room nights rose 5% to 325 million, gross bookings rose 9% and revenue grew 8%, the release shows, beating forecasts. The shares closed up 6.6% the next day, but Booking guided third-quarter room nights up only 3% to 5%, down from 8% growth a year earlier, Zacks noted. The prices here reflect the 25-for-1 stock split of 2 April.

## What analysts expect

Analysts expect EPS of about **$4.49** and revenue of $9.54 billion to $9.55 billion, up about 6% year on year, according to Zacks and MarketBeat. That sits at the top of Booking's own guide for revenue, gross bookings and adjusted EBITDA each to grow 4% to 6%.

## What to watch

- **Room nights.** The 3% to 5% guide compares with 5% last quarter; Zacks expects the Middle East conflict to have weighed on room nights and flight bookings in the quarter.
- **Fourth-quarter guide.** With oil falling below $100 over the weekend, as our [Monday Daily](/daily/2026-11-02/) covered, a steadier outlook for travel demand would be the first sign the Middle East drag is fading.
- **Margins.** Adjusted EBITDA growth in line with revenue would show Booking is holding costs while bookings slow.

## How the stock has done

Booking moved 5% or more on 14 of the last 252 sessions, and $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $1,566 today. Our [list of beaten-down quality stocks](/news/2026-10-28/3-beaten-down-quality-stocks-we-love-and-1-we-hate/) from 28 October looked at the business behind the slide.

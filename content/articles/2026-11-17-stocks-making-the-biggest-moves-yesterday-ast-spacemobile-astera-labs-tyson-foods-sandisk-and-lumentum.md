The biggest moves among companies worth $10 billion or more on Monday 16 November were almost all drops, measured close to close, and most came from the same AI hardware sell-off a day before Nvidia ($NVDA) reports. For Astera Labs, Sandisk and Lumentum it was the biggest one-day fall since 5 November, in our data. The one big gain came from a meat packer.

**AST SpaceMobile ($ASTS)**, -12.3%: the satellite-to-phone company fell to a 52-week low of $28.58 after United Launch Alliance's Vulcan rocket failed on Sunday, losing 40 Amazon satellites and dragging space stocks lower, Spaceflight Now and Benzinga reported. The stock is down 60.6% this year. [Full story](/news/2026-11-16/3-cash-machine-stocks-we-like-and-1-wed-skip-cboe-newmont-ameriprise-and-ast-spacemobile/)

**Astera Labs ($ALAB)**, -8.8%: the AI connectivity chipmaker fell with the rest of AI hardware as worries about Nvidia's leadership weighed on the Nasdaq, Reuters and CNBC reported, and research firm SemiAnalysis cut its forecast for AI chip spending, Investing.com and Futu News reported. It closed at $202.34, 58.1% below its June high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-17/2-fast-grower-stocks-we-like-and-1-wed-skip-credo-astera-labs-and-ionq/)

**Tyson Foods ($TSN)**, +8.7%: the meat company had its biggest gain since February 2022 even though its adjusted fiscal fourth-quarter earnings of $0.61 a share missed estimates, as investors looked past the quarter to its fiscal 2027 outlook, Investing.com and Finwire reported. [Full story](/news/2026-11-16/tyson-foods-tsn-misses-q4-profit-estimates-as-beef-losses-deepen/)

**Sandisk ($SNDK)**, -8.6%: the flash-memory maker fell in the same AI hardware sell-off, with hard-drive makers Seagate and Western Digital close behind, Reuters and Investing.com reported. It closed at $809.11, still up 240.8% this year but 65.3% below its June high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-16/sandisk-seagate-and-western-digital-shares-slide-what-happened/)

**Lumentum ($LITE)**, -8.5%: the laser maker, which Marvell Technology agreed to buy on 2 November, fell with other optical-networking stocks on the Nvidia turmoil, TIKR and Reuters reported, with no news of its own. At $546.00 it is 51.8% below its 6 October high. [Full story](/news/2026-11-16/lumentum-coherent-and-arista-shares-slide-what-happened/)

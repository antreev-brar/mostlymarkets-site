PDD Holdings ($PDD), the owner of the Pinduoduo app in China and Temu abroad, said before the US open on Thursday that third-quarter revenue was **flat at 108.3 billion yuan**, short of the 115.67 billion in a FactSet poll cited by the Wall Street Journal. Adjusted net income of 31 billion yuan beat the 28.03 billion analysts expected on average, Reuters reported, because PDD cut its marketing spending. The shares **rose 4.4% to $80.82** in premarket trading as of 9:14 a.m. ET.

## What stood out

The two halves of the business went opposite ways. Online marketing services, the fees merchants pay for placement, **rose 12%** to 59.8 billion yuan, while transaction services, which include Temu's cross-border sales, fell 12% to 48.5 billion yuan, the release shows and Quartz and Retail-News reported. Total revenue growth slowed from 8% in the second quarter to nothing.

Profit held up because PDD spent less to get it. Sales and marketing expenses fell about 17% to 25.3 billion yuan, Reuters reported, and GAAP net income edged up to 29.5 billion yuan from 29.3 billion. Operating profit was 31.2 billion yuan, according to the release as summarized by Quiver Quantitative and Investing.com.

## The outlook

PDD doesn't give guidance, and on the call Co-Chief Executive Zhao Jiazhen said the company would keep deepening its support for merchants, Investing.com and Blockonomi reported. It didn't size what that support will cost next quarter. This was also Temu's first full quarter under the European Union's 3-euro duty on small parcels, which [we flagged in our preview](/news/2026-11-18/what-to-expect-from-pdd-holdings-pdd-q3-earnings/) as the cost to watch.

## Our take

Investors chose profit over growth, and at 6.8 times forward earnings against a peer median of 19.3 times, that is understandable: the stock is down 31.7% this year and already priced for shrinking profits. But a 12% fall in transaction services is the weak link, and a profit beat built on marketing cuts can be repeated only so many times. The next quarter's revenue line, against JD.com ($JD), which [missed estimates this week](/news/2026-11-17/jd-com-jd-misses-q3-revenue-estimates-at-309-6-billion-yuan-as-shoppers-hold-back/), and Alibaba ($BABA), which [beat on sales](/news/2026-11-19/alibaba-baba-beats-revenue-estimates-as-ai-cloud-sales-jump-53-but-profit-misses/), will show whether PDD is losing shoppers or just spending less on them.

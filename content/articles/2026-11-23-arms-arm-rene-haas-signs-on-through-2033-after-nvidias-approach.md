Arm Holdings ($ARM) said on Monday morning that Chief Executive Rene Haas has signed a new agreement to **lead the company through 2033**, and SoftBank said he will join its board as vice chairman on 1 December while staying Arm's chief, Reuters and Investing.com reported. Neither company disclosed the financial terms.

It matters for Nvidia ($NVDA) more than for Arm. The Financial Times reported on 13 November that Nvidia's board had approached Haas, who ran its computing products business before joining Arm, about succeeding Jensen Huang ([our brief](/news/2026-11-13/nvidia-nvda-approached-arms-rene-haas-to-be-its-next-ceo-ft-reports/)), and the Wall Street Journal later named him among the candidates in its search ([our brief](/news/2026-11-17/nvidia-nvda-hires-heidrick-for-its-ceo-search-and-talks-to-arms-rene-haas/)). Arm's statement does not mention Nvidia, but a contract to 2033 takes the best-known outside name off the list, as Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent presses the board to hire an outsider ([our narrative](/news/2026-11-23/nvidias-crisis-has-moved-from-its-boss-to-its-books-what-the-evidence-says/)).

Arm rose 9.0% to $165.92 before the open, as of 7:15 a.m. ET, in a broad chip rebound after it fell 14.1% on Friday. The next marker is whether Nvidia's board names a chief executive before Huang's House interview on 1 December.

The Justice Department's antitrust division closed its investigation into NextEra Energy's ($NEE) $66.8 billion purchase of Dominion Energy ($D) on Thursday evening, Reuters and Investing.com reported. The department had worried the combined company could switch off viable power plants to push up capacity prices in New England; the companies told federal energy regulators they would let ISO New England's market monitor review proposed plant closures for five years, starting with the May 2028 capacity auction.

That removes one federal hurdle, but not the last. Virginia's State Corporation Commission, the North Carolina and South Carolina commissions, the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission and the Nuclear Regulatory Commission must still approve it, and the companies expect to close in the second half of 2027, according to their Virginia filing.

The news came after the close. NextEra **fell 1.2% to $83.79** on Thursday and Dominion fell 1.1% to $66.65, by our price data. A ruling from Virginia's commission is the next step that decides it.

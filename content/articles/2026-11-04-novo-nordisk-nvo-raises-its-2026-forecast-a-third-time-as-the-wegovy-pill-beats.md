Novo Nordisk ($NVO) raised its 2026 forecast for the third time this year on Wednesday, after its Wegovy weight-loss pill sold **5.39 billion Danish crowns** in the third quarter, up 53% from the second and above the 4.62 billion analysts expected in a company-compiled consensus, Reuters and CNBC reported. It now expects adjusted sales and operating profit to range from a 2% fall to a 2% rise at constant exchange rates, from a fall of up to 6% before.

The pill is the first real sign that Novo can win back ground from Eli Lilly ($LLY), whose rival pill Foundayo is taking a growing share of new prescriptions, Reuters reported. Adjusted operating profit of 31.72 billion crowns beat the 28.39 billion expected. But the injections that still make most of its money shrank: Wegovy shots fell 2% and Ozempic 5% at constant currencies, CNBC reported, and Chief Executive Mike Doustdar said "turnaround and recovery is not done based on one data point."

Its US-listed shares were **up 3.8%** at $41.72 before the open, by 6:14 a.m. ET in our data, after a 21.0% fall this year. The next test is the fourth quarter: whether pill sales keep growing fast enough to outrun the shrinking injections.

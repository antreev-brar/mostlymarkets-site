Vertex Pharmaceuticals ($VRTX), the maker of the leading cystic fibrosis drugs, reports third-quarter results after the market closes on Monday, 2 November, with a call at 4:30 p.m. ET, the company said on 7 October. The stock goes in at a 52-week high after **rising 3.5%** on Friday, its fourth straight gain, a week after it published new kidney-drug data.

Last quarter, revenue rose 12% to $3.33 billion, and Vertex raised its 2026 forecast to $13.1 billion to $13.2 billion, from $12.95 billion to $13.1 billion, excluding Crinetics. Non-GAAP EPS of $4.73 fell short of the $4.79 Zacks expected, the AP reported. Casgevy, its gene therapy for sickle cell disease, sold $76 million, up 151% from a year earlier. The stock **rose 1.7%** on 4 August.

## What analysts expect

- **Revenue:** $3.40 billion according to Zacks and $3.42 billion according to FactSet data cited by TradingView, up about 10% from $3.08 billion a year ago.
- **Non-GAAP EPS:** $4.92 (Zacks) or $4.80 (FactSet), against $4.80 a year ago.

The EPS line is less reliable than usual. Vertex closed its roughly $10 billion purchase of Crinetics Pharmaceuticals, a maker of hormone-disorder drugs, on 1 September and said it would give the financial details on this call. Estimates that include deal costs and those that don't are not comparable, so revenue is the cleaner test.

## What to watch

- **The new forecast.** Whether Vertex folds Crinetics into its 2026 revenue range, and how much extra spending it adds for next year.
- **Beyond cystic fibrosis.** Casgevy and Journavx, its non-opioid painkiller ($50 million last quarter), are the first sales outside the cystic fibrosis franchise. Their growth shows whether Vertex can diversify before that franchise matures.
- **The kidney drug.** On 23 October Vertex published Phase 3 results for povetacicept in IgA nephropathy, a chronic kidney disease, showing a 49.8% cut in protein in the urine against placebo. The FDA's decision is due by 30 November, so launch plans on the call matter.

## How the stock has done

Vertex closed Friday at $558.88, its highest close in a year, **up 23.3%** this year and 10.3% over the past month. It has moved 5% or more on five sessions in the past year, and $1,000 invested five years ago is worth $3,022 today.

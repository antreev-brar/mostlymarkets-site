## What happened
Shares of flash-memory maker Sandisk ($SNDK) **jumped 9.4%** to $80.51 by 10:16 a.m. ET on Friday, while data-storage maker Everpure ($P) rose 5.9% and server maker Dell Technologies ($DELL) 5.2%. Buyers returned from Thanksgiving to the AI hardware stocks that had been sold hardest in November, on a half-day session that closes at 1 p.m. ET.

Benzinga, Stocktwits and Investing.com all described the move as dip-buying in beaten-down memory and AI names, with no company news behind it. Micron ($MU) was up 3.7% at the same time, Seagate ($STX) 3.5% and Western Digital ($WDC) 3.3%. Benzinga also pointed to a storage demonstration by Nvidia ($NVDA) and Kioxia, Sandisk's flash-manufacturing partner, released after the close on Wednesday. Nvidia itself was down 1.7%.

## Why it matters
The bounce comes off deep lows. Sandisk closed Wednesday at its lowest since its 8-for-1 share split in early November, and Investing.com put its fall from the June peak at about 75%. Dell ended Wednesday 49.5% below its September high even after it [doubled its quarterly revenue and reported a $132 billion AI backlog](/news/2026-11-24/dell-technologies-dell-beats-q3-estimates-as-revenue-doubles-and-its-ai-backlog-hits-132-billion/), and Everpure sat 39.7% below its October high.

Nothing in Friday's move answers the question these stocks trade on: whether AI data centers keep paying today's prices for memory and storage. Research firm TrendForce has warned that DRAM price gains could fade through 2027, 24/7 Wall St. and Invezz reported. That is the argument in [our narrative on AI hardware being priced at its peak](/news/2026-11-25/wall-street-is-pricing-ai-hardware-at-its-peak-dells-record-didnt-change-that/).

## What the market is telling us
Dell's shares moved 5% or more on 57 of the last 252 sessions and Everpure's on 54, so a day like this is ordinary for both. Dell closed Wednesday up 136.0% this year, and $1,000 put into it five years ago would be worth $5,184. We read Friday as a relief bounce in a half-day holiday session, not a change in the demand picture; Micron's results on 17 December are the next hard number for the group.

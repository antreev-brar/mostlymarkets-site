Anthropic's revenue rose to $16.96 billion in the third quarter from $11.56 billion in the second, but it swung to a **$567 million operating loss** from a $974 million operating profit, its amended IPO filing showed on Monday. Computing and infrastructure costs rose 78% from the prior quarter to $9.86 billion, driven largely by about $5 billion of short-term computing it bought, the filing showed and Reuters and Yahoo Finance reported.

It matters beyond one company because Anthropic's computing bill is other companies' revenue. The filing puts its computing and infrastructure commitments at about $895 billion, money that flows to the cloud providers and chipmakers behind the AI build, including its investors Amazon ($AMZN) and Alphabet ($GOOGL). Revenue growth from the prior quarter slowed to 47%, from 144% in the second quarter. Those numbers will shape how the market prices an IPO that was already [getting harder to value](/news/2026-10-31/anthropics-100-billion-ipo-got-harder-to-price-in-the-ai-selloff/) during the chip selloff.

The filing is still a draft registration, so no price range for the shares has been published yet.

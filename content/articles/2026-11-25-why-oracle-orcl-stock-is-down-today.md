## What happened
Shares of database and cloud company Oracle ($ORCL) **fell 8.0%** to $100.72 by 10:15 a.m. ET on Wednesday, below its lowest close of the past year, as the cost of insuring its debt against default hit a record. Oracle's five-year credit default swap spread, the yearly price of that insurance, reached **356 basis points**, or $3.56 per $100 of debt, according to S&P Global Market Intelligence data reported by Reuters and carried by Yahoo Finance and AOL.

Oracle was not alone. The same swaps hit records for Alphabet ($GOOGL), SpaceX and CoreWeave ($CRWV), Reuters and StockAnalysis reported, even after Monday's rally on Jensen Huang's return as Nvidia's chief executive. The whole AI trade fell with them: CNN and Yahoo Finance tied Wednesday's sell-off to doubts about AI spending, new US tariff proposals and October inflation data that points to a Fed hike.

## Why it matters
Oracle is building data centers for OpenAI with borrowed money and new shares, so its debt is where the doubt shows first. On Friday it said the OpenAI build was [fully funded](/news/2026-11-20/oracle-orcl-says-its-openai-data-center-deal-is-fully-funded-after-an-8-drop/), and the stock [jumped 16.7% early on Monday](/news/2026-11-23/why-oracle-orcl-stock-is-soaring-today/). The swaps say lenders are not reassured: a statement about Oracle's own funding does nothing to the cash OpenAI has to pay its bills. Strategists at Aviva said near-term default risk is low but that rising debt and spending are squeezing cash flow, Reuters reported. [Our breakdown](/news/2026-11-21/oracle-orcl-has-a-664-billion-backlog-and-no-free-cash-is-the-business-broken/) sets out how a $664 billion backlog sits alongside no free cash.

## What the market is telling us
Oracle's shares have moved 5% or more on 41 of the last 252 sessions, and the last drop this big was on 20 November, when they fell 8.5%. The stock closed Tuesday at $109.50, down 43.8% this year and 55.9% below its 1 June high of $248.15. We think the credit market is now setting Oracle's share price: until the swaps stop rising, a funding statement or an AI rally hasn't held the stock up for more than a day.

Constellation Energy ($CEG), the largest US owner of nuclear plants, reports third-quarter results before the open on Monday, 9 November, with its call at 9 a.m. ET. It reports five days after a Democratic sweep in the midterms, including the Texas governor's race, raised the risk of a pause on new data-center permits, the business that has driven its stock.

Last quarter, on 6 August, Constellation earned $2.55 a share on an adjusted basis, beating the $2.36 Zacks consensus, and raised its 2026 forecast to $11.50 to $12.50 a share from $11.00 to $12.00, its release showed, as Nasdaq and Benzinga reported. The stock still slipped 1.5% that day, in our data.

## What analysts expect

The Zacks consensus is **$3.90 a share** on an adjusted basis, up 28% from $3.04 a year earlier, on revenue of $8.72 billion, up about a third, Zacks reported via TradingView and Yahoo Finance. MarketBeat's consensus is lower on earnings, at $3.73, and higher on revenue, at $9.21 billion. The jump comes mostly from Calpine, the gas-plant owner Constellation bought, whose plants were not in last year's numbers.

## What to watch

- **The 2026 forecast.** Constellation raised it in August; holding $11.50 to $12.50 would show the core business on track, and a lift would matter more after the stock's fall.
- **Data-center contracts.** Any new long-term deal to supply a tech company's data centers, or comments on how state permit rules after the election affect signed and planned deals.
- **Calpine.** How much the acquired plants added, against the costs of combining the two companies, which cut GAAP profit last quarter.

## How the stock has done

Constellation **fell 6.7%** on 4 November, the day after the election, alongside a 13.5% drop in NRG Energy ($NRG), and recovered 4.3% on Friday to $249.95 ([our Sunday edition](/daily/2026-11-08/)). It is down 29.2% this year and 12.3% over the past month, and sits 34.0% below its closing high of $378.60 from 11 December 2025. Vistra ($VST), which raised its outlook last week, is the bar ([our Vistra recap](/news/2026-11-06/vistra-vst-jumps-as-q3-profit-rises-50-and-it-raises-its-2026-outlook/)).

Nvidia ($NVDA) reported fiscal third-quarter revenue of **$115.8 billion** after the close on Tuesday, up 103% from a year earlier and well above the $109.1 billion analysts expected, according to LSEG data cited by CNBC and Reuters. Adjusted earnings were $2.68 a share against the $2.48 expected. In its first report since Jensen Huang resigned, the stock rose 1.7% to $158.17 after hours as of 5:15 p.m. ET, from a regular-session close of $155.46, its lowest in a year.

## What stood out

The data center business did the work. It brought in **$107.6 billion**, about 93% of sales, as Nvidia began shipping its Vera Rubin NVL72 rack systems, the release said. Edge computing, which takes in its robotics and car chips, grew 42% to $8.2 billion.

The cash answered the worry we flagged in our [preview](/news/2026-11-16/what-to-expect-from-nvidias-nvda-q3-earnings/). Free cash flow **more than doubled to $43.8 billion** from the prior quarter, after a fall that had fed the charge that Nvidia was funding its own customers ([our narrative](/news/2026-11-16/wall-street-is-betting-ais-1-trillion-money-loop-breaks-at-the-neoclouds-nvidias-report-will-test-it/)). Nvidia returned $28.1 billion through buybacks and dividends and has $227.3 billion of buyback authority left.

## The outlook

The guide of **$130.0 billion** sits about $5 billion above the LSEG consensus, Reuters reported. Two things flatter and temper it: the fourth quarter has an extra week, and the outlook assumes no data center compute revenue from China, the release said. The adjusted gross margin guide of 71% to 72% is three points below this quarter, a cost of ramping Rubin.

Interim Chief Executive Colette Kress raised the fiscal 2028 outlook to growth of about 75%, from the 70% she gave in August, Yahoo Finance and TheStreet reported from the call. Chair Harvey Jones said the board does not intend to reinstate Huang as chief executive, CNBC and Yahoo Finance reported, hours after Huang said he would be honored to return ([our story](/news/2026-11-17/jensen-huang-says-he-would-be-honored-to-return-as-nvidia-nvda-ceo/)).

## Our take

The debate going in was whether customers moved orders to AMD ($AMD) and Broadcom ($AVGO) while Nvidia's leadership was in doubt. A guide **$4.9 billion above consensus** and a higher 2028 growth target say not yet, though the extra week makes the fourth quarter look stronger than it is. The muted after-hours move says the market is pricing governance, not demand: the SEC probe and the CEO search ([our story](/news/2026-11-17/nvidia-nvda-hires-heidrick-for-its-ceo-search-and-talks-to-arms-rene-haas/)) are what we'll watch next, along with whether the gross margin returns toward 75% once Rubin is in volume.

On Friday, Intel ($INTC) **plunged 28.6%**, its worst day since 1974, CNN reported, after forecasting fourth-quarter profit far below estimates. It was the week's pattern: four of the S&P 500's five worst performers, Intel among them, beat estimates for the quarter just ended, then lost a fifth or more of their value in a day on their outlooks.

The selling stayed in tech: the Nasdaq Composite fell about 5.3% in October, its worst month since March 2025, Yahoo Finance reported, while the Dow rose. We think the week showed the AI boom's side effects reaching companies' results and forecasts: pricier memory chips for the companies that buy them, fewer visitors sent to Reddit ($RDDT) by Google, partly because of AI summaries in search results, and a spending bill that kept Alphabet's ($GOOGL) free cash flow below zero.

### Intel had its worst day since 1974

Intel forecast fourth-quarter adjusted earnings of **25 cents a share**, against the 43 cents analysts expected, Reuters reported ([full story](/news/2026-10-29/intel-intc-q3-profit-tops-estimates-shares-plunge-on-a-weak-forecast/)).

At the forecast's midpoint, revenue slips only about 6% from the third quarter, but Intel pays for its factories whether or not they run full, so adjusted profit per share falls by more than half. It blamed a memory-chip shortage for choking demand for PCs, about half its sales, and that warning sent PC maker Dell ($DELL) down 10.8% on Friday ([full story](/news/2026-10-30/why-dell-technologies-dell-stock-is-sinking-today/)).

### Even after Friday, Intel is up 78% this year

Intel had risen 149.5% this year going into Friday, so the drop took back less than half of its gain.

Even now it trades at **5.8 times its sales**, more than twice its five-year median of about 2.4 by our calculation, so the price still assumes a turnaround ([our breakdown](/news/2026-10-31/intel-intc-lost-28-6-in-a-day-is-the-business-broken/)).

## What's next

Apple ($AAPL) and Qualcomm ($QCOM) report into the same memory squeeze that hit Intel and F5 this week ([full preview](/news/2026-10-29/earnings-week-ahead-apple-amd-and-palantir-plus-the-election-and-jobs/)).

- **Monday, 2 November:** Apple reports after the close. In July it guided to a gross margin of 47% to 48% and warned of "even higher memory costs." Its stock is up 27% this year, the most in the Magnificent Seven ([preview](/news/2026-10-29/what-to-expect-from-apples-aapl-q4-earnings/)). Palantir ($PLTR) also reports after the close.
- **Tuesday, 3 November:** the midterm elections. AMD ($AMD), down 10.9% on Friday, reports after the close; analysts expect revenue up about 41% ([preview](/news/2026-10-29/amd-amd-reports-earnings-on-tuesday-what-to-watch/)).
- **Wednesday, 4 November:** Qualcomm and Arm ($ARM) report after the close. Qualcomm's handset-chip sales fell 20% from a year earlier in the June quarter, in what Chief Executive Cristiano Amon called "a challenging memory and supply environment." Uber ($UBER) reports before the open.
- **Friday, 6 November:** the October jobs report. In September employers added just 29,000 jobs, against 90,000 expected.

Take-Two Interactive ($TTWO), the video game publisher behind Grand Theft Auto and NBA 2K, reports its fiscal second quarter, the three months to September, after the close on Monday, 9 November. It is the last report before Grand Theft Auto VI goes on sale on **19 November**, so none of the game's sales will be in these numbers, Tickeron and Ad Hoc News noted.

Last quarter, Take-Two's net bookings, the sales of games and in-game items in the period, fell 3% from a year earlier to about $1.39 billion but beat the top of its guidance on NBA 2K and the Grand Theft Auto series, its release showed. It kept its fiscal-2027 net bookings forecast of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion and said GTA VI pre-orders were off to an exceptional start, without giving numbers. The stock **rose 6.0%** on 7 August, the day after, in our data.

## What analysts expect

The Zacks consensus is **$0.90 a share** on an adjusted basis, unchanged over the past 30 days, on revenue of $1.66 billion, Zacks reported via TradingView and Yahoo Finance. MarketBeat's consensus is $0.97 a share on $1.61 billion. Take-Two itself guided to net bookings of $1.62 billion to $1.67 billion for the quarter; Zacks flagged weaker mobile games and higher costs as headwinds.

## What to watch

- **The $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion forecast.** Most of it depends on GTA VI's first weeks; any change ten days before launch would move the stock more than the quarter.
- **Pre-orders.** Management called them exceptional in August; a number, or a stronger word, is the clearest read on launch demand.
- **Mobile.** Mobile fell 7% last quarter, its release said; another decline would show the rest of the business still needs GTA VI to carry the year.

## How the stock has done

Take-Two **jumped 7.1%** on 4 November and closed Friday at $214.87, up 2.6% over the past month. It is down 16.1% this year and 16.9% below its closing high of $258.41 from 6 July. $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $1,148 today.

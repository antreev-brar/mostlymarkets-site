On Monday, funds managed by Blackstone ($BX) agreed to lead a **$3.1 billion** investment for a 49.9% non-controlling stake in the Mitsubishi subsidiary that holds the Japanese trading house's 15% interest in LNG Canada, the gas-export plant in Kitimat, British Columbia, Blackstone said in a release also reported by Bloomberg and The Globe and Mail. Four Canadian pension investors join it: CPP Investments, OMERS, IMCO and PSP Investments.

About $1.6 billion of the total pays for the stake itself, Reuters and Financial News reported; the rest covers capital spending on the plant's second phase. Shell ($SHEL), which owns 40%, and its partners approved that phase in late September, and it will double the plant's capacity to 28 million tonnes of liquefied gas a year, according to World Oil and OE Digital. The deal lets Mitsubishi share the bill for its part of the expansion without giving up control of its holding.

Blackstone shares were up **4.1%** at $122.17 as of 12:30 p.m. ET, but Apollo ($APO) and KKR ($KKR) were up more than 5%, so the day's move looks like a rally across private-markets managers rather than a verdict on this deal. The full terms may only become clear in a later SEC filing, Financial News reported.

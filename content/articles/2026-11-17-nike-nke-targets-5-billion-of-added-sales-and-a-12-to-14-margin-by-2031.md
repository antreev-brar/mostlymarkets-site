Nike ($NKE) **jumped 4.8%** to $40.10 at the open on Tuesday after its first investor day in nine years set targets for fiscal 2031: about $5 billion of added revenue from a fiscal 2027 base, an operating margin of 12% to 14%, and adjusted earnings of $3.00 to $3.50 a share, the company said in a release filed with the SEC. Performance sports should bring about $4 billion of the growth and sportswear about $1 billion, Investing.com and StreetInsider reported.

The targets put a date on the recovery investors had asked for. Nike reaffirmed that fiscal 2027 revenue will fall by a high-single-digit percentage, with an adjusted operating margin of about 6% and adjusted EPS of $1.15 to $1.35, so the plan needs earnings per share to more than double in four years. It expects sales declines to ease in fiscal 2028, growth in line with the market in 2029 and faster growth after that, and about $12 billion of dividends from fiscal 2027 through 2031.

Even after the jump, the stock is down about 37% this year in our data. Its next quarterly report will show whether this year's decline is tracking the guidance.

## What happened
Shares of memory-chip maker Micron ($MU) **fell 9.8%** by 10:15 a.m. ET on Wednesday, and AI-server connectivity makers Astera Labs ($ALAB) and Credo ($CRDO) lost 9.8% and 10.5%, as the sell-off in AI chip stocks ran into a third session. Nvidia ($NVDA) was down 7.0% and Arm ($ARM) 9.0% in our data, with the Nasdaq Composite off 3.5%.

There was no company news from any of the three. CNN and Yahoo Finance tied the drop to three things landing at once: doubts that AI spending will pay off, the new tariffs the US Trade Representative proposed on 14 trading partners on Wednesday, and October inflation data that leaves a December rate hike close to certain. Core PCE prices rose 3.3% from a year earlier, a tenth more than forecast, as [we reported this morning](/news/2026-11-25/core-pce-inflation-rose-to-3-3-in-october-a-tenth-above-forecasts-before-a-fed-hike/).

The selling started in Asia. Taiwan's Taiex fell as much as 8.6% before closing **4.8% lower**, and South Korea's Kospi lost 6.2% as SK Hynix and Samsung each fell about 10%, Bloomberg and Korea JoongAng Daily reported.

## Why it matters
The tariff plan caps Taiwan, South Korea and Japan at 15%, [milder than the market feared at dawn](/news/2026-11-25/us-proposes-tariffs-on-14-trading-partners-with-20-on-china-and-a-15-cap-for-taiwan/), but it lands on stocks that had already lost their footing. On Tuesday every one of the 30 stocks in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell and the index dropped 7.9%, in a [second day of selling](/news/2026-11-24/nvidia-and-micron-shares-slide-what-happened/) that undid [Monday's rally](/news/2026-11-23/stock-market-today-nvidia-adds-952-billion-as-jensen-huang-returns-and-the-nasdaq-jumps-6-5/) on Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia. A rate hike makes the borrowed money behind data-center building dearer, which is the worry at the root of this sell-off.

## What the market is telling us
Micron's shares have moved 5% or more on 78 of the last 252 sessions, and the last drop this big was on 20 November, when they fell 13.9%. Even after Tuesday, the stock was up 120.7% this year, but at $629.79 it sat 48.1% below its 25 June high. Astera Labs closed Tuesday 60.4% below its high and Credo 64.8% below theirs, with Credo down 26.0% this year. We read this as the market cutting what it will pay for AI hardware across the board, not a verdict on any one company: none of the three put out news of its own today.

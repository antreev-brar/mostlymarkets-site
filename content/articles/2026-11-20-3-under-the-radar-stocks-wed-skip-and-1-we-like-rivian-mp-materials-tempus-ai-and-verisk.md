On Friday the market's sell-off found the companies that still need outside money. As of 2:15 p.m. ET, Tempus AI was **down 8.3%**, Rivian 7.1% and MP Materials 7.0%, while Verisk, a profitable insurance-data company, was up 2.1%. With [JPMorgan now expecting the Fed to raise rates](/news/2026-11-19/jpmorgan-now-expects-fed-rate-hikes-at-each-of-the-next-three-meetings/) and the AI trade on a [losing streak](/news/2026-11-20/anthropics-debut-couldnt-stop-a-sixth-straight-loss-and-chip-stocks-lead-futures-lower/), a company that burns cash pays more for every dollar it raises.

We screened companies worth $5 billion to $30 billion on operating margin over the latest 12 months, from SEC filings, leaving out stocks from our lists of the past 14 days and property trusts, whose rent-based margins don't compare with an operating company's.

## 3 stocks we'd skip

### 1. Rivian ($RIVN)
**Operating margin: -52.9% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Rivian makes electric pickups, SUVs and delivery vans, and is ramping up its cheaper R2.

1. That compares with a **13.8% median** for the 51 consumer companies in our coverage, bottom quarter, and its gross margin of 6.4% over two years is a sixth of the 39.9% median.
2. The third quarter showed progress, not profit. Revenue rose 38% to $2.16 billion and consolidated gross profit was $176 million, but the car business still had an $80 million gross loss, and the company guides to a 2026 adjusted EBITDA loss of $1.75 billion to $1.85 billion, it reported for the third quarter.
3. Shareholders pay for the gap: diluted shares rose 42.6% in two years and free cash flow was -64.7% of sales over the past 12 months.

**The price:** about 2.4 times sales, after a 42.7% fall this year to Thursday and a new low today, so the stock is cheap only if the losses end.
**What would change our mind:** Rivian is aiming for positive car gross profit as it exits the fourth quarter; that number in its next results would matter more than its [UBS upgrade](/news/2026-11-02/rivian-rivn-shares-jump-after-ubs-upgrades-the-stock-to-buy-on-its-r2-ramp/).

### 2. MP Materials ($MP)
**Operating margin: -20.5% in the 12 months to September 2026.** MP mines and refines rare earths at Mountain Pass, California, and is starting to make magnets.

1. That is against an **11.2% median** for its 17 materials peers and 40.7 points lower than five years ago, both bottom of the group: building a magnet business has swallowed the profit the mine used to make.
2. Third-quarter revenue rose 176% to $147.7 million and adjusted earnings of 4 cents beat the Zacks consensus loss of a cent, but $31.6 million of that revenue was a one-time sale of stockpiled material, and the net loss was still $20.7 million, the company said on 5 November.
3. Free cash flow was -134.5% of sales over 12 months, and cash fell to $1.34 billion from $1.83 billion at the end of 2025.

**The price:** about 15 times sales and 3.1 times book value, even after a 49.5% fall from its May high to Thursday's close.
**What would change our mind:** MP says saleable magnet deliveries to General Motors start this quarter; recurring magnet revenue in the fourth-quarter report would show the business it is building can earn.

### 3. Tempus AI ($TEM)
**Operating margin: -18.5% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Tempus sells genetic tests for cancer patients and licenses the resulting data to drugmakers.

1. That is against a **3.6% median** for the 17 health-care services companies in our coverage, bottom quarter, and free cash flow was -9.1% of sales.
2. Revenue rose 23% to $411.5 million, adjusted EBITDA was $25.6 million, and it raised its 2026 revenue forecast to $1.60 billion to $1.605 billion, it said on 2 November. Adjusted EBITDA is not a GAAP measure, and on a GAAP basis the company still loses money.
3. Growth is slowing from a high base: 23% in the latest quarter against 53.5% a year over two years.

**The price:** about 6.6 times sales and 19.9 times book value, high for a company with no operating profit yet.
**What would change our mind:** fourth-quarter revenue at the top of its $458 million to $463 million guide with a GAAP operating profit in sight.

## 1 stock we like

### Verisk Analytics ($VRSK)
**Operating margin: 43.9% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Verisk sells data and software that insurers use to price policies and settle claims, mostly by subscription.

1. That is in the top tenth of the 71 industrials in our coverage, against a **17.2% median**. Free cash flow was 35.7% of sales over two years, almost three times the 12.7% median, and buybacks cut diluted shares by 9.3% in two years.
2. Third-quarter revenue rose 5.3% to $809 million, 7.4% on an organic constant-currency basis, and diluted EPS rose 5.6% to $1.70; it kept its 2026 guidance on its 5 November earnings call. The weak spot: quarterly free cash flow fell 40.5% to $200 million.
3. The long run is steady rather than fast: revenue grew 6.2% a year over five fiscal years, below the 8.0% peer median, so the case is the margin and the cash, not growth.

**The price:** 30.4 times trailing earnings, below its median of 34.2 since October 2024, though 25.2 times forward earnings is above the 20.5 peer median.
**What could go wrong:** Chief Financial Officer Elizabeth Mann warned on the call that light weather on the claims side and soft property markets could weigh on the fourth quarter; its AccuLynx purchase also awaits a Federal Trade Commission review, with net debt already 2.8 years of operating profit.

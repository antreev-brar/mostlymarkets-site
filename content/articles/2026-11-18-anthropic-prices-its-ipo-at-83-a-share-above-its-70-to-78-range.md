Anthropic priced its initial public offering at **$83 a share** on Wednesday, above the $70 to $78 range it set on 10 November, a person familiar with the deal told CNBC, and TechCrunch and Yahoo Finance reported the same. On the 825 million shares in the original terms, that raises about $68.5 billion, by our arithmetic.

The price matters beyond Anthropic. At the top of the old range the offering valued the company at about $1.404 trillion, TechCrunch reported; the same terms at $83 come to roughly $1.49 trillion. It arrives in a week when Nvidia ($NVDA) fell 2.9% on Wednesday after its results and software stocks slid, partly as funds raised cash for the deal, Benzinga and TradingKey reported ([our narrative](/news/2026-11-18/wall-street-is-betting-ai-will-empty-softwares-seats-3-numbers-will-decide-it/)), so a strong debut would say public money still wants AI at a high price. Alphabet ($GOOGL) and Amazon ($AMZN), both investors and cloud suppliers, rose 0.8% and 3.8% on Wednesday ([yesterday's story](/news/2026-11-17/anthropic-is-set-to-price-its-ipo-above-its-70-to-78-range/)).

What's next: the shares are due to start trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT on Thursday, 19 November. The final share count and the company's own confirmation of the price are still to come.

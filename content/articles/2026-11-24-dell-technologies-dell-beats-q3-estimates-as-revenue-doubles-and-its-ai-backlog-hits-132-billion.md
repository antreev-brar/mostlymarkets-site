Dell Technologies ($DELL) said after the close on Tuesday that fiscal third-quarter revenue **rose 103% to $54.9 billion**, well above the $49.32 billion analysts expected, according to LSEG data cited by Reuters. Adjusted EPS of $9.45 beat the $6.59 consensus, and Dell raised its full-year revenue forecast to $202 billion from $192 billion. As of 4:15 p.m. ET, shortly after the release, the stock was down 2.1% after hours at $289.24, after falling 11.8% in the regular session.

## What stood out

The AI server business did the heavy lifting. Dell's Infrastructure Solutions Group, which sells servers, networking and storage to data centers, **grew 198% to $42.0 billion** and earned 91% of segment operating profit. The backlog rose to $132.3 billion from about $95 billion a quarter earlier, by FourWeekMBA's and Investing.com's figures, so orders still outran shipments.

The number that answers this morning's fear is the margin. Hewlett Packard Enterprise fell 16.3% today after warning that its AI server margins will [fall to about 14%](/news/2026-11-24/hpe-hpe-slides-11-5-before-the-open-as-it-sees-ai-server-margins-falling-to-14/); Dell's server-and-storage unit **earned a 17.0% operating margin**, up from 12.4% a year ago. Its company-wide adjusted gross margin held at 21.2%, against 21.1% a year earlier, by our arithmetic from the release, even as low-margin AI racks grew to 40% of sales.

Chief Operating Officer Jeff Clarke went straight at the [financing worry](/news/2026-11-24/coreweave-and-nebius-shares-slide-what-happened/) that hit AI-cloud stocks today. "We ended the quarter with a record $132.3 billion AI backlog after booking $60.2 billion of orders, each rigorously vetted for customer quality and access to capital, land and power," he said in the release.

The PC side was weaker. Client Solutions revenue rose 3% to $12.9 billion, but consumer PC sales fell 15% and the unit's operating profit fell 6%, consistent with what we found in [the AI gadget upgrade](/news/2026-11-24/wall-street-is-betting-on-an-ai-gadget-upgrade-the-units-say-its-mostly-price/): memory costs are pushing prices up and units down.

## The outlook

Dell now expects fiscal 2027 revenue of $202 billion, up 78%, above the $194.48 billion analysts expected, according to LSEG data cited by Reuters. It raised its AI server revenue forecast to $78 billion from $74 billion and its adjusted EPS forecast to $29.68 from $25.50. The board added $25 billion to its buyback authorization after a record $4.9 billion returned to shareholders in the quarter.

## Our take

This was a clean beat on every line investors watch: revenue, profit, backlog and margin. The weak spot is cash: adjusted free cash flow of $4.3 billion was 7.8% of revenue, and our data puts Dell's two-year free cash margin at 5.3%, against a 13.4% median for hardware makers. The stock trades at 10.3 times forward earnings, against 16.5 times for its peers, a price that treats this year as a peak rather than a new level. The next test is whether the backlog keeps growing in the fourth quarter after HPE's warning.

## What the market is telling us

Dell's shares have moved 5% or more on 57 of the last 252 sessions. The stock is up 134.6% this year, but at $295.28 it sits 49.8% below its 52-week closing high of $588.40 from 17 September 2026. Its call with analysts started at 4:30 p.m. ET.

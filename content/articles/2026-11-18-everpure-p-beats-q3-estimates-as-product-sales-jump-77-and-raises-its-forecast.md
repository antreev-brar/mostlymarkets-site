Everpure ($P), the data-storage company formerly called Pure Storage, reported third-quarter revenue of **$1.46 billion** after the close on Wednesday, up 51% from a year earlier and about 9% above the $1.335 billion analysts expected, according to LSEG's IBES data cited by Reuters. Adjusted earnings of 86 cents a share beat the 77 cents Zacks and Benzinga reported as the consensus. The company raised its full-year revenue forecast for the third straight quarter, and the shares were up 5.0% at $105.76 at 4:15 p.m. ET.

## What stood out

Product revenue, the hardware arrays Everpure sells outright, **rose 77%** to $948 million and did most of the work. Subscription services grew 19%, so the quarter's growth came mainly from one-off sales rather than the recurring base. The company said it won a design deal with a second top-five hyperscaler, a cloud giant that will use its DirectFlash technology in its data centers.

*Verdict: a big beat built on product sales, which are lumpier than subscriptions.*

The cost of that mix shows in gross margin. GAAP gross margin **fell to 68.0%** from 72.3% a year earlier, by our reading of the release, because hardware carries lower margins than subscriptions. Scale more than made up for it: adjusted operating income rose to $345 million, and free cash flow reached $265 million, five times last year's.

*Verdict: thinner margins per dollar, far more profit in total.*

Remaining performance obligations, the contracted revenue not yet booked, rose 44% to $4.2 billion. That is faster than the 20% growth in subscription ARR, a sign that customers are signing bigger multi-year orders.

## The outlook

Everpure now expects fiscal 2027 revenue of **$5.20 billion to $5.21 billion**, up from $5.03 billion to $5.07 billion, and adjusted operating income of $1.08 billion to $1.09 billion, up from $940 million to $960 million. "We are raising full-year guidance for the third consecutive quarter and reiterating our strong preliminary FY28 outlook," Chief Financial Officer Tarek Robbiati said in the release. The call starts at 5 p.m. ET.

## Our take

We were impressed by the size of the beat: 9% on revenue is well above the 2% to 4% a typical company beats by, and the full-year range moved up by about $150 million. On the other hand, product sales did the lifting and gross margin is four points lower than a year ago, so the quality of growth is weaker than the headline. The stock had fallen 20.4% in the past month and sits 35.8% below its 13 October high, which left room for a bounce.

By our data, Everpure's five-year revenue growth of 16.8% a year ranks in the top quarter of 27 hardware peers, and its gross margin leads them. Its 30.7 times forward earnings is still well above the 17.6 times peer median, so the price assumes this pace holds. The next test is the fourth-quarter report early next year: revenue above $1.52 billion with gross margin holding near 68% would show the hyperscaler orders are durable.

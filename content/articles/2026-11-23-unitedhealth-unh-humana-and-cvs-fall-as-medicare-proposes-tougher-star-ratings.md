UnitedHealth ($UNH) **fell 5.0% to $322.73** before the open on Monday, as of 9:15 a.m. ET, with CVS Health ($CVS) down 3.7% and Humana ($HUM) down 2.8%. The cause is a rule the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services proposed on 20 November for 2028: it would cut Medicare Advantage Star Ratings to 15 measures, weighted toward clinical outcomes and patient experience, and raise the scores needed for the top ratings, Investing.com and Quartz reported.

The stars set the bonus payments that private Medicare plans use to fund extra benefits, so fewer four- and five-star plans means less money per member. It is the second Medicare hit in a week: a proposed audit rule that could claw back overpayments to 2018 cost UnitedHealth 19.8% in three sessions ([our story](/news/2026-11-18/unitedhealth-humana-and-centene-shares-slide-what-happened/)), and UnitedHealth is already suing over its lower 2027 ratings ([our brief](/news/2026-11-20/unitedhealth-unh-sues-medicare-over-its-lower-2027-star-ratings/)).

This is a proposal, not a final rule. It is due in the Federal Register on 25 November, which opens a 60-day comment period before CMS can finalize it.

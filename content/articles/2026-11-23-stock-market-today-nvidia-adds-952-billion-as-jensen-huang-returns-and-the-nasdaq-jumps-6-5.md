Wall Street won back much of last week's rout on Monday after Nvidia ($NVDA) rehired Jensen Huang as chief executive. The Nasdaq Composite **jumped 6.5%** to 24,089.56 and the S&P 500 rose 4.7% to 7,237.17, ending a seven-day losing streak with both indexes' best day since April 2025, CNBC and Yahoo Finance reported. The Dow added 1,448 points, or 3.0%, to 49,595.31.

## Nvidia's best day since 2003

Nvidia **soared 31.5% to $165.05**, its biggest move of the past year by our price data and its best day since 2003, 24/7 Wall St. and Yahoo Finance reported. By our quote data the gain added about $952 billion to its market value, the biggest one-day dollar gain any company has had, according to Seeking Alpha and 24/7 Wall St. Its board voted unanimously before the open to [reinstate Huang](/news/2026-11-23/nvidia-nvda-shares-soar-29-before-the-open-after-it-reinstates-jensen-huang/), 11 days after he resigned, following [President Trump's call](/news/2026-11-23/trump-urges-nvidia-nvda-to-rehire-jensen-huang-and-the-shares-rise-8-before-the-open/) to bring him back.

The bounce undoes the panic over his exit, not the reason the stock fell in the first place. Nvidia still closed 31.0% below its 6 October record of $239.24, and the board's own investigation, an SEC probe that Reuters said is examining its accounting, and a House interview for Huang on 1 December are all still open. We set out why the crisis has [moved from its boss to its books](/news/2026-11-23/nvidias-crisis-has-moved-from-its-boss-to-its-books-what-the-evidence-says/), and the [bulls and bears on whether it's over](/news/2026-11-23/bulls-vs-bears-on-nvidia-nvda-after-huangs-return-is-the-crisis-over/).

## The AI suppliers bounced harder than Nvidia's rivals

The stocks that fell hardest in the AI sell-off came back hardest. Connectivity-chip maker Credo ($CRDO) **rose 24.0%**, Astera Labs ($ALAB) 23.2%, and chip-renting neoclouds CoreWeave ($CRWV) and Nebius ($NBIS) about 20% each. Dell ($DELL) gained 20.5% the day before its results, as we covered in [the AI-server rally](/news/2026-11-23/why-dell-technologies-dell-stock-is-soaring-today/), and Oracle ($ORCL) closed up 16.2% after saying its OpenAI data centers are [fully funded](/news/2026-11-23/why-oracle-orcl-stock-is-soaring-today/).

The twist was in Nvidia's competitors. AMD ($AMD) rose only 6.9% and Broadcom ($AVGO) 4.4%, after both fell more than 12% on Friday, a sign that money went back to the companies that sell to or through Nvidia, not to those hoping to take its customers. Memory maker Micron ($MU) closed up 13.7% after Citi sized the risk from Nvidia-linked financing at about 12% of DRAM demand ([our brief](/news/2026-11-23/micron-mu-jumps-19-as-citi-sizes-the-nvidia-financing-risk-at-12-of-dram-demand/)).

## What fell on the best day in 19 months

CrowdStrike ($CRWD) **slid 8.9%**, the worst performer among the big stocks we track, after agreeing to buy Zscaler ($ZS) for [$43.9 billion in stock](/news/2026-11-23/crowdstrike-crwd-agrees-to-buy-zscaler-for-43-9-billion-in-stock/); Zscaler rose 16.6% toward the offer. We tested whether the business is worth its price in [our breakdown](/news/2026-11-23/crowdstrike-crwd-bets-its-pricey-stock-on-zscaler-is-the-business-worth-it/). Health insurers lagged on the day Medicare proposed [tougher star ratings](/news/2026-11-23/unitedhealth-unh-humana-and-cvs-fall-as-medicare-proposes-tougher-star-ratings/): Elevance Health ($ELV) fell 3.8% and Humana ($HUM) 1.6%.

Energy stocks slipped as oil fell. West Texas Intermediate crude dropped 3.8% to $80.23 a barrel and Brent fell below $90 on forecasts of a supply surplus as Gulf exports recover, Invezz and OilPrice.com reported. OPEC+ meets on 29 November.

## What's next

The S&P 500 is still 7.7% below its 52-week intraday high of 7,844.52, and the VIX, Wall Street's fear gauge, fell 23.1% to 27.59 but remains well above its 13.38 low of the past year. Hewlett Packard Enterprise ($HPE) and Agilent ($A) report after the close tonight, and Dell reports on Tuesday, the first AI-hardware results since Huang's return.

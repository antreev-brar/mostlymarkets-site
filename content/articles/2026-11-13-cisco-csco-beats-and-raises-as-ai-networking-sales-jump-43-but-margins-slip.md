Cisco ($CSCO) reported fiscal first-quarter revenue of **$18.5 billion** after the close on Thursday, up 24% year on year and above the $18.1 billion analysts expected, according to LSEG data cited by CNBC. Adjusted earnings of $1.40 a share beat the $1.33 LSEG consensus. The shares closed up 1.2% at $102.01 before the report but were down 2.5% at $99.49 before Friday's open, as investors focused on a lower gross margin, Investing.com and Seeking Alpha reported.

## What stood out
Networking carried the quarter. Product revenue **rose 32%**, led by networking at 43%, while security grew 7% and services just 1%, the release shows. It was Cisco's fastest revenue and earnings growth in more than 15 years, Chief Executive Chuck Robbins said: "We are in the very early stages of a multi-year networking supercycle."

The cost of that growth showed in the margin. Adjusted gross margin fell 2.5 points to 65.6%, and product gross margin fell 3.5 points to 63.7%, which Cisco put down to a heavier mix of lower-margin AI hardware for cloud companies and higher memory prices, Investing.com and Seeking Alpha reported. Operating costs grew only 2%, so the adjusted operating margin still widened to 37.8% from 34.4%. Cash from operations rose 36% to $4.4 billion, and Cisco returned $3.7 billion through buybacks and dividends.

## The outlook
Cisco guided second-quarter revenue to **$18.0 billion to $18.2 billion** and adjusted EPS to $1.34 to $1.36, above the $17.88 billion and $1.23 analysts expected, Investing.com and TokenPost reported. It raised its full-year adjusted EPS forecast to $5.47 to $5.53, from $5.05 to $5.11. The second-quarter gross margin guide of 65% to 66% means the pressure doesn't ease next quarter.

## Our take
Demand came in better than analysts expected: guidance went up by about $2.5 billion at the midpoint in one quarter, and the AI target more than doubles last year's total. The weak spot is what Cisco keeps of each dollar, because AI switches for a few giant buyers carry thinner margins than the enterprise gear that built its 64.7% two-year gross margin, top quarter among peers in our data. At 20 times forward earnings against an 18.5x peer median, the stock already prices growth; the next report, due in February, will show whether the margin settles near 65%.

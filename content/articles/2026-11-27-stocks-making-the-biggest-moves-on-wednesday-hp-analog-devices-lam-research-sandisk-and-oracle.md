**HP ($HPQ)**, +11.5%: the PC and printer maker closed at $31.15, its best day since 22 May, the day after its fiscal fourth-quarter results beat estimates. Revenue rose 9.3% to $16.0 billion and adjusted EPS of $0.83 topped the $0.74 expected, though 15 cents of it came from tariff refunds and PC units fell 29%; MarketWatch called HP a standout gainer in the tech rout. **Analog Devices ($ADI)**, +6.4%: the analog-chip maker closed at $333.11, winning back part of Tuesday's 17.4% plunge, its worst day of the past year, after a forecast that implied almost no growth next quarter. **Lam Research ($LRCX)**, -5.7%: the chip-equipment maker closed at $185.01, its second straight fall of 5% or more, after a US bill that would curb sales of chipmaking tools to China met the third day of the AI chip selloff. **Oracle ($ORCL)**, -5.1%: the cloud and database company closed at $103.88, 58% below its June high, after the cost of insuring its debt against default hit a record 356 basis points, according to S&P Global Market Intelligence data reported by Reuters.

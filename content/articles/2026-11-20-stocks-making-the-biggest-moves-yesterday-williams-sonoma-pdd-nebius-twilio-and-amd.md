**Williams-Sonoma ($WSM)**, -15.2%: the home-furnishings retailer closed at $220.66, its biggest one-day drop in a year by our data, after a third quarter that only matched estimates with help from tariff refunds, which supplied 1.4 points of its operating margin, while it merely reiterated its full-year outlook. [Full story](/news/2026-11-19/williams-sonoma-wsm-q3-matches-estimates-with-tariff-refund-help-and-the-stock-slides/)

**PDD Holdings ($PDD)**, +11.0%: the owner of Temu and Pinduoduo closed at $86.00, its best day in a year, as a 17% cut in marketing spending lifted adjusted profit to 31 billion yuan even though revenue was flat and fell 6% short of estimates. [Full story](/news/2026-11-19/pdd-holdings-pdd-misses-q3-revenue-estimates-but-beats-on-profit-as-it-cuts-marketing/)

**Nebius Group ($NBIS)**, -9.4%: the AI cloud company, which rents out Nvidia chips, had no news of its own and fell with CoreWeave ($CRWV), down 6.1%, as AI stocks kept sliding. It closed at $96.92, down 43.5% in a month and 66.2% below its 52-week high, a slide that sped up after its third-quarter report last week pointed 2026 revenue to the low end of its forecast because of late data centers. [Our market wrap](/news/2026-11-19/stock-market-today-anthropic-jumps-17-in-its-debut-but-the-dow-drops-1101-points/)

**Twilio ($TWLO)**, -8.8%: the business-messaging software company had no news of its own and fell with the rest of tech, Seeking Alpha and GuruFocus reported, closing at $178.78. That leaves it down 33.0% in a month, after it fell 15.2% on 30 October following its third-quarter report.

**AMD ($AMD)**, -7.7%: the chipmaker closed at $489.19 after Hunterbrook Media alleged that its server software kept reaching a blacklisted Chinese server maker and its affiliated fund disclosed a short position. The drop more than erased Wednesday's 5.3% gain.

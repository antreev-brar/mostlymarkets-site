KKR ($KKR), the private-equity and private-credit firm, reports third-quarter results before the open on Monday, 9 November, with its call at 10 a.m. ET. It reports with the stock down 24.3% this year.

Last quarter, on 30 July, KKR reported adjusted net income of $1.63 a share, up 40% from a year earlier in dollars, and fee-related earnings, the steady management-fee profit investors value most, of $1.32 a share, up 37%, its results release showed and Quartr summarized. The stock rose 1.7% that day, in our data.

## What analysts expect

The Zacks consensus is **$1.58 a share** of adjusted net income, unchanged over the past week, Zacks reported via TradingView and Yahoo Finance. Zacks expects assets under management of $820.4 billion, up 13.4% from a year earlier, and management fees of $1.23 billion. KKR beat the Zacks estimate in three of the last four quarters. MarketBeat and TradingView put revenue at about $2.66 billion, on a broader definition than Zacks uses.

## What to watch

- **Fee-related earnings.** The steady part of the profit; growth near last quarter's 37% would show fundraising is holding up despite the sell-off in private-markets stocks.
- **Fundraising and assets.** Whether assets under management pass the $820 billion Zacks expects, and how much came from wealthy individuals, a channel every big private-markets firm is chasing.
- **Realizations.** Last quarter's results included KKR's largest quarter ever for selling investments, its release said; a slower pace in a weaker market would cut adjusted net income first.

## How the stock has done

KKR closed Friday at $96.48, down 24.3% this year and 32.4% below its closing high of $142.77 from 11 December 2025, though up 7.7% over the past month. It moved 5% or more on 11 of the last 252 sessions; $1,000 invested five years ago would be worth $1,202 today.

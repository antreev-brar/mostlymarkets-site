PayPal ($PYPL) said on Friday that Claire Bramley, the finance chief of New Zealand accounting-software company Xero, will become its chief financial officer on **3 February 2027**, according to a regulatory filing and Reuters. She replaces Jamie Miller, whose role as chief financial and operating officer ends the day before; the filing says her exit is not the result of any disagreement over the company's financial statements.

Bramley, 49, was finance chief of data-analytics company Teradata from 2021 to 2025 before joining Xero, and earlier held finance roles at HP Inc. Miller, who joined in November 2023, also ran PayPal as interim chief executive this year after Alex Chriss left, Fortune reported, so she leaves after running both its finances and, for a time, the whole company.

The shares were down **4.4%** at $53.90 in premarket trading as of 9:14 a.m. ET, while Nasdaq futures fell on a deepening AI selloff. The filing did not say where Miller is going next.

Fast growth is on sale this week. On Thursday the Nasdaq fell 2.3% and the S&P 500 lost 2.0% for a sixth straight day, and Palantir ($PLTR), MercadoLibre ($MELI) and Rocket Lab ($RKLB) are all down this year despite growing sales by half or more. Dell ($DELL) is the exception: it is up 148% this year and still trades at 11 times forward earnings.

Our screen: companies in our coverage ranked by revenue growth over the 12 months to their latest SEC-filed quarter (September or October 2026), leaving out any stock in our lists of the last 14 days. We then judged each on five years of filings against industry peers, on cash flow as well as sales, and on the price. Prices are Thursday's closes.

```picks
like: PLTR, MELI, DELL
skip: RKLB
```

## 3 stocks we like

### 1. Palantir Technologies ($PLTR)
**Revenue growth: 87% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Palantir sells software that lets governments and companies pull their data together and run AI models on it.

1. **It grows fast and keeps the cash.** Revenue rose 32.6% a year over five fiscal years, against a software peer median of 15.9%, and its free cash flow margin was 55.6% over the last 12 months, the best of 30 software peers in our data.
2. **The growth is speeding up.** Third-quarter revenue rose 96% to $2.31 billion, above the $2.18 billion expected, and Palantir raised its 2026 forecast to about $8.53 billion from $8.15 billion in August, Investing Live and AI Weekly reported. US commercial sales overtook government sales for the first time ([our recap](/news/2026-11-02/palantir-pltr-beats-q3-estimates-as-us-commercial-sales-overtake-government/)).
3. **The margin is a software outlier.** Its operating margin of 45.6% over the last 12 months compares with a peer median of 9.7%.

**The price:** 85.8 times forward earnings against a peer median of 24.8 times, the highest bar on this list; the stock is down 13.5% this year and 26.5% below its high.

**What would change our mind:** the total value of new contracts, which [rose only 6%](/news/2026-11-03/palantir-pltr-shares-slide-as-its-new-contracts-grow-just-6/) in the third quarter while revenue rose 96%. Another quarter like that would say the growth is running ahead of new business.

### 2. MercadoLibre ($MELI)
**Revenue growth: 47% in the 12 months to September 2026.** MercadoLibre runs Latin America's biggest online marketplace and a payments and lending business, Mercado Pago.

1. **Five years of compounding.** Revenue grew 48.7% a year over five fiscal years, the fastest of 15 broadline retail peers, whose median is 17.2%.
2. **It turns sales into cash.** Its free cash flow margin was 31.8% over the last 12 months, against a peer median of 11.9%.
3. **The margin squeeze looks chosen.** Third-quarter revenue rose 45% to $10.75 billion, but the operating margin fell to 6.3% from 9.8% as it spent on free shipping, logistics and promotions in Brazil, the release and StockStory show. Management said part of the logistics cost should ease in the fourth quarter as new capacity fills.

**The price:** 32.8 times forward earnings, above the 18.2 times peer median but well below its own median trailing multiple of 52.5 times since 2024; the stock is down 32.7% this year.

**What would change our mind:** an operating margin that keeps falling in the fourth quarter after management said logistics costs would ease, or a jump in loan losses as its credit book, up 62% to $17.9 billion, ages.

### 3. Dell Technologies ($DELL)
**Revenue growth: 49% in the 12 months to July 2026.** Dell sells PCs, storage and, now mostly, the servers that run AI models.

1. **The orders are already in.** Dell ended its fiscal second quarter with a record $95 billion backlog of AI server orders after booking $60.9 billion in the quarter, TIKR, Zacks and Network World reported.
2. **It shrinks its share count.** Diluted shares fell 9.9% over two years, the best of 31 hardware peers, so per-share earnings grow faster than profit.
3. **Growth is speeding up.** Revenue rose 57.7% in the latest quarter from a year earlier, against a peer median of 29.9%.

**The price:** 11.0 times forward earnings against a peer median of 17.2 times, even after a 148% rise this year.

**What would change our mind:** cash. Its free cash flow margin was 5.7% over the last 12 months, below the 13.5% peer median, and AI servers carry thin margins; a fiscal third quarter that converts little of the backlog into cash would weaken the case.

## 1 stock we'd skip

### Rocket Lab ($RKLB)
**Revenue growth: 59% in the 12 months to September 2026.** Rocket Lab launches small satellites on its Electron rocket, builds spacecraft parts and is developing a larger rocket, Neutron.

1. **Sales grow, losses don't shrink.** Third-quarter revenue rose 73% to a record $268 million, but the loss was wider than expected, Finviz and Runtime Wire reported. Its operating margin was -27.6% over the last 12 months, against a 17.2% median for aerospace peers.
2. **It pays for growth with new shares.** Diluted shares rose 31.1% over two years, among the most of 83 aerospace peers, and free cash flow was -36.9% of sales over the last 12 months.
3. **The backlog is real but slow.** Backlog reached a record $3.57 billion, up 52% from the previous quarter, 24/7 Wall St. and the release show, but Neutron, the rocket meant to carry its bigger contracts, has yet to fly.

**The price:** about 37 times sales, with no earnings to measure; the stock is down 26.9% this year and 66.1% below its high.

**What could go wrong:** for us, a successful first Neutron launch. It would open bigger contracts and could move the company to profit faster than its filings now suggest.

CF Industries ($CF) agreed on Tuesday to buy Mosaic's ($MOS) Faustina ammonia plant in St. James, Louisiana, in a deal valued at about **$600 million**, Seeking Alpha and The Fly reported from the companies' announcements. CF pays $360 million in cash at closing; the rest of the value comes through a 20-year contract under which CF supplies Mosaic with up to 200,000 tons of ammonia a year at cost-based prices. The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027.

For CF, the appeal is unused capacity: the plant ran at about 60% of capacity over five years, against about 96% across CF's own plants, and CF plans to spend $100 million over three years to lift output to about 570,000 tons a year by 2030, GuruFocus and Finimize reported. For Mosaic, which we put on our [skip list on Monday](/news/2026-11-23/3-under-the-radar-stocks-we-like-and-1-wed-skip-verisign-corpay-gen-digital-and-mosaic/) for its negative free cash flow, the cash goes to paying down debt, and the plant's capital spending leaves its books.

CF shares rose 2.9% and Mosaic's were little changed as of 10:30 a.m. ET.

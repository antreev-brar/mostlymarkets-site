Uber ($UBER) said early on Friday that holders of **87.13%** of Delivery Hero's share capital accepted its €41.50-a-share cash offer by the time the extra acceptance window shut on 24 November. Uber started with a stake of about 24.8%, and Prosus had agreed in advance to tender its roughly 16.7%, according to Uber's release and TipRanks.

The result gives Uber firm control of the Berlin-based food-delivery group, in a deal Bloomberg valued at **$14.8 billion** when it was agreed in July. Delivery Hero's businesses joining Uber handled $42 billion of gross bookings in 2025, Uber said at the time, and the operations in 14 markets where the two overlap go to a separate buyer, SSW Partners. Uber's shares were up 1.4% at $67.30 in premarket trading, as of 6:12 a.m. ET, and are down 18.8% this year.

What isn't known yet is how hard regulators push back. Uber said closing still needs merger-control and other clearances and is expected in the second half of 2027.

## What happened

Shares of video-game publisher Take-Two Interactive ($TTWO) **jumped 6.4%** in the morning session on Wednesday after the app-data firm Sensor Tower estimated that preorders for Grand Theft Auto VI have topped $1.5 billion, a day before the game goes on sale on 19 November. The stock was at $216.93 as of 10:30 a.m. ET.

Sensor Tower put preorders at about 15.9 million copies worth roughly $1.54 billion, Stocktwits and Benzinga reported. That is an outside estimate: Take-Two has not published a preorder figure. Investing.com tied the morning's gain to the same numbers.

## Why it matters

The stock has traded on this launch for months. Before Wednesday, the shares had **fallen 10.5%** in two sessions, from $227.82 on Friday to $203.93 on Tuesday, by our data (AOL and 24/7 Wall St. also reported the drop). The preorder estimate answers the first question, whether demand is there, and the bounce says the market had priced in less.

What isn't known yet is how many copies sell in the first days and how much players keep spending inside the game after they buy it, the two numbers that decide whether the launch lives up to the preorders.

## What the market is telling us

Take-Two's shares moved 5% or more on 11 of the last 252 sessions, so this is a rare move for the stock. Before today it was down 20.4% this year, and at $203.93 it sat 21.1% below its 52-week closing high of $258.41 from 6 July 2026. $1,000 invested in Take-Two five years ago would be worth $1,152 today.

We think the stock is pricing a big launch, not a record one: today's bounce wins back little more than half of the two-day drop, so the first sales numbers carry more weight than the preorders.

Vertex Pharmaceuticals ($VRTX) said after the close on Wednesday that the Food and Drug Administration has extended its review of povetacicept, its drug for the kidney disease IgA nephropathy, by up to three months, moving the decision from 30 November to **no later than 28 February 2027**. Late in the review the agency asked for more information; Vertex answered, and the FDA classed the answer as a major amendment that needs more time, the company said in a release. The shares **slid 7.7% to $520.50** after hours as of 5:11 p.m. ET.

Vertex had used a priority review voucher to cut the review from ten months to six and get a decision this year, it said when the FDA accepted the application. Vertex didn't say what the FDA asked for, Investing.com noted, and it said it remains confident in the data behind its request for accelerated approval. The delay landed on a day the stock had closed at a 52-week high of $564.12, up 24.4% this year; it featured among the high-margin stocks [we'd skip on price](/news/2026-11-24/3-high-margin-stocks-wed-skip-and-1-we-like-vertex-edwards-estee-lauder-and-salesforce/) on Tuesday.

What isn't known yet is what the FDA asked for, and so whether the extra review touches the trial data; the new deadline for a decision is 28 February.

Kalyan Krishnamurthy is stepping down as chief executive of Flipkart, the Indian online retailer Walmart ($WMT) controls, the company said on Wednesday, as reported by Reuters and Deccan Herald. He has run it **since January 2017**, and leaves after 11 years at the company, Deccan Herald reported. Chief Product and Technology Officer Balaji Thiagarajan will lead on an interim basis with the board, and a permanent successor will be named in the coming weeks, Flipkart said. It gave no reason for the departure.

Flipkart is Walmart's main bet on India's e-commerce market, where it competes with Amazon ($AMZN), and the exit comes ahead of a planned stock market listing in India, Startup Fortune and the Free Press Journal reported. Changing chief executives at that stage raises questions about the listing's timing, and about the value Walmart can show for its 2018 purchase.

Walmart's shares rose 2.1% on Tuesday and were up 0.9% before the open as of 4:30 a.m. ET, in our data; they sit 1.4% below their 52-week closing high from May. The successor, and whether Flipkart keeps its listing timetable, are the next things to watch.

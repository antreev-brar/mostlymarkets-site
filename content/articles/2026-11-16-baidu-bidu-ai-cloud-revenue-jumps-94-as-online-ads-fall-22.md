Baidu ($BIDU) said before Monday's open that third-quarter revenue **slipped 1%** from a year earlier to 30.8 billion yuan, about $4.6 billion, in line with estimates, Reuters reported. Adjusted earnings came to 6.78 yuan per American depositary share, short of the 7.67 yuan consensus, Investing.com reported, and Dow Jones also reported a miss, though Seeking Alpha's snapshot, using a different consensus, had it as a beat.

The quarter is a race between two businesses. Revenue from AI cloud infrastructure **rose 94%** to 8.2 billion yuan, but online marketing, still Baidu's search-ad engine, fell 22%, according to the release, Startup Fortune and MLQ. Investing.com put the profit shortfall down to the ad slump outweighing the cloud gains. Alibaba ($BABA) reports on Thursday with the same question about its own cloud ([our Qwen4-Max brief](/news/2026-11-16/alibaba-baba-launches-qwen4-max-saying-it-cuts-ai-task-costs-by-more-than-60/)).

The US shares were down 0.4% at $77.81 before the open, in our data, after a 40.2% fall this year that leaves them 51.9% below their January high.

Anthropic got the debut it wanted on Thursday and the market got nothing from it. The AI lab's shares opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, still 17% above the $83 offer price, but the S&P 500 fell 2.0% for a sixth straight loss, its longest losing streak since 2024, AP and Bloomberg reported ([our wrap](/news/2026-11-19/stock-market-today-anthropic-jumps-17-in-its-debut-but-the-dow-drops-1101-points/)). Of the 580 stocks we track, **531 fell**.

Overnight, the selling moved to the newest name. Anthropic was down about 6% before the open, CNBC and Reuters reported, and Nasdaq-100 futures fell 1.6%, led by the chipmakers that were supposed to be the IPO's biggest winners. Treasuries rallied as investors looked for cover; gold, hit by talk of Fed hikes, did not.

The shares touched $114.99 before sliding through the afternoon, and it still ranks as the largest stock-market debut on record, Benzinga said. CNBC put the pre-market fall at 6.4% at 5 a.m. ET and Reuters at about 6.8%. A week ago Wall Street was [betting the IPO could rescue the AI trade](/news/2026-11-15/wall-street-is-betting-anthropics-ipo-can-rescue-the-ai-trade-3-numbers-will-decide-it/); two sessions in, it is trading with the rest of it.

By our count, 531 stocks fell and 48 rose, a ratio of 11 to 1. AP put the S&P 500's share of decliners above nine in ten. That undercuts the rotation story [we tested on Thursday morning](/news/2026-11-19/the-average-stock-is-up-while-the-s-p-500-is-down-wall-street-is-betting-techs-lead-is-over/), in which money leaving AI would hold up everything else.

Alphabet was up 0.4% at 6:15 a.m. ET on Friday; Amazon was down another 1.6%.

## What's next

All times ET.

- **Friday, 20 November:** Anthropic's second day of trading. The University of Michigan's final November consumer sentiment reading at 10 a.m., forecast at about 51 to 52 after 51.6 in the preliminary reading, the XTB and Helious calendars show. No big US company reports today.
- **Next week:** a short week, with US markets shut on Thursday for Thanksgiving, and Black Friday's sales the next test of the consumer.

We think the test today is narrow: if Nvidia holds above Thursday's $145.98 close, the sell-off is still about the newest money leaving the oldest AI names; if it breaks lower with Anthropic, the IPO did not bring buyers back, it gave sellers one more stock to sell.

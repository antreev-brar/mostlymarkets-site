Textron ($TXT) said on Wednesday it will sell its Industrial segment, which includes fuel-tank maker Kautex, E-Z-GO golf carts, Jacobsen mowers and airport ground equipment, to an affiliate of Kingswood Capital Management for **$1.2 billion**, the company's release and Seeking Alpha reported. It expects about $1.1 billion in cash after taxes and costs, to fund extra buybacks in 2027, and its board approved repurchases of up to 35 million shares. The stock was up 2.7% at $83.87 as of 12:29 p.m. ET.

The sale finishes a plan Textron set out on 30 April to separate the segment, leaving a company built on Textron Aviation, Bell helicopters and Textron Systems. The price is modest: the segment had $3.2 billion of revenue in 2025, down nearly 9%, Manufacturing Dive reported, so Kingswood is paying under 0.4 times sales. The buyback authorization covers about a fifth of Textron's 169 million shares, worth about $2.9 billion at today's price.

The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027, and Textron will report the segment as discontinued operations from the fourth quarter.

## What happened
Shares of custom AI chip designer Broadcom ($AVGO) **sank 12.2%** on Friday to close at $304.41, after Ming-Chi Kuo, an analyst at TF International Securities, said Google had cut its planned 2027 orders for Broadcom-designed TPU chips, the AI processors Google uses in place of Nvidia's.

Citing his industry survey, Kuo said Google trimmed planned 2027 shipments of the TPU 8i by **about 1 million units, roughly 20%**, mostly in the pricier 8i Max version, and plans to ship more of the MediaTek-designed TPU 8t instead, Seeking Alpha, Yahoo Finance and StockAnalysis reported. Google has announced no change, and neither company commented, the reports said.

## Why it matters
Google's TPUs are the best-known product of Broadcom's custom-chip business, and the bull case [we tested last week](/news/2026-11-13/broadcom-avgo-sells-86-more-but-keeps-less-of-each-dollar-is-the-business-still-strong/) rests on those chips taking share from Nvidia. A survey that says the largest buyer is spreading orders to a cheaper designer hits both the volume and the pricing of that story, a week after Wall Street was [betting Nvidia's troubles would be Broadcom's gain](/news/2026-11-13/wall-street-is-betting-nvidias-loss-is-amd-and-broadcoms-gain-tuesday-will-test-it/).

The report landed on [a day the whole chip group sold off](/news/2026-11-20/stock-market-today-the-s-p-500-enters-a-correction-as-nvidia-sinks-14/), so not all of the drop is Kuo's: Marvell Technology ($MRVL), another custom-chip designer, fell 11.7%, by our data. Broadcom was the second-biggest drag on the S&P 500 after Nvidia, by our data.

## What the market is telling us
Broadcom's shares moved 5% or more on 20 of the last 252 sessions. Its last fall this big was on 4 June 2026, when it dropped 12.6%. The stock is down 12.1% this year and sits 36.8% below its 2 June closing high of $481.57. We'd treat the cut as a survey estimate until Broadcom's next results, where its AI revenue outlook for 2027 is the number that would confirm or rule it out.

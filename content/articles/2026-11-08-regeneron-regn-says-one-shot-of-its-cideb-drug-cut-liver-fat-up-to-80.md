On Sunday, Regeneron ($REGN) said a single injection of its experimental gene-silencing drug for fatty liver disease **cut liver fat by 58% to 80%** at 36 weeks, depending on dose, against a 2.9% fall on placebo. The early-stage trial treated 30 adults with MASLD, the most common form of fatty liver disease, and gave 10 a placebo, the company said in a release; the data were presented at the Liver Meeting of the American Association for the Study of Liver Diseases and published in the New England Journal of Medicine.

The drug silences CIDEB, a gene Regeneron's own genetics center linked to protection from liver disease, and was developed with Alnylam, which earns milestones and royalties. Liver fat fell to normal levels, under 5%, in 62% of treated patients and in none on placebo, according to the release and HCPLive.

One dose held its effect for 36 weeks, but the trial was small and reported no liver biopsies yet. Regeneron is testing longer-term safety and tissue effects in a phase 2b trial in MASH, the scarring form of the disease, and says a phase 3 trial is recruiting. The stock closed Friday at $904.35, 7.1% below its 3 November high and up 17.2% this year, a run helped by [its third-quarter beat](/news/2026-11-01/regeneron-regn-beats-q3-estimates-and-has-its-best-day-since-2022/); Monday's open is the first read on the data.
